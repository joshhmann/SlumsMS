Population: who is online

director tick ≥ 5 s8 logins at a time~3,000 peak on TEST

Bots are real character rows with a persona and a play window. The director compares the online count with a daily curve and logs bots in or out, preferring bots whose play window matches the hour. It never logs out a bot that has a commitment (a party, a promise).

Key parts

  • BotRoster: identity, ACTIVE / DORMANT, persona, home. New, quitting and returning bots each day (BotRosterLifecycle).
  • BotPopulationDirector: target from BotPopulationCurve, 20 min relog cooldown.
  • BotStore: character + holdings; id = BOT_ID_BASE + botId.
  • BotClient: a client with no socket; packets go nowhere.

Guard rails

  • BotDbGate: bots get a capped share of the DB pool, players keep a reserve. Refusals are logged (TEST sees ~100-160 an hour).
  • Boot waves seed the world, then the director takes over (EnvironmentManager).

Ticks: what runs and how often

macro tick 2-3 scombat 250 mslife decision every 30 salts 200 ms

One driver task hands due bots to virtual threads, so a slow bot only blocks itself. A bot's next tick is timed from when its last one finished; deep grinders off-screen slow to 60-120 s.

A TrainingBot macro tick, in order

  1. Death handling, restock and event calls.
  2. Pause if it's in a chat.
  3. Daily life: LifeRuntime.tick (the decision core below), then run any life activity it queued.
  4. Phase switch: INIT, IN_TOWN, SHOP, DECIDE, GO_TRAIN, LIFE_*, GRIND, GO_TOWN, BREAK.
  5. Party invites and menus, social intents (promises), mode switch.

Combat is not in the macro tick: a shared 250 ms ticker swings only grinding bots on maps a player can see.

Decision core: weighted GOAP + utility, no LLM

LifeRuntimeLifePolicyLifePlannerLifeLearning

This is the part you called the core. It runs every 30 seconds (sooner when something changes) while the bot is grinding, in town or dwelling.

  1. Facts (LifeGoals): level, job, gear, friends nearby, supplies. Some facts the game can't see are "soft" and marked done when the bot finishes the matching activity.
  2. Plan (LifePlanner, GOAP): cheapest step sequence toward the current goal (gear up, job advance, a prequest). The next step only gets a bonus; it doesn't force the choice.
  3. Needs: supplies, social, progress, boredom.
  4. Score options (LifePolicy): each activity scored from needs, mood, player type (Bartle: killer, socializer, explorer, achiever), people nearby, the plan bonus and learned likes.
  5. Pick (LifeDecision): the best, switching only if it beats the current one by a margin.
  6. Run (LifeExecutor): hands the activity to its owner (grind, town, PQ...). It grants nothing itself.
  7. Outcome (LifeOutcomes): done or failed, once per activity.
  8. Learn (LifeLearning): small, capped drift in what this bot likes, swept every 5 minutes.

Activities it chooses between: GRIND, HANG_OUT, PARTY_QUEST, RESTOCK, SHOW_OFF (plus channel moves, helping a friend).

Activities: what bots actually do

Grinding

  • TrainingMapChooser, LevelingPath, spot finding, looting (LootPicker).
  • Attacks through BotAttackDriver; buffs via BotBuffDriver.
  • Movement: GCMovement; travel: BotTransit.

PQs and bosses

  • mechanics/: one YAML row per PQ or boss room (stages, arms, reactors, timers, tiers).
  • Bots join a player's expedition (ExpeditionBots); GMs stage runs with !mechanics / !qa.
  • Chaos Zakum: bots share 25 runs a day.

Town and supply

  • Restock, shopping, potions (BotShopping, BotVitals).
  • Town life for town and social bots (LifeTownLife, same brain).

Gear and economy

  • Gear planner and optimizer, scrolling.
  • Merchants, the exchange traders (MarketTraders) and the NX desk.

Social: who they like and what they do together

A separate, cheaper loop (inside BotTalk.tick, no LLM, no DB reads): each bot due by its own pace picks the best social act and target.

Deciding

  • SocialMind: 12 traits, mood, emotions, needs.
  • SocialDecider + SocialActions: invite, share news, spread a rumour, help, ask...
  • SocialBudget: per act, per pair and per minute limits so bots don't loop.
  • IntentBook: promises they mean to keep.

Remembering

  • BotAffinity: ties to real players (−100..100, decays).
  • BondStore / BotBonds: bot-to-bot bonds, crews.
  • BotGossip, BotWorldGossip, BotMemory, Reputation.
  • Drama: rivalry, romance, guild wars, beef.

Chat: deciding first, wording second

  1. Read the line: ChatTriage (kind, intent; rules first, a model second), request rules in BotChatActions.
  2. Decide the move: ReplyPlanner picks yes/no/what to say from game state (on for live in 1.6.4).
  3. Word it: ChatRouter sends to the local model first, the cloud model for overflow, within a daily budget; scripted lines if neither answers.
  4. Check it: SayDo records every claim and whether the bot's state backs it.

Bot-to-bot talk uses TalkPlanner/BotTalk; ambient town chatter comes from canned packs (BotDialoguePack/*.yaml), which don't check state yet (b317).

Owned alts: your characters as companions

@botme200 ms companion tickup to 5 companions

An alt is your real character, reserved while it's out (OwnedAltService). It doesn't run the decision core: one shared 200 ms tick drives every alt, in this order.

  1. Death: a lethal hit really kills it; it gets up in town and goes home.
  2. Recovery: HP/MP like a player; drinks its own potions (same BotVitals as bots).
  3. Catch-up: stuck on another map too long, it's put beside you (never into or out of an instance).
  4. Gate (OwnedAltGate): closed, it only follows.
  5. Fight (OwnedAltCombat): nearest mob you're engaged with, its own skills, MP and ammo through the bots' BotAttackDriver.
  6. Loot into its own bag; buffs via BotBuffDriver; otherwise follow you.

Also: bonds (@bonds), weddings to your own alt, marriage EXP bonus, expeditions (an alt spends a real party slot).

Weak spots we know about

core first (1.6.5)
  • Options are scored before anyone checks they can run. LifePolicy scores whether an option fits the moment, not whether its executor can carry it out. Result: picks that fail. Invites failed 66% and news-sharing 94% before tonight's fixes; hang-out runs 172 of 2,734 picks (b318).
  • Failures teach the wrong lesson. A failure the bot couldn't help (no partner, no room) still lowers its liking for that activity.
  • Canned chatter isn't grounded. Town chatter can claim a guild or a job advance the bot doesn't have (b317).
  • Reasons aren't kept. Only counters survive; the decision trace, flagged queue and your Good/Bad review in the admin dashboard are card e18.
