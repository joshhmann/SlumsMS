Draft: server checks completed; real-client checks pending. Not released or published.

## Towns

- [Fix] Town bots now account for NPCs when choosing a place to stand, and step away when they come to rest directly in front of one.
- [Fix] Bots called into a fight now walk to the fight instead of appearing out of thin air.

## Chat

- [Fix] A delayed bot reply stays in its original channel when you switch between whisper, guild or buddy chat while waiting for it.
- [Fix] A bot you are talking to no longer wanders off mid-conversation; it keeps station while the conversation lasts.
- [Fix] Bots can hold several conversations at once on the same map, and a reply now joins the conversation it is about instead of the one nearest to it.
- [Fix] A bot only answers with something it can back up: small talk no longer gets a random claim about a topic, and a scripted shop ad only plays when the bot actually has the item.
- [Fix] An invitation to grind or hunt together is treated as an invitation, not as idle chatter.

## Quests

- [Fix] The mount quest's last step now really costs 20 million mesos: Chief Tatamo refuses you if you cannot pay instead of taking whatever you happen to carry.
