The fix-up after 1.5.0. A lot of things from the original server files were quietly broken: salons, party quests, the mount quest, some skills and NPCs. They work now, and bots behave better in town.

> **To update:** nothing to download. Just log in. You'll be asked to set a **PIC** (a short code you pick) once; you need it to delete a character.

## At a glance {#glance .cards}
- **💇 Salons work.** Hair, face, eye and lens coupons show their styles again.
- **🎉 Party quests finish.** Clears no longer crash, and rewards give the right amounts.
- **🐗 Ride a Hog.** The mount quest works from Kenta's breeding room to catching your Hog.
- **🧹 Delete characters.** The PIC is back, so the delete button works.
- **💬 Bots stay put.** A bot you're talking to stays with you instead of walking off.
- **✨ EXP cards.** 2x EXP for 7 days or 3x EXP for 1 day, and dying no longer cancels them.

## Fixed: things you couldn't do {#fixed}
- [Fix] **Hair, face, eye and lens coupons work again.** Salons and plastic surgery said "no options available" for every face and for the newer hairstyles.
- [Fix] **Party quests and events finish.** Clearing a party quest, the mount quest's breeding room or a wedding crashed at the end and left you inside with no reward.
- [Fix] **Party quest rewards give the right amounts** in Kerning, Ludibrium, Orbis, Boss Rush, the Ludibrium Maze and Magatia (Kerning potions gave 1 instead of 80).
- [Fix] **179 maps load again,** including Amoria PQ, Ellin PQ and the ghost maps.
- [Fix] **The mount quest works end to end.** Kenta's breeding room now lasts 3 minutes and sends you out with your Pheromones and Reports, Chief Tatamo's Dragon's Perfume Bottle really costs 20,000,000 mesos, and other players can see you riding.
- [Fix] **Quests charge the price they list.** Quests that cost mesos to finish used to take whatever you carried and still give the reward.
- [Fix] **You can delete characters again** (the PIC is back on).
- [Fix] **Venom** poisons again, **Silver Hawk** shows up, and **1st-job Bowmen, Thieves and Pirates** gain the right HP when they level up.
- [Change] **Summons last like the original game:** Silver Hawk, Golden Eagle, Puppet, Phoenix, Frostprey, Gaviota, Bahamut, Summon Dragon, Ifrit, Elquines and the Cygnus spirits have their v83 duration and no cooldown again.
- [Fix] **Spiegelmann** gives the item you paid Maple Coins for, **Mr. Do** in Mu Lung makes the scroll you pick, and the Maple Administrator on Maple Island answers.
- [Fix] **Quick chat isn't swallowed.** A second line typed right after the first was sometimes dropped.

## Bots {#bots}
- [Fix] **A bot you're talking to stays with you** for a while after each exchange, whatever it was doing: grinders back from shopping stay in town, Henesys regulars don't hop maps, and nobody logs off mid-chat.
- [Fix] **Bots called into a KS war come in like players,** through a portal on your map, and walk over.
- [Fix] Town bots keep clear of NPCs and stand on the real ground.
- [Fix] A bot's late reply lands in the chat you asked in, conversations follow the topic they're about, and bots only claim what they can back up.
- [Fix] The "Get off my lawn!!" tiger banner and bots' avatar megaphones leave the screen after 10 seconds.

## EXP cards {#cards}
- [New] **Two EXP cards in the Cash Shop:** 2x EXP for 7 days (6,000 NX) and 3x EXP for 1 day (3,000 NX). Both work all day, every day. If you hold both, the stronger one works.
- [New] **`@coupons`** shows your cards: when they work (server and Pacific time), whether they're on right now, and when they expire.
- [Change] **Dying no longer cancels your EXP card.** Other buffs still end when you die.
- Drop cards and the old timed cards aren't sold anymore; cards you already have keep working. The card's hover text still says "4 hour" or "2 hour"; the expiry date shown is the real one.

## Also {#also}
- [Fix] **Security:** a login shortcut from the original server files could let someone into another account. It's gone, along with `@autologin`.
- [Fix] The `@guide` pages fit the window.
- The bot roster finished its first-week growth (about 9,000 characters).

Thanks for the bug reports, keep them coming. See you in Bera~ 🍁
