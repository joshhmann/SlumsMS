## Changes {#changes}
- [Fix] **Bot attacks no longer close your game.** A few bot attacks, like a Dawn Warrior's Soul Blade, an Aran swinging a pole arm, and a gun bot's idle swing, used a move your game can't draw. If you were nearby, your game closed. Bots now only use attacks your game can show, and they still fight just as hard.
