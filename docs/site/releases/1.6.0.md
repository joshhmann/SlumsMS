The regulars have a life now. They pick their own day, keep their word, remember what you did together and start conversations of their own. Henesys throws a party (or gets invaded) every evening, and the website opens tonight too.

> **To update:** press **Play** in the launcher. It downloads the new 1.6.0 game client for you. Older clients are told to update and can't log in. New accounts are invite-only now: press **How to join** in the launcher, or sign up at **theslumsms.asslorde.com** with an invite code from a GM.

## Changes {#changes}

### Regulars with a life {#life}
- [New] **Regulars choose their own day.** Each one weighs what it needs (pots, a better spot, friends, a rest) and its own personality, then grinds, moves on, restocks, hangs out, joins a party quest or logs off. Personalities drift slowly with what happens to them.
- [New] **They do what they say.** A regular that says "brb pots" walks to town, restocks and comes back. One that says it's selling junk actually sells it.
- [New] **Towns come and go.** Instead of the same faces standing in place, regulars drift into town to hang out near where they hunt, at home or in Henesys, then leave again. Channel 1 is the busiest, and towns fill up a bit more when real players are around.
- [Fix] **The Free Market entrance is crowded again.** Its merchants and shoppers all show up again, about twice as many on channel 1 as before.
- [New] **Regulars feel more like players.** Some go AFK in town on a chair for a while and come back, they stroll and emote when you're nearby instead of standing frozen, they keep a little personal space, and they type like it's 2009.
- [Change] **The regulars you meet match the area.** Victoria's dungeons, Ludibrium and El Nath get regulars of the level their monsters suit.
- [Fix] Pirate regulars carry pirate weapons, and a regular with a full bag sells its junk in town instead of carrying on.

### They remember you {#social}
- [New] **Regulars remember more about you:** what you did together (party quests, bosses, trades, parties), favours and grudges, and what their friends say about you. A scam makes them wary, and your reputation travels through friend groups.
- [New] **They start things.** Friends may say hi when you log in, invite you to a party quest that fits your level, send a buddy request after you've played a lot together, or ask a day later how something went ("did u ever get that zhelm?").
- [New] **Regulars have their own drama.** They tease each other, pick rivals, rage-quit after a boomed scroll (and come back), get crushes, couple up and break up, and their guilds feud. Now and then a nice one turns villain, or a villain turns nice.
- [Change] **Beef only if you start it.** A regular only gets rival-level mean with you (trash talk, KS sprees, call-outs) after you started it with that same regular: KS'd it, took its drop, scammed it or insulted it. Its friends may side-eye and gossip, but they don't come after you. Friendly teasing is for everyone.
- [Change] **Romance:** `@romance on` still opens flirting. Now, if you flirt with a regular first, that one regular may flirt back without `@romance on`. `@romance off` shuts it all.
- [Change] **`@quiet bots` covers everything unasked** aimed at you: whispers, party and guild invites, buddy requests, greetings and taunts. Regulars still answer when you talk to them, and party chat still shows. It now stays on after you log out.
- [Change] **Regulars leave you alone when you're busy:** during party quests, boss fights, trades, shops and NPC chats. One regular won't repeat the same line to you.
- [New] **Party quest groups that really go.** When a regular shouts "lf party" in town and another one fits, they make a party and head to the party quest together.
- [New] Regulars answer in party chat, say "gz" on hunting maps, gossip about real things that happened, and trade with each other.
- [Change] `@botdrama off` also makes regulars forget any scam they think you pulled.

### World events {#events}
- [New] **A nightly event in Henesys, around 8 PM Pacific, on channel 1.** A warning goes out 10 minutes and 1 minute before it starts. Two events take turns:
  - **Giant Cake:** everyone gathers in town to smash a giant birthday cake while cake monsters and candles spill out.
  - **Henesys Invasion:** six waves of Drakes, Wild Kargos, Tauromacis, Werewolves (led by a Zombie Mushmom), Taurospears and Lycanthropes, then a **Jr. Balrog**. Regulars level 1-40 rush in, the townsfolk fight or run, and plenty of them fall. Bring friends: it's built for a crowd.
- [New] **Smaller events** pop up now and then at busy times: a Mushroom Invasion in the Ant Tunnel, a Boar Stampede, a Field Boss Sighting, a Treasure Hunt chest and a Wandering Explorer with a small quest.
- [New] **Rewards are potions and cakes.** The real player who does the most damage to the boss or cake gets the main prize, and everyone who took part in a won fight gets something too. Event monsters give their normal EXP and drops. Regulars never take event rewards.
- [New] Regulars talk about the event afterwards.

### Your character {#character}
- [New] **HP and MP go up to 99,999** (they stopped at 30,000). This needs the new 1.6.0 client.
- [New] **Free Relaxer and Fishing Chair.** Every character gets both: new characters at creation, existing ones once at their next login. No room? Free two Set-up slots and log in again.
- [New] **Your alts can tag along.** `@botme` lists your other characters on this account and world; `@botme <name>` brings one along as a party companion that fights, follows and chats like a regular. Up to 5 companions at a time. `@dismiss <name>` or `@dismiss all` sends them home, and they keep what they earned.
- [New] **Fishing.** Sit on any chair (the free Fishing Chair works) at a fishing spot: On the Way to the Harbor near Lith Harbor, the Pier on the Beach in Herb Town, or the Peaceful Ship. Every minute and a half you reel something in: fish, a snail shell, now and then a potion. Some regulars fish too.
- [Change] **Rebirth uses up its key item.** The item the rebirth asks for is now taken when you rebirth (before, it was only checked).
- [Change] **Daily Check-In:** NX now comes on the weekly milestone days (1,000 NX on days 7, 14, 21 and 28); the items and slots are unchanged.

### Trading {#trading}
- [New] **Buy megaphones get answers.** Megaphone something like "B> red whip 5m", "buying clean claw" or "wtb 60% glove dex", and a regular who really has the item may whisper you an offer within a minute or two. Then trade and haggle as usual. Sell megaphones don't get offers.
- [Fix] **NX merchants always hand over your code.** When you buy a 10,000 NX code for 50m, the code is also saved to your notes, so a closed or missed messenger window can't lose it. Each code can be redeemed only once, and codes typed with dashes or spaces work.
- [New] **The Buy/Sell board** at the Free Market entrance (a merchant's stall) shows the website board's open posts. Regulars who own the item (or have the mesos) answer a post with an offer.

### The website {#site}
- [New] **The SlumsMS website opens** with 1.6.0 at **theslumsms.asslorde.com**. Sign in with your game username and password.
- [New] **Slums Street:** look up items, monsters and drops, with the places where our server differs from the original game marked.
- [New] **Slums Market:** what's for sale in the Free Market right now, recent sales, daily prices and the NX-to-meso rate.
- [New] **Watchlist and price alerts.** Watch up to 20 items and set up to 10 alerts. When an alert hits, you get a yellow message in game if you're on, or a note at your next login. An alert fires once; re-arm it on the website.
- [New] **Buy/Sell board.** Post up to 5 offers at a time as one of your characters. Posts last 3 days. Nothing on the website moves items or mesos: you still trade in game.
- [New] **Rankings** by level, fame, guilds and trade ratings (trades finished vs trades that fell through), with each character's look.
- [New] **Live world and news:** players online per channel, the busiest maps, tonight's event, and a feed of big moments.

### NPCs {#npcs}
- [New] **Custom NPCs are open:**
  - **Coco** makes Chaos Scroll 60%, at most 5 per talk.
  - **Agent E** crafts accessories. The random belt roll works and gives belts only. Stat rings cost 1-2m mesos plus their materials.
  - **Dalair** merges equipment from level 160 (110 for Cygnus Knights) with Maker level 3. An item can be merged at most 3 times, and Dalair shows what will be used up and asks first.
  - **The Maple TV scroll generator** gives up to 5 prizes per character per day.
  - **Spiegelmann** refines ore.
- [New] **Nimble** (forging and ore refining) now also stands at the Free Market entrance, where the old refiners send you.
- [Change] **Rooty and the Donor Shop are one shop.** Both menus open the same catalog, with each item listed once.
- [Change] **Monster Carnival is back to normal:** a party of 2 or more, levels 30-50 (51-70 for the second Carnival).
- [Fix] The Pink Bean exit NPC no longer breaks when the room is gone, and pays its extra prize once per player, only when you have room.

### Party quests and bosses {#pq}
- [New] **Party regulars join your boss expedition** when they fit its level.
- [Fix] Party regulars fight inside your party quest (never the friendly monsters), and no longer walk through closed stage gates.
- [Fix] Script errors in the Henesys, Orbis and Crimsonwood Keep party quests are fixed.
- [Fix] Regulars never attack the Mu Lung Dojo's clear marker.

### Accounts {#accounts}
- [Change] **Accounts are invite-only.** New players press **How to join** in the launcher (or go to the website) and sign up with an invite code from a GM. The login screen no longer makes new accounts.
- [Change] **Any number of accounts per household.**
- [New] **Forgot your password?** Ask for a reset on the website. A GM approves it, then you set a new one.

### Chat fixes {#chat}
- [Fix] Regulars no longer tell you to meet on a channel that doesn't exist.
- [Fix] Regulars understand you better: "don't buff me", "he said he'd buff me" and stories about what already happened no longer make them do things.
- [Fix] Regulars stop making up player names and stop tacking catchphrases onto the end of their lines.
- [Fix] Cygnus Knight and Aran regulars answer class requests like "buff pls".

## Still settling in {#settling}
- **Regulars' days, busy towns and events are new.** Expect tuning over the next weeks: how busy each town gets, how often regulars restock or log off, and how wild the invasion gets.
- **Fishing rewards are a first draft:** small fish, shells and the odd potion. Better catches may come later.
- **Companions are brand new.** If an alt gets stuck or acts strange, `@dismiss` it and call it again, and tell us with `@bug`.
- **The website's prices fill in as people trade.** Some items and the NX rate show "not enough trades" until there's enough history.
