1.6.6 is a fix release for the regulars. They clear the whole first part of Zakum's party quest, get around many maps better, and no longer stand frozen where you cannot see them. Throwing stars and bullets can be recharged again. It is small on purpose: the known problems are listed at the bottom.

> **To update:** press **Play** in the launcher. There is no new client in this release.

## At a glance {#glance .cards}
- **{icon:item:1002357|🔥} Zakum's party quest part one, start to finish.** Regulars in your party now get through stage 6 and the big chest at stage 33. They share the keys between them, so the one key you had to fetch yourself is no longer yours to fetch.
- **{icon:item:2070016|⭐} Every throwing star and bullet can be recharged.**
- **{icon:item:4031180|🧭} Better rope routes and no more frozen regulars.** First version: tried in trial runs before release.

## Changes {#changes}

### Regulars {#regulars}

- [Fix] Zakum's party quest part one: regulars in your party now clear the whole thing, including stage 6 and the big chest at stage 33. They pool the keys between them, so you no longer need to grab one yourself. In our full trial run they cleared every stage in about 21 minutes.
- [Change] Rope routes: regulars can now jump from one rope to the next on many maps, so they reach places they used to get stuck at. This is a first version. We measured the jump on a real client and kept a safety margin, but we have only watched it play out in one full run (Zakum's party quest).
- [Fix] Regulars no longer stand frozen in the air off-screen after being moved into a map, and you are no longer shown a character that has already left. First version: it passed its tests, and we are still counting how often it happens in trial runs.

### Shops and items {#shops}

- [Fix] Crystal Ilbi throwing stars, Balanced Fury and Bullets for Novice Pirates can be recharged at shops again, and so can every other throwing star and bullet. Checked in tests against all 26 of them; we have not yet recharged one in a game session.

## Known issues {#known}

- Regulars sometimes turn down your party invites, or hold back. We are working on it and it moves to the regulars upgrade after 1.7.0.
- Orbis party quest, stage 6: regulars cannot make the jump onto the ladder, so you do that stage yourselves.

## Good to know {#know}

- Regulars still finish only some party quests on their own. See [What's coming](../whats-coming/) for the plan.
- Guide: [Zakum and Horntail](../../guide/bosses).
