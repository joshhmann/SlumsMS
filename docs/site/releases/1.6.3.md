1.6.3 is a stability update. The world stays up with a full crowd of regulars, NX moves to the SlumsBank desk, and a handful of fixes land for things you told us about.

> **To update:** press **Play** in the launcher.

## At a glance {#glance .cards}
- **{icon:item:2030000|🕰️} A steadier world.** The October 31 freeze and the login lockout after 1.6.2 are fixed, and the regulars come back gently after a restart.
- **{icon:item:4001126|💱} The SlumsBank NX desk.** Buy and sell NX instantly at SlumsBank's price, in game or on the website's Slums Exchange page.
- **{icon:item:4031099|🚶} Alts spread out.** The `lanes` order gives each alt its own spot instead of stacking them on one.
- **{icon:item:2340000|📜} Maple TV scroll prizes always pay.** A roll no longer comes up empty.
- **{icon:item:4031180|📘} Guide: star force and Spell Traces.** Where Spell Traces come from, and where they don't.
- **{icon:item:9000000|💰} A calmer Slums Exchange page.** The explanations, fees and limits fold away behind a click.

## Changes {#changes}

### A steadier world {#stability}
- [Fix] **The freeze that stopped the world for about three hours on October 31 is fixed.** Regulars copying trends (chairs, spots, phrases) is back on.
- [Fix] **The login lockout right after the 1.6.2 restart is fixed.** The world is back to its full crowd of regulars.
- [Change] **A restart no longer floods the towns.** Regulars drift back in over about 25 minutes, the way players come back after maintenance, instead of all at once.

### The SlumsBank NX desk {#desk}
- [New] **Buy and sell NX instantly.** SlumsBank sets buy and sell prices around a rate that drifts slowly with what players and bots buy and sell (capped per day). Trade at that quote in game with `@nx`, or on the website's Slums Exchange page.
- [New] **The quote is about 2% either side of the going rate,** and there's no extra fee on top of the gap between the two prices.
- [Change] **The rate moves with what everyone buys and sells:** at most 0.5% an hour and 20% a day.
- [New] **Selling NX asks for your password first.** If the desk runs short of NX or mesos it says so; try again later.
- [Change] **The desk replaces the NX order book.** Open NX orders were cancelled and refunded when it switched over.
- [New] Some regulars trade NX at the desk too, within hourly limits.

### Your alts {#alts}
- [Change] **Alts given the "lanes" order spread out** to their own spots instead of stacking on one. They stay within a screen of you; when you have more than one alt in the party, the extras can range farther across the map.

### Fixes {#fixes}
- [Fix] **Maple TV scroll prizes:** a roll no longer comes up empty; it always pays a scroll from the same family.
- [Fix] **Moren's Maker test** now accepts an Intermediate Monster Crystal 1 made either from monster drops or by disassembling an item (after you start the test).
- [Fix] **Free Market regulars** no longer stall on items without a market price.

### Website {#website}
- [Change] **Slums Exchange:** the explanations, fees and limits now fold away behind a click; prices, the form and the chart stay in view.
- [New] **Guide: a new "Star force and Spell Traces" section.** Spell Traces come from boss rewards and the Maker's refund; monsters don't drop them.

## Known issues {#known}
- **Will's and Magnus's entry portals** may not respond in some newer areas. We're on it for the next update.
- **Pet auto-potion** on some newer maps is being investigated (thanks for the report).

Thanks for playing 🍁
