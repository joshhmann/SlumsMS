The regulars get a little sharper this time. NX stops being a board of orders and becomes a desk with one quote, your
companions fan out around you instead of standing on your tile, and a regular works out what it wants to say before it
opens its mouth.

> **To update:** nothing to download. Just log in.

## At a glance {#glance .cards}
- **{icon:item:9000000|💱} NX at one posted quote.** `@nx buy 1000` or `@nx sell 1000`, then `@nx confirm`. You get the quote you were shown, or a fresh one if the rate moved.
- **{icon:item:1142001|🧑‍🤝‍🧑} Your companions spread out.** Say `lanes` and each one takes its own spot around you.
- **{icon:item:4031099|💬} Regulars speak up.** One may start a chat with you on its own, about once an hour, and its first line is thought out.
- **{icon:item:4031156|👥} More regulars.** 3,000 at peak instead of 1,200, with a 3,500 ceiling for the whole world.
- **{icon:item:2040000|🛠️} A pile of fixes.** Party quest passes, Moren's Maker lesson, the Maple TV scrolls, and a freeze that could hit the whole server.

## Changes {#changes}

### NX {#nx}
- [Change] **NX has a desk instead of a board.** `@nx` shows the rate; `@nx buy 1000` or `@nx sell 1000` (no price) then `@nx confirm` fills at once, at the quote you were shown or a fresh one if it moved while you were reading. The desk holds a pile of NX and mesos, so there is always someone to trade with, and it moves the rate a step at a time instead of letting one trade jump it. Item trading on `@nx <item>` is unchanged.
- [Change] **Any order you still had resting on the old board was cancelled** and your NX handed back. If you were offline, it is waiting for you at your next login.
- [New] **The regulars trade at the desk too,** with an allowance each hour, so they add to the market without one of them moving the rate on its own.
- [Fix] **A regular that trades NX keeps the NX it already had.** The hourly sweep no longer empties a regular's own purse.

### Companions {#companions}
- [New] **Lanes.** Say `lanes` and your companions take one spot each around you instead of stacking on your tile, and one of them tells you where they went ("k, 2 lanes here, 1 across the map"). A party too big for one screen gets split, with the rest spread across the map. `lanes screen` and `lanes map` still pick a single spot for everyone, and `lanes` on a map with no room says so.

### Regulars and chat {#social}
- [New] **A regular can start a chat with you on its own.** It only does it when it is free (not mid-grind, not in a party quest), and no more than about one an hour will do it, no matter how many regulars are nearby.
- [New] **Regulars decide what to say before they say it.** An answer about its level names its real next goal, a party or party quest ask gets a straight answer instead of small talk, and a regular only offers you places it can actually walk to.
- [Fix] **A regular standing in town says what it is doing there** instead of claiming it is out training.
- [Fix] **Free Market shop regulars keep selling** when they pick up an item nobody can price.
- [Fix] **Job errands reach the Bowman and Magician schools.** Regulars on an errand now walk in the door instead of skipping the trip.
- [Fix] **A busy moment on the server no longer quietly loses things.** A buddy request, a romance choice or a social setting you already made is kept, and a regular logging out while the database is busy keeps what it earned.

### Party quests {#pq}
- [Fix] **Kerning party quest stage 5.** A pass that drops for a party member no longer leaves a regular party stuck: the leader picks it up like any other party loot.

### Quests and shops {#quests}
- [Fix] **Moren's second Maker lesson** now counts the Intermediate Monster Crystal 1 you get by taking an item apart after he sets the test, so he accepts it instead of sending you off to make one you are already holding. A crystal made before he asks still proves nothing, as before.
- [Fix] **The Maple TV scroll generator always pays out.** Every roll now hands over a scroll from the family you rolled instead of "there are no scrolls on store right now".

### The world {#world}
- [Change] **More regulars at peak:** 3,000 of them, up from 1,200, with a 3,500 ceiling for the world. The ceiling now really holds: it counts regulars on their way in, so a busy night can't sneak past it.
- [Change] **The world fills gently when the server comes up,** over about 25 minutes instead of all at once, so logging in during the fill stays quick.
- [Fix] **Fixed a freeze that could hit the whole server** after a while, when fad activity waited on a lock that a login was holding. You won't notice anything in game, and the fads regulars follow are back on.

## Still settling in {#settling}
- **The NX desk is brand new.** The spread and how far the rate may step in one go will be tuned once it has real trading behind it.
- **Lanes and regulars' own chats are new.** If a companion gets stuck in a bad spot, say `lanes` again or `@dismiss` and call it back, and tell us with `@bug`.
- **3,000 regulars is a big jump.** Watch for a busier login or a laggy town, and report it.

## Coming in 1.7.0 {#next}
- **Lion King's Castle,** with Von Leon, and Cygnus: new lands, monsters and gear, and regulars that raid the bosses.
- **Neo Tokyo,** an endgame area with its own crafting coins.
- **A new class,** with its own quests and regulars who play it.
- **Smaller quality-of-life fixes** to the game client, each checked on its own.
- **Further out:** an exchange that delivers your items by mail, hyper teleport rocks and damage skins.

Thanks for playing, and see you in the Free Market~ 🍁
