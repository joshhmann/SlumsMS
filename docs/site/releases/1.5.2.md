The last 1.5.x update. We went through jobs, quests, NPCs, shops, party quests and items, and fixed what the original server files left broken. Next stop: 1.6.0.

> **To update:** nothing to download. Just log in.

## At a glance {#glance .cards}
- **💾 Your progress saves.** Some job advances and skill NPCs could stop your character from saving. Fixed.
- **📜 Quests make sense.** Quests that couldn't be finished are hidden, and Zipangu, Romeo and Juliet and the Horntail egg work again.
- **🛒 Shops restocked.** 84 stock items are back in Rien, Thorr's shop and the Singapore pirate shops.
- **🎓 Job Master fees.** Skipping a job test now costs mesos; the instructors stay free.
- **🐉 Bosses solo.** Von Leon, Cygnus, Lotus, Magnus and the other big bosses can be entered alone or as a pair.
- **📦 Duey is open.** Send items and mesos to friends.

## Jobs and skills {#jobs}
- [Fix] **Advancing no longer breaks saving.** The Leafre 4th job for Fire/Poison, Ice/Lightning, Bishop and Buccaneer, the "teach me my class skills" option, the Aran 1st job quest and Kyrin's test tried to teach skills this server doesn't have, and the character stopped saving after that.
- [Change] **The Job Master charges for skipping the test:** 2nd job 800,000, 3rd job 15,000,000, 4th job 100,000,000 mesos. 1st job is free, and your class instructors and the Leafre tests are still free.
- [Fix] **A 4th job from the Job Master comes with its master levels**, like the Leafre instructors give.
- [Fix] Dark Tidings checks its cooldown before freezing the map, and Super Beginners get their full HP and MP when they advance.
- [Change] The Gold Compass NPC ("4th job skills") sells skills from your own job line and your earlier rebirth jobs only.
- [Change] Mastery books for skills this server doesn't have no longer drop or sell.

## Quests {#quests}
- [Fix] **The Zipangu quests at the Mushroom Shrine** can be started again.
- [Fix] **Romeo and Juliet** (Magatia PQ) can be started again, and **the Horntail egg quest** gives its Dragon Stone.
- [New] **Dalair** stands in Henesys (lower street, between Bruce and Roca), so medal quests can be handed in.
- [Change] **Quests that can't be finished here are hidden:** skill-book quests for removed skills and the unfinished Von Leon and Henesys Ruins chains. If you had one in progress, you can still finish or forfeit it.
- [Fix] NPCs stop offering skills that don't exist, and nine stock quests that ended without their reward pay out.

## NPCs, shops and items {#npcs}
- [Fix] **84 stock shop items are back:** Rien's weapon and potion shops, Thorr's shop and the Singapore pirate gear.
- [Change] **The Key Binder gives 100 SP once per rebirth** (it gave unlimited SP before).
- [Change] **Mu Lung Dojo needs level 25** again. **Jump Quest chests** pay their meso and EXP bonus once a day per chest.
- [Fix] Stylists only take real styles, quick sell (`@qs`) can no longer freeze the server, Vega's Spell and Hyper Teleport Rocks behave, and several shop and storage edge cases are fixed.
- [New] **Godly items:** a rare drop now and then rolls extra stats. Maker crystals can only improve an item.
- [Change] The Maple Leaf sells to NPC shops for 1 meso, like the original game.

## Combat and bosses {#combat}
- [Change] **Meso Guard costs mesos again**, like the original game.
- [Change] **The big bosses can be entered alone or in pairs:** Von Leon, Cygnus, Lotus, Magnus, Lucid, Will, Verus, Darknell, Krexel and Castellan.
- [Change] **Amoria PQ** runs with 2 players of any gender.

## World and bots {#world}
- [New] **Duey is open.** GMs can't trade, use storage, Duey or drop items.
- [Fix] Bot guilds always keep room for players who want to join.
- [Fix] Bots walk off ledges better and stack on the same spot less.
- [Fix] A lot of behind-the-scenes stability work: the server ran 4 hours under full bot load with no freezes.

Thanks for testing and reporting. See you in Bera~ 🍁
