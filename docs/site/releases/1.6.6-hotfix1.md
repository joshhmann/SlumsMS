A small fix for the second rebirth, plus the bounce pads back to full strength for every job.

> **To update:** press **Play** in the launcher. There is no new client in this release.

## Changes {#changes}

### Rebirth {#rebirth}

- [Fix] Plunk stands in Von Leon's Audience Room and in Cygnus's room again. Clear the fight, stay in the room and talk to him for your reward. Von Leon's Seal, which the second rebirth's stone needs, comes from him and not from a monster drop. Until now nobody could get a Seal.
- [Change] Want another go at Von Leon? Once you have started "Defeat the Lion King", Ifia on the Roof of the Fifth Tower can send you back to the Audience Room corridor: pick "Please send me to the Audience Room Corridor" in her list. This is new and a first version.
- [Change] A shortcut to Wyatt the Reborn: type @rebirth while you are in Base of Roots. You can still click him and pick the last option in his list.

### Maps {#maps}

- [Change] Bounce pads throw every job as far as they used to, so the pit at Lion King's Castle can be jumped again. The ropes there still work.
