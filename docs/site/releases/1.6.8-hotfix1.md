A small fix for 1.6.8: the rebirth EXP rates go back to what they were, and two quests and fights are repaired.

> **To update:** press **Play** in the launcher. There is no new client in this release.

## Changes {#changes}

### Rebirth {#rebirth}

- [Change] Rebirth EXP rates are back to 3x / 2x / 1x (after the first, second and third rebirth), as before 1.6.8. The faster rates listed in the 1.6.8 notes did not stay. The +5 Ability Points for each rebirth and the class swap at Wyatt stay.

### Quests and bosses {#fixes}

- [Fix] El Nath's Protecting Tylus quest works again: Tylus drops his Trust while you protect him, so Heroes, Paladins and Dark Knights can earn Power Stance.
- [Fix] If Von Leon throws you into the Aerial Prison during the fight, its portal now takes you back into the fight.

## Good to know {#know}

- Rebirth EXP rates: [Rates](../../guide/rates). What the 1.6.8 notes said about them is corrected on [that page](../1.6.8/).
