1.6.4 teaches the regulars to run party quests and boss expeditions as a team, opens the Slums Exchange to every scroll, use item and etc item, brings weddings back and fixes a long list of things you told us about.

> **To update:** press **Play** in the launcher.

## At a glance {#glance .cards}
- **{icon:item:2030000|⚔️} Regulars run party quests and bosses.** Bot parties clear Kerning City, Orbis, Ludibrium, Henesys and the rest on their own, and share expedition fights from Zakum to Pink Bean.
- **{icon:item:2049100|📜} Every scroll, use item and etc item trades on the Slums Exchange.** Browse by category on the website, buy, or sell from a character's bags.
- **{icon:item:1112001|💍} Weddings work again.** The chapel NPCs load, rings are made safely and a stuck marriage can be ended.
- **{icon:item:4031180|🤝} Bonds.** `@bonds` lists your friends, partner, guild, family and alts.
- **{icon:item:2000005|🛠️} Fixes.** Crack in Time and the Temple of Time portals, the Lion King's Castle questline, the Evan tutorial pictures and more.
- **{icon:item:4001126|🏛️} Slums Street.** The website's database now lives at `/street/`; old links still work.

## Changes {#changes}

### Party quests {#pq}
- [New] **Regulars run party quests themselves:** Kerning City, Orbis, Ludibrium, Henesys, the six Cafe areas, LudiMaze, Pirate, Treasure, Save Delli, Magatia and its Zenumist band, Ellin Forest, Crimsonwood Keep and the Sharenian guild quest. They answer the NPCs, collect the coupons and passes, hit the reactors, take the portals and clear the stages.
- [New] **A party quest can be started by the regulars from a lobby, or led by you** with regulars and your own alts following.

### Bosses {#bosses}
- [New] **Regulars share boss expedition fights** instead of standing outside: Zakum, Horntail, Chaos Zakum, the five Twin Dragon caves, Maha, Castellan, Papulatus, Krexel, Showa, Balrog, Scarga, Elemental Thanatos, Pink Bean, Captain Latanica, Magnus, the four Hard bosses (Will, Lucid, Verus Hilla, Darknell), the six GuardianNex caves, the eight BossRush waves and the Zakum-mines chest hunt.
- [New] **Chaos Zakum is playable at the same altar,** as its own expedition. Players have no daily limit on it; the regulars share one run a day.
- [Change] **Regulars can die in a boss fight now,** like any other member. A Bishop's Resurrection brings one back, and the run waits for them instead of ending.
- [New] **A friendly regular tells you how a run ended,** in one line.
- [New] **Your own alt follows you into an expedition** you are already in, and counts as one of the party.

### The Slums Exchange {#exchange}
- [New] **Every scroll, use item and etc item has a ticker.** On the website's Slums Exchange page, an Items panel lists them with search, categories and sorting, and a Buy and a Sell button on every row. Post a buy or a sell at your own price and the game matches them.
- [New] **Sell from a character's bags on the website.** The items come out of that character's bags: log out of the game first, or sell while you're playing that character.
- [Change] **Sales of items are capped at 150,000,000 mesos per account per day;** the cap resets at midnight UTC.
- [New] **Item pages in Slums Street say when an item trades** and link straight to Buy and Sell.
- [Change] **`@nx book` and `@nx tickers` now describe the NX desk's quote** instead of an empty order book.

### Love and weddings {#love}
- [Fix] **If your wedding booking expires** because the couple never showed up, one ticket is returned to whoever booked it (by Duey if they are offline).
- [Fix] **The wedding NPCs load again** in the Chapel and the Cathedral.
- [Fix] **Rings are checked before they are handed over,** the wedding ticket comes back if the couple never start the ceremony, and the vows NPC stops cleanly if the rings could not be made.
- [Fix] **`@romance divorce` can end a marriage** whose ring is gone.
- [New] **You can marry your own alt** at the Cathedral or the Chapel: bring it out, talk to the priest and pay one wedding ticket. You both get the medal and the rings.
- [New] **Married couples hunting on the same map earn bonus EXP:** +10% with a regular as your spouse, +5% with your own alt.
- [New] **`@bonds`** lists your friends, partner, guild, family and alts.

### Fixes {#fixes}
- [Fix] **Crack in Time and the Temple of Time portals** take you to the right maps.
- [Fix] **The Lion King's Castle questline is open:** quest-only drops, the Von Leon NPCs, the Rose Garden golem.
- [Fix] **The Evan tutorial shows its illustrations again.**
- [Fix] **Magatia PQ's third stage** asked you to break beakers in the room next door. It is the right room now.
- [Fix] **Items dropped on the ground by regulars** could stop a gate, chest or altar from reacting to the other items lying on it. They react again.
- [Fix] **A boss part that revives into a form the data doesn't have** no longer breaks the boss's death: the loot, the clear and the next form still happen.
- [Fix] **Maker NPCs** refuse a character below the recipe's level.
- [Fix] **Attacking without MP deals no damage,** and a party Heal you can't afford heals nobody.
- [Fix] **A shop charges for a stack of stars** the price it pays for it.
- [Fix] **Elnath PQ:** a 2nd-job warrior in a party still gets Tylus's 3rd-job dialogue.
- [Fix] **The Holiday PQ exit portal** exists again.
- [Fix] **Cash items that have no effect yet** are kept instead of being used up.

### Website {#website}
- [New] **Slums Street.** The database is called Slums Street and lives at `/street/`. Every old `/database/` link and bookmark takes you to the same page.
- [New] **The Items panel on the Slums Exchange** (above), with names instead of codes.
- [Change] **Money pages** show Slums-chan with her bag of mesos.

## Known issues {#known}
- **Will's and Magnus's entry portals** may not respond in some newer areas.
- **Some newer-area routes** (for example the Future Leafre route) can crash the client for some players. We're on it.

Thanks for playing 🍁
