1.6.4 is the first version of the regulars taking part in party quests and boss expeditions, opens the Slums Exchange to every scroll, use item and etc item, lets you marry your own alt, and fixes a long list of things you told us about. See the correction below: parts of the first version of these notes said more than we had checked.

> **Correction, November 8, 2026.** When we published these notes we said more than we had checked. This box says what we said, what works today and what is still being fixed. The wrong lines below are struck out and corrected where they stand.

- **We said:** regulars clear 15 party quests on their own. **Today:** regulars take part in some party quest stages and do not finish most quests alone. Zakum part one: they clear the first stages in our tests, but they give up at stage 6 and cannot reach one key in the mines, so you do those yourself. Orbis and Ludibrium have stages they cannot clear yet. **Next:** the Zakum fixes are in testing for 1.6.6; the rest are planned, and Magatia and Ellin come later.
- **We said:** regulars join 23 boss expeditions. **Today:** regulars and your alts have been watched in real runs only in Zakum and Horntail, and only in trial runs before release. Horntail needed two fixes after 1.6.4: your alts stood still in two head rooms, and Horntail could end while some parts were still alive. Both are fixed in the 1.6.5 update and its hotfix. The other bosses are first versions that were tested in code only.
- **We said:** regulars can die in a fight and a Bishop's Resurrection brings one back, Chaos Zakum is open at the altar, and a regular tells you how a run ended. **Today:** these are first versions that have not been tested in a real fight.
- **We said:** regulars remember each other, swap small gifts and make plans to meet, and pick what to say from what is going on around them. **Today:** they greet and chat, but gifts and plans happen rarely, and some of what they say does not match where they are or what they are doing. **Next:** work on this is planned for later updates.
- **We said:** forging is simpler and Nimble hands out the new gear sources. **Today:** the forging changes are in the game, but the whole path from a hard boss to the finished piece (including the Lucid stone) has not been run through in game yet.
- **What did hold up:** the Slums Exchange items and the daily cap, `@bonds`, and the fixes listed under Fixes, which each have a test. We have not watched most of those fixes happen in a real game session.
- **Known gaps, plainly:** the Zakum stage 6 and key gap above, Orbis and Ludibrium stage gaps, regulars going frozen in the air after being moved into a map (fix in testing for 1.6.6), and regulars that do not always follow through on what they say. See [What's coming](../whats-coming/).

> **To update:** press **Play** in the launcher. This release needs the latest client (build 10740 or later); older clients are told to update through the launcher.

### Gear and forging {#gear}

- [New] Normal gear drops from monsters all the way to level 200, and Nimble's guide lists clearer sources for levels 120 to 149. **Corrected:** added in the game data; not yet farmed in a real game session.
- [Change] Rebirth forging is simpler: fewer materials per step, the later stages use the tokens hard bosses already give, and Nimble swaps one class weapon core for another for free. **Corrected:** the Lucid stone path has not been run from the boss to the finished piece yet.
- [Change] Nimble checks your level, rebirth and the exact items you picked before taking anything, and forged gear keeps its HP and MP. **Corrected:** not yet checked in a real game session.
- [Change] When you craft a Star Forced item into new gear, its STR, DEX, INT and LUK now carry over with the same 20% bonus that weapon and magic attack already get.
- [Change] A failed Star Force upgrade now costs a star, and a superior item that breaks is destroyed and tells you so.

### New places and quests {#places}

- [New] The Lion King's Castle questline is open: its quest drops, Von Leon's NPCs and Murt.
- [New] The casino chip shop is back, and chips sell back at their face value.

### Exchange {#exchange}

- [New] The exchange trades items now: scrolls, potions and other use items, and etc items, each with its own market price next to the NX and meso markets. Find them under Items on the website's exchange page, with search, categories and sorting. Selling there takes the items out of that character's bags: log out of the game first, or sell while you're playing that character.
- [New] Item sales on the exchange are capped at 150,000,000 mesos per account per day, enough for a few Chaos Scrolls. The cap resets at midnight UTC.

### Marriage and alts {#love}

- [New] You can marry your own alt at the cathedral or chapel: bring it out, talk to the priest and pay one wedding ticket. You both get the medal and the rings.
- [New] Married couples hunting on the same map earn bonus EXP: +10% with a regular as your spouse, +5% with your own alt.
- [Fix] If your wedding booking expires because the couple never showed up, one ticket goes back to whoever booked it (by Duey if they're offline).

### Fixes {#fixes}

- [Fix] Crack in Time and the Temple of Time no longer trap you: the portals lead where they should.
- [Fix] Buying throwing stars or bullets costs the same as selling them back, so the buy-low, sell-high loop is closed.
- [Fix] Vega's Spell uses the scroll you feed it.
- [Change] Maker NPCs refuse characters below the recipe's level.
- [Fix] A failed storage save no longer wipes your storage.
- [Fix] Attacking with no MP does no damage, and a party Heal you can't afford heals nobody.
- [Fix] Three megaphone items are no longer used up without any effect, and they're off Rooty's shop.
- [Fix] Only a party or alliance leader can use leader actions.
- [Fix] Weddings: chapel booking works, the ticket is spent only once the booking and invitations succeed, the booking text never shows "null", the divorce fee is charged only after the marriage ends, and a missing ring no longer breaks a divorce or blocks a new proposal.
- [Fix] A failed save no longer locks your alt out until the next restart, and when an alt is held back it tells you why.
- [Change] An alt can't be deleted while it's married (divorce first), and an alt turned away at someone else's wedding is told why.
- [Fix] Family reputation pays out, and the family window's buttons work.
- [Fix] Monsters in some newer areas that had no drop list now drop the map's usual items again (NX cards, leaves, scrolls).
- [Fix] Alcaster's Crystal completes its quest anywhere on its map, and scripted item uses no longer leave you frozen.
- [Fix] Monster Book: choosing a cover no longer drops your login, the link medal goes to the character that earned it, and tiers survive a relog.
- [Fix] Omok and memory-game rooms: closed rooms leave the map, boards reset, and ties are handled.
- [Change] Party search remembers 10 re-entries and only matches players near you.
- [Fix] A note that fails to send tells you why.
- [Fix] Quests: nine medal quests that could never finish are hidden, wrong-job quests finish their text, and quests 2233 and 2234 finish properly.
- [Fix] Job advancement NPCs no longer offer jobs that don't exist here, and only rebirthed level-1 characters choose a path.
- [Fix] Cutscenes: the Cannoneer and subway openings, the Evan tutorial pictures and Mr. Lim's menu work again.
- [Fix] The teleport rock refuses destinations that aren't on your list. Reward-box gear lasts hours, not seconds, and the world message names the prize. The glove stimulator gives the glove you paid for.
- [Fix] A silent leader can no longer freeze a Carnival challenge.
- [Fix] Magatia PQ's third stage can be finished again: the beakers you need to break are in the right room now.
- [Fix] Gates, chests and altars react to items again, even when a regular dropped one of the items on them.
- [Fix] A boss whose next form is missing no longer stops everything else on the map from dying properly: the loot, the clear and the next form all happen.
