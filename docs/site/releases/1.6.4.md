1.6.4 teaches the regulars to run party quests and boss expeditions as a team, opens the Slums Exchange to every scroll, use item and etc item, lets you marry your own alt, and fixes a long list of things you told us about.

> **To update:** press **Play** in the launcher. This release needs the latest client (build 10740 or later); older clients are told to update through the launcher.

## At a glance {#glance .cards}
- **{icon:item:2030000|⚔️} Regulars run party quests and bosses.** Parties clear Kerning City, Orbis, Ludibrium, Henesys and the rest on their own, and share expedition fights from Zakum to Pink Bean.
- **{icon:item:2049100|📜} Every scroll, use item and etc item trades on the Slums Exchange.** Browse by category on the website, buy, or sell from a character's bags.
- **{icon:item:1112001|💍} Marry your own alt.** Couples hunting on the same map earn bonus EXP, and weddings are fixed up.
- **{icon:item:4031180|🤝} `@bonds`.** See how your friends, partner, guild, family and alts feel about you.
- **{icon:item:1302000|⭐} Star Force and gear.** Normal gear drops all the way to level 200, and Star Force gives a little more.
- **{icon:item:2000005|🛠️} A long list of fixes,** from Crack in Time to the storage save.

## Changes {#changes}

### Party quests {#pq}
- [New] **Regulars run party quests themselves:** Kerning City, Orbis, Ludibrium, Henesys, the six Cafe areas, LudiMaze, Pirate, Treasure, Save Delli, Magatia and its Zenumist band, Ellin Forest, Crimsonwood Keep and the Sharenian guild quest. They answer the NPCs, collect the coupons and passes, hit the reactors, take the portals and clear the stages on their own timers.
- [New] **Lead a party quest with regulars and your own alts following you.**

### Bosses {#bosses}
- [New] **Regulars share boss expedition fights** instead of standing outside: Zakum, Horntail, Chaos Zakum, the five Twin Dragon caves, Maha, Castellan, Papulatus, Krexel, Showa, Balrog, Scarga, Elemental Thanatos, Pink Bean, Captain Latanica, Magnus, the four Hard bosses (Will, Lucid, Verus Hilla, Darknell), the six GuardianNex caves, the eight BossRush waves and the Zakum-mines chest hunt.
- [New] **Chaos Zakum is playable at the same altar,** as its own expedition, with the arms and the bodies in the order the client expects. Players have no daily entry limit on it.
- [New] **Regulars can die in a boss fight now,** like any other member. A Bishop's Resurrection brings one back, and the run waits for them instead of ending.
- [New] **A friendly regular tells you how a run ended,** in one line.
- [Change] **Von Leon's castle:** only the Audience Room fight needs rebirth 1. The corridor, prisons and roofs are open to everyone.
- [New] **Your own alt follows you into an expedition** you are already in, and counts as one of the party.

### Friends, regulars and chat {#friends}
- [New] **Type `@bonds`** to see how your friends, partner, guild, family and alts feel about you, alts that are out included.
- [New] **Regulars have moods, friendships and memories of each other.** They greet, chat, trade small gifts, argue, avoid each other and make plans to meet.
- [Change] **Regulars pick what to say from what is actually going on around them,** so their answers fit where they are and what you asked.
- [Fix] **A regular no longer answers a busy player, answers in the wrong chat, or replies with several lines at once.** Whispers get whispered back.
- [Fix] **A chat line you send no longer disappears** when the server hits an error while delivering it.

### Gear and forging {#gear}
- [New] **Normal gear drops from monsters all the way to level 200,** and Nimble's guide lists clearer sources for levels 120 to 149.
- [Change] **Rebirth forging is simpler:** fewer materials per step, and Nimble swaps one class weapon core for another for free.
- [Change] **Nimble checks your level, rebirth and the exact items you picked** before taking anything, and forged gear keeps its HP and MP.
- [Change] **Star Force upgrades give a little more STR, DEX, INT and LUK** than before.
- [Change] **A failed Star Force upgrade now costs a star,** and a superior item that breaks is destroyed and tells you so.

### New places and quests {#places}
- [New] **The Lion King's Castle questline is open:** its quest drops, Von Leon's NPCs, a Rose Garden golem and Murt.
- [New] **The casino chip shop is back,** and chips sell back at their face value.

### The Slums Exchange {#exchange}
- [New] **The exchange trades items now:** scrolls, potions and other use items, and etc items, each with its own market price next to the NX and meso markets.
- [New] **On the website,** an Items panel lists them with search, categories and sorting, and a Buy and a Sell button on every row. Selling takes the items out of that character's bags: log out of the game first, or sell while you're playing that character.
- [Change] **Item sales on the exchange are capped at 150,000,000 mesos per account per day,** enough for a few Chaos Scrolls. The cap resets at midnight UTC.

### Marriage and alts {#love}
- [New] **You can marry your own alt** at the Cathedral or the Chapel: bring it out, talk to the priest and pay one wedding ticket. You both get the medal and the rings.
- [New] **Married couples hunting on the same map earn bonus EXP:** +10% with a regular as your spouse, +5% with your own alt.
- [Fix] **If your wedding booking expires** because the couple never showed up, one ticket is returned to whoever booked it (by Duey if they are offline).
- [Fix] **Weddings:** chapel booking works, the ticket is spent only once the booking and invitations succeed, the booking text never shows "null", the divorce fee is charged only after the marriage ends, and a missing ring no longer breaks a divorce or blocks a new proposal.
- [Change] **An alt can't be deleted while it's married** (divorce first), and an alt turned away at someone else's wedding is told why.
- [Fix] **A failed save no longer locks your alt out** until the next restart, and when an alt is held back it tells you why.

### Fixes {#fixes}
- [Fix] **Crack in Time and the Temple of Time no longer trap you:** the portals lead where they should.
- [Fix] **Buying throwing stars or bullets costs the same as selling them back,** so the buy-low, sell-high loop is closed.
- [Fix] **Vega's Spell uses the scroll you feed it.**
- [Change] **Maker NPCs refuse characters below the recipe's level.**
- [Fix] **A failed storage save no longer wipes your storage.**
- [Fix] **Attacking with no MP does no damage,** and a party Heal you can't afford heals nobody.
- [Fix] **Three megaphone items are no longer used up without any effect,** and they're off Rooty's shop.
- [Fix] **Only a party or alliance leader can use leader actions.**
- [Fix] **Family reputation pays out,** and the family window's buttons work.
- [Fix] **Monsters in some newer areas that had no drop list** now drop the map's usual items again (NX cards, leaves, scrolls).
- [Fix] **Alcaster's Crystal completes its quest anywhere on its map,** and scripted item uses no longer leave you frozen.
- [Fix] **Monster Book:** choosing a cover no longer drops your login, the link medal goes to the character that earned it, and tiers survive a relog.
- [Fix] **Omok and memory-game rooms:** closed rooms leave the map, boards reset, and ties are handled.
- [Change] **Party search remembers 10 re-entries** and only matches players near you.
- [Fix] **A note that fails to send tells you why.**
- [Fix] **Quests:** nine medal quests that could never finish are hidden, wrong-job quests finish their text, and quests 2233 and 2234 finish properly.
- [Fix] **Job advancement NPCs no longer offer jobs that don't exist here,** and only rebirthed level-1 characters choose a path.
- [Fix] **Cutscenes:** the Cannoneer and subway openings, the Evan tutorial pictures and Mr. Lim's menu work again.
- [Fix] **The teleport rock refuses destinations that aren't on your list.** Reward-box gear lasts hours, not seconds, and the world message names the prize. The glove stimulator gives the glove you paid for.
- [Fix] **A silent leader can no longer freeze a Carnival challenge.**
- [Fix] **Magatia PQ's third stage** asked you to break three beakers that stand in the room next door, so the stage could never finish. It is the right room now, and the route in takes you there.
- [Fix] **Items dropped on the ground by regulars** could stop a gate, chest or altar from reacting to the other items lying on it. They react again.
- [Fix] **A boss part that revives into a form the data doesn't have** no longer breaks the boss's death: the loot, the clear and the next form still happen.

### Website {#website}
- [New] **Slums Street.** The database is called Slums Street and lives at `/street/`. Every old `/database/` link and bookmark takes you to the same page.
- [New] **The Items panel on the Slums Exchange** (above), with names instead of codes, and item pages in Slums Street that say when an item trades there.
- [Change] **`@nx book` and `@nx tickers` describe the NX desk's quote** instead of an empty order book.
- [Change] **Money pages** show Slums-chan with her bag of mesos.

## Known issues {#known}
- **Will's and Magnus's entry portals** may not respond in some newer areas. We're on it for the next update.
- **The Rose Garden** (Lion King's Castle) stays closed for now: the route is guarded because it can crash the client.
- **Pet auto-potion** doesn't work with some pets. We're on it for the next update.

Thanks for playing 🍁
