1.6.7 opens the road to the third rebirth. You can enter the Cygnus fight and clear it, the Aerial Prison no longer leaves you with no way out, and Plunk, Adobis and Tana keep your reward when your bag is full. It also brings a round of smaller fixes.

> **To update:** press **Play** in the launcher. There is no new client in this release.

## Changes {#changes}

### Rebirth {#rebirth}

- [New] The Cygnus fight is open. Thunder in Knight District drops the Thunder Stone, which makes the Dream Key from 5 each of the Dawn, Blaze, Wind and Night stones. The recruiter waits in Cygnus Garden. It is the boss for the third rebirth.
- [Fix] The Aerial Prison no longer has a dead end.
- [Fix] Plunk, Adobis and Tana keep your boss rewards when your bag is full, and tell you what to free.
- All three rebirths can now be completed. How the road to the second one runs: [Rebirth 2: the path](../../guide/rebirth2).

### Fixes {#fixes}

- [Fix] Pets no longer show as dried up. Nothing was lost.
- [Fix] Leaving the Crack in Time now puts you at the Gate of Time again.
- [Fix] The Job Master now checks your bag before your job advancement, so nothing is lost when it is full.
- [Fix] Logging in now always asks for your PIC when you have one.
- [Fix] Pressing Enter on some NPC windows no longer closes the game.

### Regulars {#regulars}

- [Fix] Regulars running Horntail with you now wake Horntail in the last room instead of giving up.
- [Fix] Regulars fighting Zakum now walk up to his arms and hit them with melee attacks instead of standing by.

### Shops and items {#shops}

- [Fix] Shops no longer list stars and bullets they do not sell with a price.
- [Fix] Chaos scrolls can roll every value, including the full drop.
- [Fix] Cat's Eyes for the Fancy Sword quest drop from Meerkats, as the item says.
- [Fix] The Cash Shop's Surprise no longer gives a package item.

### Server {#server}

- [New] Before an update restarts the server, everyone in game gets a notice. First version: it starts working with the update after this one.
