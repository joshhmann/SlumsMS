A quick hotfix after 1.5.2. Dropping the Maple Leaf to 1 meso turned out to be the wrong call: leaves drop often here, and they're a big part of how people earn. They're back to 500,000, and the job fees were raised to match.

> **To update:** nothing to download. Just log in.

## At a glance {#glance .cards}
- **🍁 Maple Leaf back to 500k.** Sell one to any shop for 500,000 mesos, like before 1.5.2.
- **🎓 Job Master fees x5.** Skipping a job test costs 4m for 2nd job, 75m for 3rd and 500m for 4th.
- **🧭 Gold Compass 50m.** The 4th job skill NPC now costs 50,000,000 mesos.
- **↩️ Leaves given back.** Leaves sold for 1 meso during 1.5.2 were returned.

## Changes {#changes}
- [Fix] **The Maple Leaf sells for 500,000 mesos again.** In 1.5.2 the shop showed 500,000 but paid 1 meso.
- [Change] **The Job Master charges 5 times as much for skipping a test:** 2nd job 4,000,000, 3rd job 75,000,000, 4th job 500,000,000 mesos. 1st job, your class instructors and the Leafre tests are still free.
- [Change] **The Gold Compass costs 50,000,000 mesos**, also 5 times as much.
- [Fix] **Leaves sold for 1 meso were refunded** to the player who sold them.

Thanks for the quick reports. See you in Bera~ 🍁
