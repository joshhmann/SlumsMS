1.6.8 makes rebirths worth more with Ability Points and a class swap, puts the Cygnus Knight Emblem back in the game, and teaches your alts to fight boss runs by job. It also fixes a long list of smaller problems around bosses and quests.

> **To update:** press **Play** in the launcher. There is no new client in this release.

> **Correction, November 10, 2026.** We said rebirth EXP now gets faster each time. We have withdrawn that: rebirth EXP rates are back to 3x / 2x / 1x (after the first, second and third rebirth), as before 1.6.8. The struck-out line below is corrected where it stands. The rest of this page stands.

## Changes {#changes}

### Rebirth {#rebirth}

- [Change] ~~Rebirth EXP now gets faster each time: 3x after the first rebirth, 4x after the second, 5x after the third, up to level 255.~~ **Corrected:** rebirth EXP rates are back to 3x / 2x / 1x, as before 1.6.8.
- [New] Each rebirth now gives +5 Ability Points. Characters that already rebirthed get theirs once.
- [New] At Wyatt you can return to a class you have mastered, at your current level. Only your job changes, and you can swap again after about 2 hours. It is refused in a party or a boss run.
- [Change] Wyatt now refuses a rebirth to a job that is not on his list, and refuses a rebirth while you are inside a boss run or party quest.
- [Fix] The Cygnus Knight Emblem now drops from Official Knight A at the Stronghold Entrance. It opens the way into the Knight Stronghold and is needed for the stone of the third rebirth.
- [Fix] A Von Leon clear now always gives you the Seal, even if you log out or leave the room before talking to Plunk. It waits for you and is handed over when you log in or leave the room.
- [Fix] A reborn level-1 character no longer gets the "Basic Configuration" popup at every login.
- [Fix] After a rebirth, a weapon your new level-1 character cannot use goes to your Equip bag instead of staying equipped. If the bag is full Wyatt warns you first.

### Alts in boss runs {#alts}

- [New] A Bishop alt in your boss run now casts Resurrection on you when you fall, and the run keeps fighting. If nobody in the run can revive you, it waits for you as before.
- [Change] In boss runs your alts now stand by job. Ranged and magic alts take the head platforms and melee alts stay low, instead of all bunching in one corner. Regulars are unchanged.
- [New] Your alts attack Horntail's parts in a set order: legs and tail first, then wings and the right hand, then the right head, left head, middle head and the left hand last. Ranged alts go for the heads, melee alts for the legs and tail.
- [Fix] In Horntail's last room your alts no longer start on the wrong head. Their first pick is now the head they should be fighting.
- [New] A sealed or cursed alt now uses its own Holy Water or All Cure in a boss fight, within a couple of seconds.
- [Change] "Start the fight!" now names every alt going in short of potions, arrows or Holy Water.
- [Change] Alts stay out of Solomon's and Rex's attack zones on Pink Bean.
- [Fix] Dismissing an alt in the middle of a boss fight no longer makes the run wait for it. An alt whose owner is down now says "you are down" instead of calling you idle.
- [Fix] An alt that is registered in your expedition but not at the altar when you press Start is now brought into the run. First version: it passed its tests, and we have not yet seen it in a game window.
- [Change] An alt whose buffs a boss dispels now puts them back up at once. First version: it passed its tests, and no boss has dispelled an alt in our trial runs yet.
- [Fix] `@botme <alt>` on an alt that is already with you now says "<alt> is already with you." instead of nothing.
- [Fix] Your alts can now hand a gear upgrade to each other, not just to you.

### Bosses and quests {#bosses}

- [Fix] Kao, who starts the Krexel fight, now stands in a map you can walk to (the boss portal in the map before it, or the Henesys boss teleporter). After Krexel falls you have five minutes to loot before everyone is taken out.
- [Fix] After you leave a boss fight that your alts are still in, you can start that boss again right away instead of being told the expedition is still in progress.
- [Fix] Boss entry rules now match what the boss menus say: each boss shows its real level range (for example Papulatus 80+), and one player with their alts can start any boss.
- [Fix] If you log back in inside the Zakum prequest maze you are now sent out instead of getting a script error at the exit portal.
- [Fix] A member who joins a Pink Bean expedition late is now let in, instead of being told the fight already started. First version: it passed its tests, and we have not yet seen it in a game window.
- [Change] Daily boss entry limits are removed.
- [Fix] Explorer medal quests (such as Beginner Explorer) can be completed again after you have visited more maps than they ask for.
