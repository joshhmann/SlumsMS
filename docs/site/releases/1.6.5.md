1.6.5 is a safety release for the big fights and the shops: regulars help in Zakum's party quest mines, your alts and the regulars fight Horntail, shop buyback is all-or-nothing, buying from hired merchants can no longer lose or duplicate anything, and the Incubator hatches again. A hotfix followed within hours and is listed at the bottom.

> **To update:** press **Play** in the launcher. There is no new client in this release.

## At a glance {#glance .cards}
- **{icon:item:1002357|🔥} Zakum's party quest: regulars help in the mines.** Known: regulars can't reach one key in the mines yet; grab that one yourself. (Fixed in the next update.)
- **{icon:item:1122000|🐉} Horntail gets fought properly.** Your alts and the regulars fight him, melee classes included, and the hotfix covers the head rooms and the clear.
- **{icon:item:4170000|🥚} The Incubator hatches again** instead of eating your Pigmy Eggs.
- **{icon:item:2030000|🛒} Honest shops.** Buyback is all-or-nothing, and buying from hired merchants can't lose or duplicate items or mesos.
- **{icon:item:4031180|🔒} A ban reaches the website.** A banned account is signed out within a minute.

## Changes {#changes}

### Bosses and party quests {#bosses}

- [Fix] Zakum's party quest: regulars in your party now go into the mines and help. Known: regulars can't reach one key in the mines yet; grab that one yourself. (Fixed in the next update.)
- [Fix] Your alts and regulars now fight Horntail, melee classes included.
- [Change] Expeditions: a member who isn't at the altar when the fight starts stays behind, and the leader is told who. A dismissed alt leaves the member list. The fight still starts if that leaves the room a little short.

### Shops and items {#shops}

- [Fix] Shop buyback is all-or-nothing: if anything goes wrong you keep your mesos and can try again.
- [Fix] Buying from hired merchants can no longer lose or duplicate items or mesos.
- [Fix] The Incubator hatches Pigmy Eggs again instead of eating them.
- [Fix] A Duey package can be claimed on the very second it arrives.
- [Change] 16 leftover junk drops from the old forge recipes no longer drop; items you already have are unchanged.

### Regulars, parties and guilds {#people}

- [Fix] Regulars no longer forget parts of your conversations when the server is busy.
- [Fix] Passing party leadership works when the old leader is offline, and guild expel no longer fails.

### Accounts and website {#accounts}

- [Fix] A banned account is signed out of the website within a minute.
- [Fix] Account and login security fixes.

### Fixed in the hotfix {#hotfix}

The hotfix went live on November 7 at 11:21 PM PT (07:21 UTC on November 8).

- [Fix] Your alts now fight in every Horntail room, including the two head rooms where they used to stand still.
- [Fix] Horntail now only counts as cleared once every part is down.
