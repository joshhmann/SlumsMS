1.6.2 brings training past level 120, rebirths for everyone, the Maker skill, the Slums Exchange, your alts as companions, smarter regulars and a new game client. There is no 1.6.1: it became part of this patch, so everything that was meant for it lands here. Update the game through the launcher; older clients are asked to update. The launcher's own update gets its own post a day or two later.

> **To update:** press **Play** in the launcher. It downloads the new 1.6.2 game client. Older clients are told to update and can't log in.

## Changes {#changes}

### Training after 120 {#training}
- [New] **Training past 120.** The Future, Twilight Perion, Warrior Grounds and lower Grandis (Pantheon, Savage Terminal's streets, Sanctuary, the Caravan Refuge, Fox Ridge, Cheong-woon Valley, Ristonia, Toolen City) open at level 120, or after your 1st rebirth at any level. Get there by the Henesys teleporter, the Hyper Teleport Rock, or the Gate of Time, which also goes to Leafre of the Past (level 124 Corrupted monsters).
- [New] **Deeper Grandis** (Heliseum, the Fox Tree, Cernium, the rougher back streets) opens after your 1st rebirth, and Arcane River after your 2nd (it needed 3).
- [New] **Rebirths for everyone.** Wyatt the Reborn in the Base of Roots now makes the Essence Stone your next rebirth needs from its boss drops (Zakum Diamond + Nine Spirit's Egg for the 1st), and tells you what's missing. The Black Stone is made from a Cygnus Circlet while you hold a Cygnus Knight Emblem (the emblem is not used up). Magnus, Lucid, Will, Verus and Darknell still need 3 rebirths.
- [New] Monsters there have health that fits their level and pay about twice the Temple of Time, climbing higher up. They drop mesos, potions, ores, scrolls, 4th-job mastery books and gear; rarely Eclectic or Muspell armour, and once in a long while Pensalir armour or a Jaihin, Utgard or AbsoLab weapon.
- [Change] **Leafre and Minar Forest, the Temple of Time and Ulu City give 40% more EXP.**
- [New] Cygnus, her knights and Castellan now drop loot.
- [Change] **Lion King's Castle is reworked:** level-fair monsters, good EXP, real drops, and no rebirth needed. The Rose Garden's entrance, Gardener's Spot, is open from the Fifth Tower roof; the garden itself comes back in a later patch.
- [New] Regulars of level 120+ train there too.
- [Change] **Cygnus and Magnus are rebalanced** to fit their level: Cygnus 110M HP, Magnus 2.1B.

### New for everyone {#everyone}
- [New] **Achievements and medals:** 32 one-time goals, each a cosmetic medal; a yellow line tells you. `@achievements` lists them. Old job, level, guild, marriage and rebirths count at your next login.
- [New] **Family works:** every perk (Reunion and Summon on any channel, the 1.5x/2x rates, party 2x, Bonding), once a day each. Perks stack with coupons and with EXP and drop events, and survive a restart. Regulars can be your juniors, earn you rep while they train, and come when summoned.
- [New] **Slums Stamps:** one account balance, earned from `@daily` (48 over 28 days; level 10+, a gap over 48 h restarts the streak). Spend at Rooty: Donor NX cosmetics 6 each, the Rose Vac and the Hyper Teleport Rock 40 each. Billion Coins and vote points became 6 stamps each, wherever they sat.
- [New] **Slums-chan**, the new guide, welcomes you once and stands in the Base of Roots and Henesys.
- [New] **Start on Maple Island:** every level 1-9 starter character is asked once (new ones and existing ones that never were); Shanks sails you to Lith Harbor free at level 7.
- [New] **`@invite`:** level 30+ characters make up to 2 invite codes a week, in game or on the website.
- [New] **`@bug`** tells you your report number and attaches your versions, map and recent steps; reports from the game, launcher and website land in one place.
- [New] **Lazy Penguins** nap in 15 towns; click one.
- [New] **Monster Book:** Arcane River and Grandis monsters drop Monster Book cards, the book lists them, and their Dropping tabs show the cards.
- [New] **New commands:** `@stamps` shows your balance and next bonus day; `@points` now shows stamps; `@achievements all` lists every goal. `@daily` explains its rules and says why a claim is refused.

### Maker {#maker}
- [New] **The Maker skill is back.** Moren in Magatia teaches it through his quest chain (levels 45, 75 and 105). Make gear, gems and monster crystals in the Maker window; now and then a basic gem comes out a grade higher. Dalair's equipment merge now needs Maker level 3. The Maker charges the fee its window shows, and takes it before your materials, so a craft or disassembly you can't pay for never uses them up.

### Your alts (@botme) {#alts}
- [New] **Bring your other characters along.** Your alts take orders in party chat or with `@alts`: follow, stay, patrol, sentry, move here, and formations like spread or stack. Invite your offline character to your party to call it.
- [New] Ask them to "trade pots", "trade scrolls", "trade 100k" or "show me your hat"; they point out gear upgrades for you and share pots when you're low.
- [New] Your alts talk in party chat, so your whole party sees them.

### Slums Exchange {#exchange}
- [New] **Trade NX for mesos** with other players on the Slums Exchange, in game with `@nx` or on the website. Prices are per 1 NX (`@nx sell 1000 5000`, or "4.5k"); the smallest order is 100 NX.
- [New] The official rate is the average of the last 7 days' trades (at least 3 trades); until then it starts at 5,000 mesos per NX. NX trades always stay between 2,500 and 10,000 mesos per NX. Within that, NX can't be sold for less than half the rate or bought for more than double it (at the start: sell from 2,500, buy up to 10,000 per NX). Higher asks and lower bids are fine; they wait for a match.
- [New] Regulars trade on the Slums Exchange too: about a third of them buy and sell leaves, elixirs and the Chaos and White Scrolls they find while hunting. They don't trade NX.
- [New] Besides NX, the exchange trades Maple Leaves, Chaos Scrolls, White Scrolls and Elixirs for mesos the same way, with no price band on those.
- [Change] No daily NX limits; up to 20 exchange orders a day per account. For your safety, exchange actions on the website need a sign-in from the last 12 hours, and after a server restart the site asks you to sign in again before you trade. Selling on the website, or buying more than 10M mesos' worth in 12 hours, asks for your password for that order.

### Regulars {#regulars}
- [New] Regulars greet the regulars they know, chat, and ask each other to party, come along, help or trade; a yes is carried out, and a regular that can't says no. Friends follow each other to town.
- [New] Regulars read the last few lines of the chat before they answer, and remember your last few whispers to them, so a follow-up makes sense to them.
- [Fix] Regulars keep their story straight: they talk about the map they're really on and what's really there, only say "again" to players they've actually met, and answer your offer when you haggle instead of repeating their price.
- [New] They take real threats seriously (death wishes, slurs, real-life threats); friendly trash talk like "ur so trash" is fine.
- [New] One rude line gets a brush-off; keep it up with the same regular within 10 minutes and they get angry and like you less. Be rude to three or more regulars on a map and the whole map cools on you and may tease you for an hour (not with `@botdrama` off). Saying "sorry" to a regular resets their count, so your next rude line counts as a first one again. Death wishes, slurs and threats count from the first line.
- [New] Now and then a regular asks to be your junior; the offer stands for 30 minutes.
- [New] Regulars take and finish quests while they train. Ask one what quests it's doing.
- [New] Regulars walk to their job instructor to advance, and read the skill books their 4th job needs.
- [New] Regulars can run Kerning City's party quest with you, and sometimes form their own groups for it, and work through the Temple of Time's story quests.
- [New] Cleric regulars heal and buff their party.
- [New] Ask a regular who likes you for a pot or two; they'll share a little if they can.
- [New] Ask a friendly regular for fame and they may give you some.
- [New] When an event monster like the Giant Cake falls, the regulars around it stay to grab the loot, just like players do.
- [Change] Regulars dress smarter, and scrolled gear in their shops is priced from what scrolls really sell for: mid-scrolled items cost much less than before.
- [New] Regulars who reach 200 can be reborn: they earn the key items in real boss runs (and join yours), then re-gear at level 1.

### Game client (update through the launcher; older clients are asked to update) {#client}
- [New] **Make the screen yours.** The first time you log in, a card shows you **Edit UI** (Ctrl+F11): move and resize the bars, the status bar and the quick slots. "Show me" points at it. You see it once.
- [New] **New HUD modules** in the HUD window (Ctrl+F10), both off until you tick them:
  - **EXP rate:** your EXP per hour, how long until your next level, and what you've earned this session.
  - **DPS:** your damage per second and the fight's total. In a party, after a boss or a big monster dies, it also shows who did the most damage, with each player's share.
- [New] **Short damage numbers.** Tick "Short damage numbers" in the HUD window and big hits show as 12.3K or 1.23M instead of long rows of digits. Off by default.
- [New] **Hide other players' cash effects.** A Display switch in the HUD window for busy maps: the glows and sparkles from other players' cash items go away. Their gear still shows, and so do your own effects.
- [New] **Borderless window** and **sharp text on scaled screens**, both off: switch them in the launcher's Game options once the launcher update arrives, a day or two after this patch. They stay the right size when you change the game's resolution.
- [New] **Crash reports.** If the game crashes, it writes a plain-language note to `crash_reports\LATEST_CRASH.txt` in your game folder: what happened, the map you were on and what the game was loading. Your password, account and chat are never in it. Attach it to a `@bug` report.
- [Fix] Some custom images could make the game close on its own (for example when the first damage number showed).
- [Fix] Clicking a spot on the world map (to use a Hyper Teleport Rock) works without pixel-perfect aim, and the Temple of Time page links to Grandis.

### Website {#website}
- [New] **Most hunted:** the monsters hunted most in the last 7 days and 24 hours. Counts include off-screen kills, so they're marked estimated.
- [New] **My invites:** make, list and cancel your invite codes on the website (same 2 a week as `@invite`). The Join and FAQ pages explain how to get a code.
- [Change] The **bug form** attaches your versions and lets you pick your character.
- [New] **My stamps:** see how many Slums Stamps you have on the website, under Slums Market → My stuff.
- [Change] Shorter page addresses; old links still work.
- [New] **Buy/Sell board:** when you pick an item for a new post, the board shows what it sells for (the latest market price and that day's low to high) and what's already posted for it. One click puts the market price in your price box.

- [Fix] The Adventurer's Bag only takes what it can give back exactly (untradeable, karma'd, expiring, name-tagged and account-shared stacks stay out); looting with its box open no longer loses items.
- [Fix] Slums-chan, the Lazy Penguins and other custom NPCs no longer stand behind scenery.
- [Fix] Regulars' shops no longer sell boss-only drops (all three Zakum Helmets, the Horntail Necklace), Chaos and White Scrolls, or godly gear they didn't scroll themselves; those come from bosses, drops, quests and events. The website's sales counts show real trades.
- [Fix] Regulars' shops now stock 10% and 60% attack scrolls for every weapon type (spears, polearms, swords, axes, blunt weapons, bows, crossbows, wands, staves, knuckles and guns), not just a few.
- [Fix] No more repeated greetings from the same regular, and two regulars on a map don't say the identical line back to back.
- [Fix] Regular chat never shows a blank name placeholder like "{player}" any more.
- [Fix] With `@romance off`, regulars no longer bring up a past relationship with you.
- [Fix] Other players now see the green column when you cast Heal.
- [Change] Dark Knight regulars hit bosses much harder (Dark Impale).
- [Fix] Regulars stand next to monsters, not inside them.
- [Fix] Boss portals say "You need 3 rebirths" when that's what's missing.
- [Fix] Characters made after the last 1.6 update who never got their starter chair now have it.
- [Fix] A door or warp into a map that would crash the game or leave you stuck now takes you somewhere safe instead.
- [Fix] Kerning City's party quest: passes the regulars hand you now show up in your Etc tab.
- [Fix] Kerning City's party quest: the next group on a channel is no longer blocked by a group still finishing its bonus stage.
- [Fix] Opening the Dimensional Mirror could sometimes close the game.
- [Fix] Your alts' pets follow them when you call them with `@botme`.
- [Fix] Regulars in town no longer claim they're grinding.
- [Fix] Regulars arrive fully dressed; they no longer show up bald for a moment when they enter a map.
- [Fix] The Giant Cake lasts about 5 minutes, however many players and regulars join.

## Still settling in {#settling}
- **Achievements, Stamps and Family are new.** Old job, level, guild, marriage and rebirths count at your next login, so a goal you already reached shows up then rather than straight away.
- **The NX rate starts at 5,000 mesos per NX** and only follows real trades once there are 3 in the last 7 days. Until then every NX trade stays inside the 2,500 to 10,000 band.
- **Most hunted marks off-screen kills as estimated:** they're worked out from the regulars' usual pace, so they're close but not exact.
- **The new HUD modules start off.** If the HUD window (Ctrl+F10) looks the same, tick EXP rate and DPS there.
