A small fix for anyone working on their mount.

> **To update:** nothing to download. Just log in.

## At a glance {#glance .cards}
- **🐗 The hog can be hit.** Every swing at the Tamable Hog now counts, even when it shows MISS.

## Changes {#changes}
- [Fix] **The Tamable Hog in the mount quest takes damage.** It has huge avoid and defense, so almost every attack missed and only poison could wear it down. Now every swing takes about a tenth of its HP, about 7 swings to get it low enough for the perfume. It can't die from this, so it's still there to catch.

Happy riding. See you in Bera~ 🍁
