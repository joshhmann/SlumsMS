  • Friends only. 5 to 15 real players. Everyone else in town is a regular.

How to play

  1. Get the launcher. SlumsMS is friends-only: ask an admin for the launcher. It's one small program with everything built in, so there's nothing to set up. Please don't pass it on; ask an admin to invite your friends.
  2. Install. Press Install. The first download is about 23.6 GB and resumes if it gets interrupted. Updates only fetch what changed.
  3. Make an account. On the game's login screen, type a new username and a passphrase of at least 6 characters; the account is made for you.
  4. Say hi. A short tour opens at your first login. Type @guide to see it again.

More detail, including what to do if Windows or your antivirus warns you and the fix for the Parsec start-up error, is in Getting started.
