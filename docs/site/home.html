What it's like

  • Thousands of regulars. About 3,000 bots at the evening peak. Talk to them in plain words: "wanna party?", "come to henesys", "how much for the work gloves".
  • They remember you. Friends buff you and send party invites. Rivals roast your gear. @botdrama off keeps everyone friendly.
  • Guilds, wars and weddings. Ten bot guilds, guild wars over grind maps, and couples marrying in Amoria.
  • MapleRoot content. Custom jobs and skills, rebirths, Arcane River and Grandis maps, and a 5th tier for every class.

How to play

  1. Get the launcher. SlumsMS is friends-only: ask an admin for the download and your key.
  2. Add your key. If the launcher asks for one, open Settings (the gear), paste the key and press Save.
  3. Install. Press Install. The first download is about 23.6 GB and resumes if it gets interrupted. Updates only fetch what changed.
  4. Make an account. On the game's login screen, type a new username and a passphrase of at least 6 characters; the account is made for you.
  5. Say hi. A short tour opens at your first login. Type @guide to see it again.

More detail, including the fix for the Parsec start-up error, is in Getting started.
