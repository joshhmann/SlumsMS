This is what we are working on, in order. It is a plan, not a promise: things can move, change or drop, and we will say so here when they do. Nothing below has a date. "In testing" means it is built and being tried out, but has not been checked in a real game session yet.

## 1.6.6: next {#next}

- [New] Regulars finishing Zakum's party quest part one past stage 6. *In testing.* Today they give up at stage 6, and one key in the mines is out of their reach, so you do those yourself.
- [Fix] Better rope routes on many maps, so regulars reach places they used to get stuck at. *In testing.*
- [Fix] Regulars no longer stand frozen in the air off-screen after they are moved into a map. *In testing.*
- [Fix] Every throwing star and bullet can be recharged at a general store. *In testing.*

## 1.6.7 and 1.6.8: a big upgrade for your alts {#alts}

Your own alts are meant to be good partners in boss fights. The goal: you clear Zakum and Horntail with alts that have these features, and we measure each run. Planned, may change.

- **Potions:** alts drink their own potions at set levels, and ask you when they are out of potions or mesos.
- **Boss deaths and revive:** rules for when an alt rests or sits out, and a way for you to bring one back with a cooldown.
- **Target picking:** alts help on your target and follow a per-boss order, for example Zakum's arms before the body.
- **Positioning by job:** melee classes close in, ranged classes spread out on higher ground.
- **Joining late:** an alt that arrives after the fight starts can still join.
- **Party buffs and Dispel:** real buffs on alts, and curing when sealed.
- **Arriving one at a time** instead of all together, and a few small quality-of-life items.
- **A new NPC, Pia,** modelled on Slums-chan.

Still being designed, with no final plan yet, so these may change or not happen: a revive wait that grows with each death or a way to pay mesos to come back at once, alts catching up when they get stuck, simple chat reports and settings, alts looting their own kills by default, and selling junk or following your quests only when you ask.

## 1.7.1: regulars get the same {#regulars}

After 1.7.0, the regulars in your party get the same upgrades. The alt features are built so they can be switched on for them too.

## Known gaps today {#gaps}

- Regulars do not finish most party quests on their own yet. Zakum part one needs your help at stage 6 and at one key; Orbis and Ludibrium have stages they cannot clear; Magatia and Ellin are planned for later.
- Regulars and alts are fully watched in real runs only in Zakum and Horntail, and only in trial runs before release. The other boss fights are still first versions.
