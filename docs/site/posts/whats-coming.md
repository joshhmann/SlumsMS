This is what we are working on, in order. It is a plan, not a promise: things can move, change or drop, and we will say so here when they do. Nothing below has a date. "In testing" means it is built and being tried out, but has not been checked in a real game session yet.

**Engine fixes first:** we're reviewing years of fixes from the open-source server our game builds on and bringing over the ones that apply.

## 1.6.6: next {#next}

- [New] Regulars finishing Zakum's party quest part one past stage 6. *In testing.* Today they give up at stage 6, and one key in the mines is out of their reach, so you do those yourself.
- [Fix] Better rope routes on many maps, so regulars reach places they used to get stuck at. *In testing.*
- [Fix] Regulars no longer stand frozen in the air off-screen after they are moved into a map. *In testing.*
- [Fix] Every throwing star and bullet can be recharged at a general store. *In testing.*

## 1.6.7 and 1.6.8: a big upgrade for your alts {#alts}

Your own alts are meant to be good partners in boss fights. The goal: you clear Zakum and Horntail with alts that have these features, and we measure each run. Planned, may change. The first wave is being built now.

- **Coming back after a death:** a revive wait that grows with each death, or pay mesos to bring an alt back at once.
- **Potions:** alts drink their own potions at set levels and warn you when they are low, and before a boss run the leader sees who is short.
- **Catching up:** an alt that gets stuck catches up to you.
- **Joining late:** an alt that arrives after the fight starts can still join.
- **Helping on your target:** alts pick targets the way your own party does.
- **Simple chat reports and settings:** ask an alt for a short report and change a few settings.
- **Looting:** alts loot their own kills by default. They sell junk or follow your quests only when you ask.
- **A new NPC, Pia,** modelled on Slums-chan. The design is not settled yet.

## Later {#later}

Ideas we may do after that, with no version yet: a set kill order for each boss, positioning by job, real party buffs and Dispel from alts, and alts arriving one at a time.

## 1.7.0: checking everything {#certify}

Not a feature release. Before we call it 1.7.0 we check the whole game end to end: the main paths a player takes, a day-long run with many regulars, and restoring from a backup.

## 1.7.1: regulars get the same {#regulars}

After 1.7.0, the regulars in your party get the same upgrades. The alt features are built so they can be switched on for them too.

## Known gaps today {#gaps}

- Regulars do not finish most party quests on their own yet. Zakum part one needs your help at stage 6 and one key; Orbis and Ludibrium have stages they cannot clear; Magatia and Ellin are planned for later.
- Regulars and alts are fully watched in real runs only in Zakum and Horntail, in trial runs before release. The other boss fights are first versions.
