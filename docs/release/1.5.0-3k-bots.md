# 1.5.0: 3,000 roaming bots (deploy notes)

Owner decision 2026-10-23: 1.5.0 ships with 3,000 roaming bots on live, a `-Xms4G -Xmx6G` heap and a per-map bot cap
starting at 50. The settings are in `docs/release/1.5.0.conf` (`SERVER_SET`, `UNIT_EDITS`); nothing here is applied by
hand.

## The soak behind it

Stepped soak on 164 (12 cores / 48 GB LXC, shared with live), 2026-10-22/23 UTC, rc5 jar, results in
`/opt/mapleroot-dev/soak3k/metrics/summary.txt`. At 3,000 director bots (3,182 online with the environment, roster
9,000, 6 h): about 0.4 core (cpu med 40 %, p95 57 %), RSS 3.5 GB, heap used max 2.9 GB, old gen after GC max 1.8 GB,
GC pauses p50 8 ms / max 31 ms, no full GC, 0 errors; live's own CPU and memory were unaffected. Channels
1,494/821/523/299/224. The busiest maps reached 45-57 bots summed over all 5 channels (Showa 600020300, Henesys,
El Nath 211040300, Sleepywood 105070001...), so a single map instance stays well under 50 at this population.

## Roster growth: no prep boot

The roster's size is `BOT_ROSTER_PEAK_ONLINE x BOT_ROSTER_SIZE_MULTIPLE` = 9,000. Live's roster is about 2,500-3,600
active (seeded 2,508 on 1.3.0, then +40 new players a day; veterans only fill while the roster is under half its
size). With the defaults that is about a week to 4,500 and then about 110 days at 40 a day, and on the first evenings
the director has fewer than 3,000 members to put online (every one of them online, no variety).

The soak reached 9,000 with a prep boot (fill below 1.0, 57,600 veterans a day: 2,400 an hour, 154 s of work an
hour). For live, with players on, the safer middle: **fill below 1.0, 7,200 veterans a day** = about 300 veterans in
each hourly lifecycle step, about 20 s of work on the director's thread (the soak's ~64 ms a bot). The roster reaches
about 9,000 some 18-22 h after the deploy and then stops by itself: once it is at its size there is no room, and the
0.2 %/day quitters are refilled by the 40 new players a day before any veteran. No second restart is needed. 1.5.1
sets the two keys back to 0.5 and 300 (`SERVER_SET`).

Before the deploy (read-only), to know the starting point:

```sql
SELECT Status, COUNT(*) FROM botroster GROUP BY Status;
```

After the deploy, watch the server log for `Roster: N new players, ~300 veterans, ...` once an hour, `!roster` /
`!env` for the director's target and online count, and `!botmapcap` for the busiest maps. If the hourly step should
stop early, nothing else depends on it: the director runs on whatever roster exists.

## Per-map bot cap (`BOT_MAP_CAP`, `BOT_MAP_CAP_OVERRIDES`)

`soloMapling.ArtificialPlayer.BotMapCap`. At most `BOT_MAP_CAP` bots (default 50; 0 = off) are put on one map
instance (one map on one channel) by the systems that choose where a bot goes on its own. Optional per-map caps:
`BOT_MAP_CAP_OVERRIDES: "910000000:60,100000000:70"` (map id:cap; 0 = no cap on that map; a malformed entry is skipped
with a warning). Not set on live.

Where it hooks in:

- **Director login placement** (`BotPopulationDirector.logInClaimed`): the bot's home map on the reserved channel;
  full -> the same map on the channel with room and the fewest bots on it (the channel reservation moves with it);
  else the map's return town (same channel first); else the login waits for a later tick. New beginners and returning
  roster bots go through the same path.
- **Grind picks** (`TrainingMapChooser.choose`): maps full on the bot's channel join the crowd-cooldown exclusions, so
  the leveling path, region moves, the local search and the first-trip warp all skip them. A bot grinding where it
  stands is not an addition. A world-event answer and the walk back after a death fall through to a normal pick when
  their map is full (`TrainingBot`).
- **Town wandering** (`TownWandererBot`, `HenesysBot`): full family maps are not picked.
- **FM browsing** (`FMBot`): a full room is passed over for the next; with all twelve full the bot browses the entrance
  and tries again.
- **Bot shops** (`Economy.BotShops.findSpot`): a shop is not opened in a full FM room.

Exempt but counted (they are placed on purpose and do not roam, or follow a person): environment set pieces
(merchants, dealers, casino tables, gacha, tutorial, the boot town dressing), bots following or partied with a real
player, chat-asked walks, story scenes (weddings, drama, guild boss runs), a grinder's short errands to its home town
or the sauna, GM commands. They count toward the map's load, so roaming bots fill what is left.

Bots already on a map are never moved; the cap only stops additions, so a map can sit over its cap for a while and
drains as bots leave. Cost: each `MapleMap` keeps a bot counter (updated in `addPlayer`/`removePlayer` under the map's
lock), and instances at or over their cap are kept in a small set; a pick reads the counter or that set, never a player
list.

Read-outs:

- `!botmapcap` (GM 4): the cap and overrides, picks turned away since boot, and the 5 busiest map instances of each
  channel as `mapId bots/cap` (`!` = over the cap); `!botmapcap <n>` for n per channel, `!botmapcap <mapId>` for one map
  on every channel.
- Auto-QA health sweep check (g) `mapCap` (`docs/ops/qa-suite.md`): WARN when an instance holds more bots than its cap.
