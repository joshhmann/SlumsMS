# 1.5.0 rc3 economy lane: bot shops, direct trades, price board, meso sinks, gear audit

Branch `economy-trade-150` (from `test-1.5.0-rc2`). The owner decided all of this in the 2026-10-21 grill. Every
number is in `src/main/java/soloMapling/Economy/economy.yaml` with the same safe default in code; any key can be
overridden with `-Dsolomapling.economy.<key>=<value>`, and `!economy reload` re-reads the file. The tables below are
for the owner to veto: change the yaml, nothing else.

## What was built (package `soloMapling.Economy`)

| Owner decision | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Bot shops | `BotShops`, `SaleRules`, `GearGrade` | About 10% of grinding bots (level 15+) open a hired merchant under their own name in FM rooms 1-12 on channel 1 at a town stop: up to 8 world-wide, 6 h each. They sell their own drops and scrolls (bag only) and items bots bought from players (resale pool, +15%). Never: godly gear, Chaos/White scrolls, boss-only drops (from `drop_data`: every dropper a boss, plus a yaml list), cash, untradeable/expiring items. Takings (after the tax) are paid at the bot's next town stop. FM shopper bots and consignment skip these shops. |
| 1. Prices from real trades | `PriceBook` | 7-day median of player shop / merchant sales, single-item meso trades, bot shop sales and direct trades. Bot purchases seed it (max 6 per item per day) until an item has 5 trades with a real player; fewer than 3 samples shows the old static value. Equips are stored as a clean-equivalent price and scaled by each equip's quality. |
| 2. Budget for bots buying from players | `EconomyMeters.tryBotBuy`, `BotBuying`, `FMBot` | World budget 40m a day on top of 56A (1.0x fair value, 5m per seller account) and the FM allowance. From players, bots buy gear only when it is above average (not godly), at the board's fair price. What they buy goes to the resale pool. |
| 3. Direct trades | `BotDirectTrade`, `TradeSession`, `PriceBand`, `HaggleBrain` | Invite a grinding, social or wandering bot to trade. **Sell:** put an item in, the bot offers inside 70-95% of fair value and only raises. It confirms after you lock, and only if the items are the ones it priced. **Buy:** ask in chat ("how much for the work gloves"); it names a price inside 95-125% and remembers it for 5 min, then puts the item in the window. **Haggling:** in chat or the window. Rules, then the Decide model (no reasoning), read the move; `PriceBand` decides the price in code; the chat brain only words it, and a reply that drops the price is replaced by the scripted line. Rivals and nemeses refuse through affinity's `refusesTrade`. Merchant-type bots keep their own flows. |
| 4. Gifts | `BotGifts`, hook in `BotPlayerReaction` | A friend bot (affinity public API) that notices you: 3% chance, at most once per 7 days per bot and player (`botgifts`), 20 a day world-wide. Potions for your level (the bot pays the NPC price), a common 60%+ scroll from its bag, or 100k-500k of its own mesos (only if it keeps half its ceiling). Nothing is minted. |
| 5. Price board | `@price <item>` | Median, range and sale count for up to 3 matching items. |
| 6. Meso sinks | `MesoSinks`, `Shop`, `StorageProcessor`, `NPCTalkHandler` | FM shop tax, NPC price bumps, storage upkeep by slot, and the cosmetics merchant (Hanako, FM entrance). |
| 7. Gear audit | below; `GearGrade`, `ScrollRules` / `BotScrolling` tuning | Measured, then proposed. Drop and scroll rates are **unchanged**. Bot scrolling is tuned so bot-made godlies stay rare. |
| 8. GM + QA | `!economy`, `!qa run tr-1..tr-7` | See "QA steps". |

## Gear audit (read-only, 2026-10-21) and proposed numbers

Sources:
- `mapleroot_soak` (live-like soak, read-only SELECTs): `drop_data`, bot gear in `inventoryequipment`, `botstore`.
- WZ in `/root/work/bake/wz`.
- The friends' real characters are **not** in the soak (only test accounts), and the other test schemas were not
  readable from here, so "what the friends have" is still open.

**Drops** (effective = raw × 4 for common mobs, × 3 for bosses):

| | Common mobs (757) | Bosses (249) |
|---|---|---|
| Equip row, median chance per kill | 0.28% | 6.6% |
| Scroll row, median per kill | 0.12% | 0.9% |
| Any equip per kill (median mob) | 4.2% | 33% |
| Any scroll per kill (median mob) | 0.6% | 12% |

- Boss-only items: 508 distinct, including 215 equips and 43 scrolls. Examples: Zakum Helmets, Timeless/Reverse,
  MV's/Pink Bean/Scarlion/Targar hats, Balrog's Twilight 5%. Bots never sell these.
- Chaos 60% and White Scroll are global drops at 1,200 per 1e6 (about 1 in 833 kills) and are not scaled by the
  drop rate.

**Scroll success** is the WZ odds: `SCROLL_CHANCE_ROLLS` = 1 in the repo's config.

| Scroll | Success | Boom on fail | Gain (weapon / glove) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100% | 100% | none | +1 ATT |
| 60% / 65% | 60% / 65% | none | +2 ATT (+1 stat on weapons) / +2 |
| 10% / 15% | 10% / 15% | none | +5 ATT +3 stat / +3 |
| Dark 70% / 30% | 70% / 30% | 50% | as the 60% / 10% |

**Bot gear now:**
- 62,416 bot equips, 27,709 of them equipped.
- **None has been scrolled yet**: every item has level 0 and full slots.
- So gains are only the drop roll: weapon ATT p99 +5, non-weapon ATT max +1, main stats max +2.
- Godly candidates today: 0.

**Simulated bot scrolling.** This uses `ScrollRules` and the WZ odds, all slots used, no white scrolls.

| Godly line | Old rules (normal long shot 25%, gamblers 15%) | Proposed (10%, gamblers 10%) |
|---|---|---|
| 8-slot weapon, +17 ATT from scrolls | 0.66% | ~0.43% |
| 8-slot weapon, +20 ATT ("god" tier) | 0.19% | ~0.10% |
| 7-slot weapon, +15 ATT | 1.28% | ~0.80% |
| 5-slot glove, +11 ATT | 0.29% | ~0.20% |

**Proposed numbers.** The first five are **in code now as defaults**; veto by changing the yaml.

1. **The godly rule** (`gear.*`):
   - attack gain over clean above 2 × slots, plus 5 on weapons for the drop roll;
   - or main-stat gain above 2 × slots + 2.
   - That is the most 60% scrolls can give, so only 10%/30% passes or white scrolls get past it.
   - In practice: an 8-slot weapon at +22 ATT, a 5-slot glove at +11.
   - The audit warned that lower lines (weapon +12, glove +5/+8) would make 17-68% of ordinary 60%-scrolled gear
     "godly".
2. **Bot scrolling:**
   - normal long-shot chance 25% -> **10%** (`scrolling.normalLongShotChance`);
   - gamblers 15% -> **10%** of bots, so normal bots are 50% (`scrolling.gamblerPercent`).
3. **No white scrolls on long shots** for bots (`scrolling.whiteOnLongShots: false`). Whites are the one input that
   would push godlies past target. Careful bots now never take a 10%.
4. **World cap:** after **5** bot-made godlies in a day (`scrolling.godliesPerDay`, meter `godlies`), every bot uses
   only 60%+ non-dark scrolls until the next day.
5. **The "ScrollPerfect" megaphone** fires only for a godly result. Careful bots with the 69k NPC 100% weapon scrolls
   would otherwise megaphone every 8-slot weapon.
6. **Targets for the owner to confirm:** under 1% of bot-scrolled items godly, and under 0.25% at the "god" tier.
   The simulation puts the new rules at about 0.4-0.8% and 0.1%.
7. **Rates: no change proposed.** Bot holdings show the market is supply-driven: 11,797 scrolls in bags, mostly
   Claw ATT 10%. Keep 20 rolls per bot per day.

## QA steps

On the test server, `!qa run <id>`. Logs go to `logs/qa/tr-N-*.log`, under topic TRADE.

| Id | What to do |
|---|---|
| `tr-1 [room]` | Channel 1. Warps you to FM room 1; a bot opens its shop next to you. The Chaos Scroll it holds must not be listed. Buy something: you see the sale, the 3% tax, the price sample, and "owed to the bot". `!qa do collect` pays the bot. |
| `tr-2` | A bot with 10m; you get Glove ATT 60% x2 and Sword ATT 60% x2. Invite it, put a scroll in: it offers. Haggle ("1.5m?"), lock: it confirms inside 70-95%. Swap the item after its offer: it closes. `!qa do sim [line]` runs the haggle headless. |
| `tr-3` | A bot holding a sword. Say "how much for the sword?" (or `!qa do quote sword`): it names a price. Offer less: it counters, never under 95%. Open a trade: the sword is in the window. |
| `tr-4` | A friend bot with 5m. Walk past it: a gift (certain for the run). `!qa do gift potions\|scroll\|mesos` forces one. |
| `tr-5 [item]` | The board's detail and the tax at 100k/1m/5m/25m/100m are logged. `@price <name>` in game. Every sample and fee while it runs. |
| `tr-6 [fair]` | 11 haggle lines, prompt injections included, both ways. SUMMARY: **0 answers outside the band**. `!qa do line <text>` tries yours. |
| `tr-7` | FM entrance with +50m (taken back at stop). Buy from Hanako, store items (upkeep line), buy pet food. `!qa do meters`. |

GM characters cannot use the trade window with bots unless the server runs `-Dslumsms.gm.allowGiving=true`
(ticket 43). Otherwise use a non-GM character for tr-2 and tr-3, and the `sim` and `quote` steps.

`!economy` covers the same ground outside QA:
- `!economy` shows the meters, shops and open trades;
- `!economy shop [bot]` stages a shop, and `!economy close <bot>` closes it;
- `!economy gift [bot] [kind]` forces a gift;
- `!economy quote [bot] <item>` makes a bot name a price;
- `!economy price <id>` and `!economy tax <price>` read out the board and the tax;
- `!economy haggle <fair> <line>` runs a haggle test;
- `!economy reload` re-reads economy.yaml.
