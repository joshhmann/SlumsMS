## Player notes (draft)

### Party quests

- [New] Bots run party quests themselves: Kerning City, Orbis, Ludibrium, Henesys, the six Cafe areas, LudiMaze, Pirate, Treasure, Save Delli, Magatia, Ellin Forest, Crimsonwood Keep and the Sharenian guild quest. They answer the NPCs, collect the coupons and passes, hit the reactors, take the portals and clear the stages on their own timers.
- [New] A party quest can be started by the bots themselves from a lobby, or led by you with bots and your own alts following.

### Bosses

- [New] Bot parties share boss expedition fights instead of standing outside: Zakum, Horntail, Chaos Zakum, the five Twin Dragon caves, Maha, Castellan, Papulatus, Krexel, Showa, Balrog, Scarga, Elemental Thanatos, Pink Bean, Captain Latanica, Magnus, the four Hard bosses (Will, Lucid, Verus Hilla, Darknell), the six GuardianNex caves, the eight BossRush waves and the Zakum-mines chest hunt.
- [New] Chaos Zakum is playable at the same altar, as its own expedition, with the arms and the bodies in the order the client expects.
- [New] A friendly bot can tell you how a run ended, in one line, if that is switched on.

> Every bot party quest and boss row is switch-gated and off by default. Server owners turn on what they want with `-Dslums.mechanics` (party quests), `-Dslums.mechanics.boss` (bosses) and, per row, `-Dslums.mechanics.<row>`.

### Fixes

- [Fix] Items dropped by bots on the ground could stop a gate, chest or altar reacting to the other items lying on it. A drop's owner was looked up through a client the server had cached, and a bot's drop carried one that was already gone; item reactors now get the drop's own owner, so the gate, the chest and the altar react again.
- [Fix] A boss part that revives into a monster the data does not have no longer breaks the death handling: the missing form is skipped, named once in the server log, and the rest of the death - the loot, the clear, the next form - runs. Before, that one id could stall every death on the map.
