# 1.5.0 release notes: Gachapon odds

Paste into the 1.5.0 patch notes. Regenerate after any pool change with
`python3 tools/gachapon/gachapon_pools.py odds --markdown`.

**Gachapon is back, one machine per town.** Each town's machine gives that town's v83 flavour (Perion: warrior gear,
Ellinia: mage gear and scrolls, Kerning: thief gear, ...) plus a shared list, cleaned up (no GM scrolls, boss sacks,
expiring items, beginner shop weapons). One ticket, one prize. Rare and jackpot prizes are announced to the whole
server. `@gacha <town>` lists every prize; the machine shows its odds. Every one of the 12 towns has a machine
standing in town now (new ones in Ellinia, Perion, Kerning City, Sleepywood, Mushroom Shrine and New Leaf City), and
a **Remote Gachapon Ticket** plays any of them from anywhere.

| Tier | Chance per ticket | Server notice |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 70% | no |
| Uncommon | 24.5% (1 in 4) | no |
| Rare | 5% (1 in 20) | yes |
| Jackpot | 0.5% (1 in 200) | yes |

- **Jackpot:** Chaos Scroll 60% and White Scroll (each about 1 in 970 tickets), and MapleRoot's chairs from the old
  chair machine (any one of them about 1 in 340). Sleepywood also has Ilbi Throwing-Stars.
- **Rare:** 10% weapon and glove ATT scrolls, 30% and dark 30% scrolls, level 70+ gear, the classic v83 chairs, NX
  cosmetics. A given rare item is about 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 2,400 tickets, depending on the town.
- **Uncommon:** 60% weapon and glove ATT scrolls, 10% armour scrolls, dark 70% scrolls, level 40-69 gear, work
  gloves, food buffs.
- **Common:** 100% and 60% armour scrolls, level 11-39 gear, potions, ores.
- A full inventory tab refuses the roll and keeps your ticket.
- Bots play too, with their own mesos; now and then one of them hits a jackpot.
