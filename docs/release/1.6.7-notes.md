## Player lines (draft)

- [New] Your alts follow you more like a player: they climb ropes and ladders after you, get themselves unstuck, keep out of the way at party quest entrances, and greet you when you're back.
- [New] Your alts tell you what they did, ask before dropping your items, and quest with you: take the quest you take, count your kills and turn it in alongside you.
- [New] Your alts' settings: per alt, turn heals, support and buff potions on or off and set when it drinks HP and MP potions. Kept when you relog.
- [Change] After a boss revive your alt rests until it's back to 70% HP and MP, drinking its own potions.
- [New] A minute before an update restarts the server, everyone in game gets a notice.
- [Fix] Shops no longer list stars and bullets they don't sell with a price.
- [Fix] Chaos scrolls can roll every value, including the full drop.
- [Fix] Cat's Eyes for the Fancy Sword quest drop from Meerkats, as the item says.
- [Fix] The Cash Shop's Surprise no longer gives a package item.
- [Change] Rebirth no longer offers a swap back to a previous rebirth class.
- [Fix] The statue a character earns at level 200 is now created properly, even right after a server restart.
- [New] Your alts drink potions earlier in boss fights, and tell you when they're running low or out.
- [New] When an alt goes down in a boss fight, it stays with you and gets back up after a short wait. You can also pay
  to bring it back right away, or use `@botme revive`.
