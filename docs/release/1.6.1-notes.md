# SlumsMS 1.6.1 — patch notes draft (players)

Draft, filled in as 1.6.1 work lands on release-1.6.1. Not published.

## Slums Stamps (ticket 111)
- New: **Slums Stamps**, one balance per account shared by all your characters. They can't be traded.
- Earn them from the Daily Check-In (`@daily`): 1 stamp every day you claim, and 6 on days 7, 14, 21 and 28 —
  48 over the 28-day cycle. The claim says "+1 Slums Stamp, day 4/28" in yellow.
- Spend them at Rooty: Donor NX cosmetics cost 6 stamps, the High-Five Stamps 40. Rooty shows your balance.
- `@stamps` shows your balance, your check-in day and the next bonus day. `@points` shows stamps instead of vote
  points.
- Billion Coins and vote points are retired: every Billion Coin you had (bags, storage, your shop, Duey) and every
  vote point became 6 stamps.

## Family (ticket 53)
- New: the **Family** button works. Add a junior (same map, level 11+, within 20 levels), earn reputation when your
  juniors hunt and level, and spend it in the Family window.
- Every perk works now: Family Reunion and Summon Family, **My Drop Rate / EXP 1.5x and 2x** (15 or 30 minutes),
  **My Party Drop Rate / EXP 2x** (30 minutes, your party on your channel) and **Family Bonding** (2x EXP and drops
  for 30 minutes for you and your juniors' line, with 6 of them online). Each once a day. Family buffs don't stack
  with each other (the best one counts) but do stack with coupons.
- Regular bots can be your juniors too: they say yes to people they know, have partied or chatted with, and now and
  then one asks to be yours.

## Bots talk to each other (ticket 114)
- Bots now greet the bots they know when they run into each other, chat a little, and ask each other things: to
  party up, to come along to another town, for help training, to buy something, or "did u hear...". The other bot
  answers the way it actually feels — yes, no, or "how about Ellinia instead?", warmly, curtly or rudely — and then
  they really do it: they party, head to town together, trade.
- Groups of friends standing together chat as a group, taking turns.
- Friends hang out together: when one heads to a town, its friends tend to follow a few seconds later.
- Talk near them to join in; the bot that answers you talks to you like any bot does.

## NX exchange (ticket 113) — staff note, not for players yet
- Not in this release for players: the exchange stays switched off on live (owner 2026-10-28). On TEST it is on:
  `@nx` to trade NX for mesos in game, `!qa run xc-1`, auto-QA t-xc-1.
