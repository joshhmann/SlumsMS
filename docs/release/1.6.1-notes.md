## Player notes (from the release book, 2026-10-29, notes1 v6)

### Training after 120

- The Future, Twilight Perion and lower Grandis (Pantheon, Savage Terminal's streets, Sanctuary, the Caravan Refuge, Fox Ridge, Cheong-woon Valley, Ristonia, Toolen City) open at level 120 or after your 1st rebirth. Get there by the Henesys teleporter, the Hyper Teleport Rock, or the Gate of Time, which also goes to Leafre of the Past (level 124 Corrupted monsters).
- Deeper Grandis (Heliseum, Tyrant's Castle, the Fox Tree, Cernium, the rougher back streets) opens at your 1st rebirth and Arcane River at your 2nd, at any level.
- Monsters there have health that fits their level and pay about twice the Temple of Time, climbing higher up. They drop mesos, potions, ores, scrolls, 4th-job mastery books and gear; rarely Eclectic or Muspell armour, and once in a long while Pensalir armour or a Jaihin, Utgard or AbsoLab weapon.
- Leafre and Minar Forest, the Temple of Time and Ulu City give 40% more EXP.
- Lion King's Castle and the Rose Garden are reworked: level-fair monsters, good EXP, real drops. The castle needs no rebirth, and you can walk from the Fifth Tower roof into the Rose Garden.
- Regulars of level 120+ train there too.
- **Cygnus and Magnus are rebalanced** to fit their level: Cygnus 110M HP, Magnus 2.1B.

### New for everyone

- **Achievements and medals**: 32 one-time goals, each a cosmetic medal; a yellow line tells you. `@achievements` lists them. Old job, level, guild, marriage and rebirths count at your next login.
- **Family** works: every perk (Reunion and Summon on any channel, the 1.5x/2x rates, party 2x, Bonding), once a day each. Perks stack with coupons and with EXP and drop events, and survive a restart. Regulars can be your juniors, earn you rep while they train, and come when summoned.
- **Slums Stamps**: one account balance, earned from `@daily` (48 over 28 days; level 10+, a gap over 48 h restarts the streak). Spend at Rooty: Donor NX cosmetics 6 each, the Rose Vac and the Hyper Teleport Rock 40 each. Billion Coins and vote points became 6 stamps each, wherever they sat.
- **Slums-chan**, the new guide, welcomes you once and stands in the Base of Roots and Henesys.
- **Start on Maple Island**: every level 1-9 starter character is asked once (new ones and existing ones that never were); Shanks sails you to Lith Harbor free at level 7.
- **@invite**: level 30+ characters make up to 2 invite codes a week, in game or on the website.
- **@bug** tells you your report number and attaches your versions, map and recent steps; reports from the game, launcher and website land in one place.
- **Lazy Penguins** nap in 15 towns; click one.
- **Monster Book**: Arcane River and Grandis monsters drop Monster Book cards, the book lists them, and their Dropping tabs show the cards. (#77: rc9 shows no drops for level 130+ monsters)
- **New commands**: `@stamps` shows your balance and next bonus day; `@points` now shows stamps; `@achievements all` lists every goal. `@daily` explains its rules and says why a claim is refused.

### Regulars

- Bots greet the bots they know, chat, and ask each other to party, come along, help or trade; a yes is carried out, and a bot that can't says no. Friends follow each other to town.
- Regulars read the last few lines of the chat before they answer, and remember your last few whispers to them, so a follow-up makes sense to them.
- They take real threats seriously (death wishes, slurs, real-life threats); friendly trash talk like "ur so trash" is fine.
- Now and then a regular asks to be your junior; the offer stands for 30 minutes.
- Regulars who reach 200 can be reborn: they earn the key items in real boss runs (and join yours), then re-gear at level 1.

### Game client (update through the launcher; older clients are asked to update)

- **Make the screen yours.** The first time you log in, a card shows you **Edit UI** (Ctrl+F11): move and resize the bars, the status bar and the quick slots. "Show me" points at it. You see it once.
- **New HUD modules** in the HUD window (Ctrl+F10), both off until you tick them:
  - **EXP rate:** your EXP per hour, how long until your next level, and what you've earned this session.
  - **DPS:** your damage per second and the fight's total. In a party, after a boss or a big monster dies, it also shows who did the most damage, with each player's share.
- **Short damage numbers.** Tick "Short damage numbers" in the HUD window and big hits show as 12.3K, 1.23M or 2.14B instead of long rows of digits. Off by default.
- **Hide other players' cash effects.** A Display switch in the HUD window for busy maps: the glows and sparkles from other players' cash items go away. Their gear still shows, and so do your own effects.
- **Borderless window** and **sharp text on scaled screens**, both off: switch them in the launcher's Game options once the launcher update arrives, a day or two after this patch. They stay the right size when you change the game's resolution.
- **Crash reports.** If the game crashes, it writes a plain-language note to `crash_reports\LATEST_CRASH.txt` in your game folder: what happened, the map you were on and what the game was loading. Your password, account and chat are never in it. Attach it to a `@bug` report.
- **Fixed:** some custom images could make the game close on its own (for example when the first damage number showed).

### Launcher 1.6.1 (its own post, a day or two later)

- **Report a bug** opens the website's form with your launcher and game versions filled in.
- **Game options**: Borderless window and Sharp on scaled displays (they need the 1.6.1 game client).
- Settings shows which server each channel plays on, and the launcher checks other channels only at start-up or when you change server, not every 10 minutes.
- When you're signed in, the launcher downloads updates with a pass tied to your account.

### Website

- **Most hunted**: the monsters hunted most in the last 7 days and 24 hours. Counts include off-screen kills, so they're marked estimated.
- **My invites**: make, list and cancel your invite codes on the website (same 2 a week as `@invite`). The Join and FAQ pages explain how to get a code.
- The **bug form** attaches your versions and lets you pick your character.
- Shorter page addresses; old links still work.

### Fixes

- Permanent pets and items no longer show a 1970 expiry.
- The Adventurer's Bag only takes what it can give back exactly (untradeable, karma'd, expiring, name-tagged and account-shared stacks stay out); looting with its box open no longer loses items.
- Slums-chan, the Lazy Penguins and other custom NPCs no longer stand behind scenery.
- Two bots no longer say the same small-talk line back to back.

  of Roots and in Henesys: talk to her for the guide and your settings.

### Slums Stamps (ticket 111)
- New: **Slums Stamps**, one balance per account shared by all your characters. They can't be traded.
- Earn them from the Daily Check-In (`@daily`): 1 stamp every day you claim, and 6 on days 7, 14, 21 and 28 —
  48 over the 28-day cycle. The claim says "+1 Slums Stamp, day 4/28" in yellow.
- Spend them at Rooty: Donor NX cosmetics cost 6 stamps, the Rose Vac (your pet picks up all your loot on the map) and the Hyper Teleport Rock 40 each. Rooty shows your balance.
- `@stamps` shows your balance, your check-in day and the next bonus day. `@points` shows stamps instead of vote
  points.
- Billion Coins and vote points are retired: every Billion Coin you had (bags, storage, your shop, Duey) and every
  vote point became 6 stamps.

### Family (ticket 53)
- New: the **Family** button works. Add a junior (same map, level 11+, within 20 levels), earn reputation when your
  juniors hunt and level, and spend it in the Family window.
- Every perk works now: **Family Reunion** (go to a family member, on any channel: you change channel and land on
  their map) and **Summon Family** (call one to you, from any channel), **My Drop Rate / EXP 1.5x and 2x** (15 or 30
  minutes), **My Party Drop Rate / EXP 2x** (30 minutes, everyone in your party who is online when you use it, on any
  channel) and **Family Bonding** (2x EXP and drops for 30 minutes for you and your juniors' line, with 6 of them
  online; regular juniors count and get it too). Each once a day. Family buffs don't stack with each other (the best one counts); they do stack with
  coupons. They also stack with EXP and drop events (a world event hot spot, a staff rate event), as the perks' descriptions now say. Your buff survives a server restart.
- Regular bots can be your juniors too: they say yes to people they know, have partied or chatted with (always; never
  to strangers). A regular junior earns you reputation while it trains, up to 300 a day, and Family Bonding buffs the
  regular juniors it counts as well. Now and
  then one asks to be yours (a whisper). Its offer stands for 30 minutes: open the Family window, Add Junior, type its
  name, even if it has wandered off, and it says yes. Summon Family on a regular junior works like on a player: it
  comes to you, unless it is busy with something it can't drop (a party quest, a party with someone else, a shop it
  runs...), and then you are told so and nothing is spent.

### Regulars rebirth (ticket 30)
- Regulars who reach level 200 can now be reborn the way you are: they earn the key item from boss drops, take it to
  Wyatt the Reborn in the Base of Roots, and start again at level 1 with the rebirth EXP rate, keeping their gear.
- They earn the items where you do. Regulars form their own Von Leon and Cygnus runs (with strong regulars who are
  free), and join your expedition for any of the key bosses when you open one near them; the item comes from the
  kill. Zakum, Horntail and Pink Bean they also run where nobody is watching, on your rules: the same daily clear limit,
  and only if their group can really kill the boss in time. The Cygnus Knight Emblem they hunt like you do.
- After the rebirth they wear what a level 1 can and keep the rest, putting it back on as they level.
- **You can make your Essence Stone now (branch 161-stones, not on TEST yet; check t-rb-3).** Talk to Wyatt the Reborn
  in the Base of Roots and pick "Make an Essence Stone". He makes the stone your next rebirth needs from two boss items:
  Blue from a Zakum Diamond and a Nine Spirit's Egg, Red from a Rock of Time and Von Leon's Seal, Black from a Cygnus
  Circlet, while you hold a Cygnus Knight Emblem (the emblem is not used up). Without both he tells you what you have and what is still missing; you can hold
  one stone at a time. (Before this only regulars could make them: the old @craft was switched off.)
- It takes them time: only a few are reborn a day across the whole server, and not every regular bothers. Over the
  coming weeks you will start to meet reborn regulars, and later a few in Grandis and Arcane River.

### Your alts fight with you (ticket 13) — held: not for players until the owner says ship
(Staff: built on branch 161-alts 2026-10-29, merged into release-1.6.1, on TEST after this deploy; live keeps OWNED_ALTS_SUMMON_ENABLED=false in
1.6.1.conf. Check t-oa-1. Gate rules and details: docs/tickets/13-owned-alt-bots.md. The lines below are the draft.)
- `@botme <name>` calls one of your other characters into your party. It follows you everywhere you walk, and after
  a boat it catches up when you arrive.
- On a hunting map it fights what you fight - the monsters you hit and the ones that hit you or it - with its own
  skills, stats, MP and potions, and picks up its own and your party's drops into its own bag (it tells you when its
  bag is full).
- It never takes another player's monster or loot, and never fights in town.
- Monsters hit it and it can die: it loses EXP like you would, wakes up in town and goes home. Call it again with
  `@botme`.
- Alts play only while you do: they stop if you stand idle for 3 minutes or wander more than a screen away, and join
  in again when you move. Up to five companions (alts and bot friends together), at most six fighting alts on a map.
