## Start on Maple Island (ticket 29)
- New characters can choose: right after your first login you're asked **"Start on Maple Island?"** Yes takes you to
  Mushroom Town for the classic island and its quests (Shanks sails you to Lith Harbor when you're ready); No keeps
  the usual start. You're only asked once.

## Invite your friends
- Have a level 30+ character? Type `@invite` to make an invite code for a friend: up to 2 a week, each works once and
  never expires. `@invite list` shows which of your codes have been used.

## Slums-chan (merged, not on TEST yet; needs the 1.6.1 client)
- Meet **Slums-chan**, the new guide. She welcomes you on your first login, opens `@settings`, and stands in the Base
  of Roots and in Henesys: talk to her for the guide and your settings.

## Slums Stamps (ticket 111)
- New: **Slums Stamps**, one balance per account shared by all your characters. They can't be traded.
- Earn them from the Daily Check-In (`@daily`): 1 stamp every day you claim, and 6 on days 7, 14, 21 and 28 —
  48 over the 28-day cycle. The claim says "+1 Slums Stamp, day 4/28" in yellow.
- Spend them at Rooty: Donor NX cosmetics cost 6 stamps, the High-Five Stamps 40. Rooty shows your balance.
- `@stamps` shows your balance, your check-in day and the next bonus day. `@points` shows stamps instead of vote
  points.
- Billion Coins and vote points are retired: every Billion Coin you had (bags, storage, your shop, Duey) and every
  vote point became 6 stamps.

## Family (ticket 53)
- New: the **Family** button works. Add a junior (same map, level 11+, within 20 levels), earn reputation when your
  juniors hunt and level, and spend it in the Family window.
- Every perk works now: Family Reunion and Summon Family, **My Drop Rate / EXP 1.5x and 2x** (15 or 30 minutes),
  **My Party Drop Rate / EXP 2x** (30 minutes, your party on your channel) and **Family Bonding** (2x EXP and drops
  for 30 minutes for you and your juniors' line, with 6 of them online). Each once a day. Family buffs don't stack
  with each other (the best one counts) but do stack with coupons.
- Regular bots can be your juniors too: they say yes to people they know, have partied or chatted with, and now and
  then one asks to be yours.

## Regulars rebirth (ticket 30)
- Regulars who reach level 200 can now be reborn the way you are: they earn the key item from boss drops, take it to
  Wyatt the Reborn in the Base of Roots, and start again at level 1 with the rebirth EXP rate, keeping their gear.
- It takes them time: only a few are reborn a day across the whole server, and not every regular bothers. Over the
  coming weeks you will start to meet reborn regulars, and later a few in the rebirth-2 and rebirth-3 areas.

## Achievements and medals (ticket 93) (merged, not on TEST yet: auto-QA t-ac-1 not run)
- New: **32 achievements**, each a one-time goal that gives you a cosmetic **medal** (no stats): your job advances,
  levels 30 / 70 / 120 and your class's top level, your first kill of Mano, Stumpy, Mushmom and Jr. Balrog, clearing
  Papulatus, Zakum and Horntail, the Kerning, Ludibrium, Orbis and Pirate party quests, 10 and 100 fishing catches,
  your wedding, 50 quests, your first guild, days 7 and 28 of the Daily Check-In, touring Victoria Island, Ossyria and
  Aquarium, 10 trades, 5 Free Market purchases and your first rebirth.
- A yellow line tells you when you earn one and the medal goes to your Equip tab; with a full tab it waits until you
  have room (type `@achievements`). Old facts count at your next login (your current job and level); past boss
  clears, PQs, fish and streaks were not recorded and don't count.
- `@achievements` lists what you earned and what is under way; `@achievements all` lists every goal.
- Bots earn them too, and what they earn shows on the website's news feed.

## Fixes
- Permanent pets and items no longer look expired (they showed a 1970 date).
- Bots in the Henesys invasion walk up to a standing boss again.
- An alt you call with `@botme` lands on the ground next to you (also in hotfix 1.6.0.1).
