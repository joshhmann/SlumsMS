## Better bug reports (ticket 131) (merged c2a785e6b, on on TEST since 2026-10-28 22:09 UTC, t-br-1 PASS; live switch commented out in 1.6.1.conf until the site report_service lock patch is live)
- `@bug <what went wrong>` now tells you your report number (e.g. **Bug #B-12 sent.**) and attaches what the
  developers need automatically: your game and client version, where you are, your last few maps and steps, and any
  server errors about your character. No passwords, addresses or private chat are ever included. Up to 3 reports
  every 10 minutes.

## Start on Maple Island (ticket 29)
- New characters can choose: right after your first login you're asked **"Start on Maple Island?"** Yes takes you to
  Mushroom Town for the classic island and its quests (Shanks sails you to Lith Harbor when you're ready); No keeps
  the usual start. You're only asked once.

## Invite your friends
- Have a level 30+ character? Type `@invite` to make an invite code for a friend: up to 2 a week, each works once and
  never expires. `@invite list` shows which of your codes have been used.
- (Staff: only if the site's invite page ships with 1.6.1.) Signed in on the website, you can also make, list and
  cancel your invite codes there. Same rules and the same 2 a week: a code made in game counts on the site and the
  other way round. Status: server API merged (5eb0baea4, ticket 105, "Site invite page"), on TEST since 2026-10-28,
  auto-QA t-iv-2 PASS; no switch; the page itself is the site session's. QA: make a code on the site, then `@invite` in game says 1 left;
  cancel an unused one on the site and `@invite list` shows it "cancelled".

## Slums-chan (merged, on TEST, t-sn-1 PASS 2026-10-28; needs the 1.6.1 client)
- Meet **Slums-chan**, the new guide. She welcomes you on your first login, opens `@settings`, and stands in the Base
  of Roots and in Henesys: talk to her for the guide and your settings.

## Slums Stamps (ticket 111)
- New: **Slums Stamps**, one balance per account shared by all your characters. They can't be traded.
- Earn them from the Daily Check-In (`@daily`): 1 stamp every day you claim, and 6 on days 7, 14, 21 and 28 —
  48 over the 28-day cycle. The claim says "+1 Slums Stamp, day 4/28" in yellow.
- Spend them at Rooty: Donor NX cosmetics cost 6 stamps, the High-Five Stamps 40. Rooty shows your balance.
- `@stamps` shows your balance, your check-in day and the next bonus day. `@points` shows stamps instead of vote
  points.
- Billion Coins and vote points are retired: every Billion Coin you had (bags, storage, your shop, Duey) and every
  vote point became 6 stamps.

## Family (ticket 53)
- New: the **Family** button works. Add a junior (same map, level 11+, within 20 levels), earn reputation when your
  juniors hunt and level, and spend it in the Family window.
- Every perk works now: **Family Reunion** (go to a family member, on any channel: you change channel and land on
  their map) and **Summon Family** (call one to you, from any channel), **My Drop Rate / EXP 1.5x and 2x** (15 or 30
  minutes), **My Party Drop Rate / EXP 2x** (30 minutes, everyone in your party who is online when you use it, on any
  channel) and **Family Bonding** (2x EXP and drops for 30 minutes for you and your juniors' line, with 6 of them
  online). Each once a day. Family buffs don't stack with each other (the best one counts); they do stack with
  coupons. As the perk's own description says, a buff does nothing extra while an EXP (or drop) event is on (a world
  event hot spot on your map, or a staff rate event); its time keeps running. Your buff survives a server restart.
- Regular bots can be your juniors too: they say yes to people they know, have partied or chatted with, and now and
  then one asks to be yours (a whisper). Its offer stands for 30 minutes: open the Family window, Add Junior, type its
  name, even if it has wandered off, and it says yes. Summon Family on a regular junior works like on a player: it
  comes to you, unless it is busy with something it can't drop (a party quest, a party with someone else, a shop it
  runs...), and then you are told so and nothing is spent.

## Regulars rebirth (ticket 30)
- Regulars who reach level 200 can now be reborn the way you are: they earn the key item from boss drops, take it to
  Wyatt the Reborn in the Base of Roots, and start again at level 1 with the rebirth EXP rate, keeping their gear.
- It takes them time: only a few are reborn a day across the whole server, and not every regular bothers. Over the
  coming weeks you will start to meet reborn regulars, and later a few in the rebirth-2 and rebirth-3 areas.

## Achievements and medals (ticket 93) (merged, on TEST, t-ac-1 PASS 2026-10-28; audit fixes on 161-auditfix, t-ac-2 to run)
- New: **32 achievements**, each a one-time goal that gives you a cosmetic **medal** (no stats): your job advances,
  levels 30 / 70 / 120 and your class's top level, your first kill of Mano, Stumpy, Mushmom and Jr. Balrog, clearing
  Papulatus, Zakum and Horntail, the Kerning, Ludibrium, Orbis and Pirate party quests, 10 and 100 fishing catches,
  your wedding, 50 quests, your first guild, days 7 and 28 of the Daily Check-In, touring Victoria Island, Ossyria and
  Aquarium, 10 trades, 5 Free Market purchases and your first rebirth.
- A yellow line tells you when you earn one and the medal goes to your Equip tab; with a full tab it waits until you
  have room (type `@achievements`). Old facts count at your next login (your current job and level, your guild, your
  marriage and your rebirths); past boss clears, PQs, quests, fish and streaks were not recorded and don't count.
  Quests count whether you finish them at the quest window or an NPC finishes them for you.
- `@achievements` lists what you earned and what is under way; `@achievements all` lists every goal.
- Bots earn them too, and what they earn shows on the website's news feed.

## Bots talk to each other (ticket 114) (merged, on TEST; t-bt-2 PASS; t-bt-1 FAIL 2026-10-28 on a07a2fabc: talk tick 25-29 ms against the 20 ms budget at ~2,250 TEST bots, once also only 2 of 3 in the group spoke; the 20 ms limit is the owner's call. 161-auditfix: t-bt-1 now forces a yes and checks the party, t-bt-2 needs a follower to arrive)
- Bots now greet the bots they know when they run into each other, chat a little, and ask each other things: to
  party up, to come along to another town, for help training, to buy something, or "did u hear...". The other bot
  answers the way it actually feels — yes, no, or "how about Ellinia instead?", warmly, curtly or rudely — and often
  acts on it: they party, head to town together, trade.
- Groups of friends standing together chat as a group, taking turns.
- Friends hang out together: when one heads to a town, its friends tend to follow a few seconds later.
- Talk near them to join in; the bot that answers you talks to you like any bot does.

## Adventurer's Bag fixes
- The bag (`@bp`) only takes items it can give back exactly as they were. Untradeable, karma'd, expiring or
  name-tagged stacks, quest and party-quest items stay in your inventory (also with `@backpack on`) instead of coming
  back out as plain, permanent items.
- Items you loot while the "how many?" box is open are no longer lost, a withdraw your inventory can't take leaves
  the items in the bag, and "Withdraw all ores" works for big piles.

## Training after 120 (ticket 81) (merged b4665ff91 2026-10-29 ~02:25 UTC; not yet on TEST; check t-ld-1 plus a manual walk of a lvl 119/120 character through the 4 boundary portals; switch -Dslums.mobStatOverrides=false; migration 050)

- **New areas open at level 120.** The Future and Twilight Perion, plus the lower half of Grandis, no longer need
  rebirths. The lower half of Grandis is Pantheon, Savage Terminal's streets, Sanctuary, the Caravan Refuge, Fox Ridge,
  Cheong-woon Valley, Ristonia and Toolen City, with monsters from level 109 to 142. Reach them with the Henesys
  teleporter, the Gate of Time in the Temple of Time, or the Hyper Teleport Rock. The deeper parts of Grandis
  (Heliseum, the Fox Tree, Cernium and the rougher back streets of Savage Terminal) and Arcane River still need 3
  rebirths.
- **These monsters are worth fighting now.** Monsters in the Future, Grandis and Arcane River used to take forever to
  kill and give almost nothing. They now have health that fits their level. Their EXP is set so that training there
  pays roughly twice what the Temple of Time does, and it climbs as the areas get higher.
- **Real loot.** These monsters now drop mesos, potions, ores, scrolls, 4th-job mastery books and equipment. Some of
  them drop their own materials. Rarely, they drop gear from the new areas: Eclectic and Muspell armour, and once in
  a long while Pensalir armour or a Jaihin, Utgard or AbsoLab weapon. Their Monster Book cards still drop as before.
- **Classic spots pay more too.** Leafre and Minar Forest, the Temple of Time and Ulu City give 40% more EXP.
- **Lion King's Castle and the Rose Garden got a rework.** Their monsters had far too much health and dropped
  nothing. They now fight like level 117-134 monsters should, pay good EXP and drop mesos and loot.
- **Regulars use these areas too.** Bots of level 120 and up can now train in the Future and lower Grandis, the same
  as you.
