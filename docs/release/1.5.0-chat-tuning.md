# 1.5.0 rc3: bot chat tuning

Bots hold off on player-directed social actions during PQ/event instances, GM event maps,
boss fights (including empty expedition arenas), trades, player shops, hired merchants,
NPC dialogs, NPC shops/storage, and after **more than five minutes without player input**.
Keepalives, pet/summon movement, passive damage and automatic healing do not reset AFK time.
Storage tracks its open window separately from merely owning a storage account.

The shared `PlayerBusy.isBusy` gate runs at delivery for buddy requests, party/guild invites,
gifts, trade requests/quotes, guild recruitment/kick whispers, shop price notifications and
chat-brain whispers. Delayed actions recheck the recipient. Suppressed actions are dropped;
there is no catch-up queue. Bot couples court and party with bots only, so have no outgoing
player invitations to gate. QA listeners on the existing `BOT_CHAT` topic see
`HOLD OFF <player>: <reason> (action dropped)`, limited to one line per player per 30 seconds.

All properties below use JVM `-D` flags and are read at runtime.

| Property | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `solomapling.botHoldOffBusy` | `true` | Enable the shared busy/AFK guard. |
| `solomapling.chat.townMultiplier` | `1.5` | Ambient rate multiplier for towns, FM entrance/rooms and MapleRoot hub/rooms. |
| `solomapling.chat.huntingMultiplier` | `0.15` | Ambient rate multiplier for ordinary hunting/travel maps. |
| `solomapling.chat.dungeonMultiplier` | `0.01` | Ambient rate multiplier for mini-dungeons, event instances, GM event maps and boss arenas. |
| `solomapling.chat.ambientMapGapMs` | `3000` | Base minimum spacing between ambient lines per world/channel/map; divided by the map multiplier. Defaults: town 2 s, hunting 20 s, dungeon/boss 5 min. |
| `solomapling.chat.ambientWorldPerMinute` | `60` | Maximum ambient lines in a rolling minute per world, across maps and channels. |
| `solomapling.chat.whisperPlayerGapMs` | `600000` | At most one unsolicited bot whisper per character per ten minutes, across all bots and channels. Replies to the player's own whispers bypass this budget, but still respect busy state. |

Multipliers accept 0 (mute ambient speech), are bounded to 10, and fall back to their defaults
on invalid, negative or nonfinite values. A world cap of 0 mutes ambient speech. All budgets
are in memory; a server restart resets them. Relogging and channel changes retain a character's
unsolicited whisper cooldown. Changing the busy switch does not disable whisper or ambient caps.

The original dialogue packs still supply the lines. Town paired chatter's 10% start roll is
scaled by map type; its existing 30–90 s per-bot cooldown remains. The town bark ticker's
8–20 s base interval is divided by the town multiplier (about 5–13 s by default), and now
includes FM maps. Wanderer chatter uses the multiplier on its original 5% roll. Training bots
keep their 2–4 min attempt interval and use the map multiplier as their speech roll (15% in
hunting maps). Nearby-player reactions stretch their 2–4 min shared recipient cooldown by
the inverse map multiplier. Their lines, paired chatter, town barks, Henesys/wanderer idle
lines and training mutters all share the new ambient map/world budget. Ordinary prompted
conversations keep their existing chat-brain/model budgets and typing pace. The existing
server-wide bot megaphone gate (`solomapling.botMegaphoneGapSeconds`, 120 s) remains in force.

Boss classification reuses `MapId` rebirth/dojo/boss-rush predicates and Papulatus/Pianus
constants, supplements them with arenas from `scripts/event/*Battle*.js`, and detects live
boss monsters. This keeps arenas quiet between boss spawns as well as during fights.
