# 1.5.2 sweep: jobs and skills, MapleRoot vs stock Cosmic

Branch `152-sweep-jobs` (from `origin/release-1.5.1`). Read-only on TEST and live; nothing deployed.

## How

- **Code.** Stock baseline = Cosmic `2d7525f2b` (2023-08-16), the Cosmic commit closest to the MapleRoot import
  `03d13cb21` (picked by smallest whole-tree diff; later Cosmic refactors such as `AttackTarget`, exploded-meso delays
  and the too-many-lines autoban are newer than MapleRoot and are not MapleRoot changes). Read side by side:
  `StatEffect`, `AbstractDealDamageHandler`, `CloseRange`/`Ranged`/`MagicDamageHandler`, `SpecialMoveHandler`,
  `SummonDamageHandler`, `TakeDamageHandler`, `Monster.applyStatus`, `Character` (job advance, level up, AP/SP,
  summons, buffs), `SkillFactory`, `Job`, `constants/skills`.
- **Data.** `Skill.wz` XML on TEST (`/opt/mapleroot-dev/persistence-test/Server/wz/Skill.wz`, two files spot-checked
  identical to live) against `/root/work/cosmic-triage/Cosmic/wz/Skill.wz`, every skill and level, keyed by skill id
  across all job files.
- **Scripts.** Every script that calls `changeJob*` or `teachSkill`, against the Cosmic baseline, plus a check that
  every `teachSkill` id exists in our `Skill.wz`.

## The big picture

MapleRoot did not tweak the v83 skill set, it replaced a large part of it. Our `Skill.wz` has **64 stock skills
removed, 147 new skills and 230 stock skills with changed numbers**:

- Removed: every weapon Booster and Spell Booster, the second weapon type of each Warrior branch (Axe, BW, Pole Arm
  masteries, Final Attacks, charges, Panic/Coma Axe, Crusher/Fury Pole Arm), Big Bang (all three), Slow (F/P, I/L,
  Blaze), Heaven's Hammer, Ninja Ambush, Taunt, Steal, Assassinate, Energy Drain, Transformation, Super
  Transformation, Shockwave, Demolition, Snatch, Barrage, Hypnotize, Maker, Wind Archer's Eagle Eye/Wind
  Piercing/Wind Shot, Night Walker's Vanish, Aran's Polearm Booster.
- New: a 4th job for every Cygnus class (1112/1212/1312/1412/1512), extra 4th-job attacks for every explorer
  branch, a "5th job" (113, 123, ..., 523, 1113..1513, 2113) with four skills each, Super Beginner (700) and
  Synth Master (710) with their own skills, Ninja Bounce/Vertical Bounce for every beginner.
- Changed: almost every buff lasts 3600 s; attack skills hit many more targets and lines for 2-10x the damage;
  Seal (F/P, I/L, Blaze) and Doom are now one-level crit buffs (Sharp Eyes style) instead of mob debuffs;
  summons were 20 s / 60 s cooldown (1.5.1 already puts them back to stock); Hero's Will has a 10 s cooldown.

All of that is MapleRoot's design (the client ships the same data) and is listed below for the owner, not
changed. The code fixes are for the places where stock code or scripts still assume the stock skill set.

## Other MapleRoot code changes to stock skill handling

Deliberate content (keep, listed so nobody "restores" them):

- `StatEffect`: Seal/Doom (2111004, 2211004, 2311005, 12111002) give `SHARP_EYES` instead of a mob debuff (matches the
  data); Mana Reflection also gives `STANCE` 80; Holy Shield also gives `DIVINE_BODY`; new buffs/freezes for
  1321016, 2221011, 5221016, 5221018, 13121008, 7101102-7101105, `DawnWarrior.STANCE` 11121204.
- `Corsair` 5221010 renamed `HEROS_WILL` (it is Hero's Will in the data; Cosmic's `SPEED_INFUSION` name was wrong).
- `RangedAttackHandler`: Aran combo costs 10/30/200 combo (stock 30/100/200) and keep the remainder instead of
  resetting to 0.
- `TakeDamageHandler`: Mana Reflection reflects a flat 20 damage (stock x % of the hit, capped at 1/5 mob HP); Meso
  Guard costs nothing; battleship HP gone (owner kept that 2026-10-21).
- `Monster`: Armor Crash, Magic Crash and Power Crash each remove both weapon and magic immunity (stock: one each).
- `AbstractDealDamageHandler`: Steal does nothing on bosses; White Knight charges no longer make the mob weak to the
  element for y s (WK/Paladin `setTempEffectiveness` block removed); Snipe's fixed 195k-200k damage removed (our
  Snipe is 4000 % x4 in the data).
- `StatEffect`: freeze skills no longer double their WZ time (`ret.duration *= 2` removed): Paralyze, Combo Tempest
  and the rest freeze half as long as stock.
- `Character.levelUp`: beginners gain 60 HP / 60 MP a level (stock 12-16 / 10-12) and never auto-assign AP
  (`level < 1` instead of `< 11`).
- `Character.dispel`: a mob's Dispel no longer removes Magic Guard or Echo of Hero.
- `Character.changeJob`: no `setMasteries` call (stock gives 10 master levels in three 4th-job skills on advance; the
  Leafre NPCs still do it themselves, the Job Master does not; see owner list).
- `SummonDamageHandler`: the summon damage cap is gone (anti-cheat).
- `MakerService`: the Maker skill (1007/10001007/20001007) is not in the data, so the client Maker window only
  accepts recipes that need skill level 0; MapleRoot crafts through NPCs instead (`NpcMakerCraftingService`).
