open), then the player lines at the bottom, which are the only part that ships. Owner decision 2026-11-03 (question 24):

> **Staff status, 2026-11-03 (expedition policy, branch `164-expedpolicy`):** two policy switches, both **on by
> default** and needing no line in any start script - the conf lists them so Server Dev can see them:
> `-Dslums.exped.botcaptain` (a bot may captain an expedition; a real player in the party always outranks the bot
> captain, `preferredLeader` inside `EventManager.startInstance`) and `-Dslums.exped.altfollow` (an owned alt follows
> its owner into any expedition the owner is a member of and takes a membership slot, so it counts toward the size
> gate exactly like a party bot - `Expedition.members` was always the only membership record). **In 1.6.4 a
> bot-captained run has exactly one caller: the GM command `!mechanics boss <TYPE> [n]`** (`BossStaging` →
> `ExpeditionBots.startAsCaptain`); there is no scheduler, no lobby tick and no autonomous trigger, because a bot that
> opens boss rooms by itself is bot farming and needs an economy decision first. Every refusal prints a sentence
> (`STARTED`, `HUMAN_LEADS`, `ROOM_TAKEN`, `NO_SCRIPT`, `TOO_FEW`, `SWITCH_OFF`, `REFUSED`). Checks: `BossStagingTest`
> (6), `ExpeditionBotsCaptainTest`, `ExpeditionBotsTest`.

> **Staff status, 2026-11-03 (mortality, branch `164-botmortal`): bots can die in boss rooms now.** Before 1.6.4 a bot
> inside an event instance could not die at all (`BotDeaths.protectedFromDeath` turned every lethal hit into nothing).
> Now a lethal hit kills the bot; a dead bot is a lost member and its hold goes back; only a Bishop's Resurrection ends
> the dead state (a heal cannot lift 0 HP); with `-Dslums.bossfight.revivepause=true` - which the conf sets - the run
> pauses for the dead bot and resumes when it is registered and alive again, and with the switch off the run ends through
> the ordinary member paths instead of waiting on a corpse. A bot's death also spends the expedition's death-count
> budget when `-Dslums.bossfight.deathcount` is on (default off: a five-bot wipe would spend the default budget of 10 in
> one room). The old immortality is one switch, `-Dslums.bots.immortal=true`, default **false**, TEST only. This is a
> visible change in every boss room. Checks: `BotMortalityTest`, `BotResurrectionTest`, `BotDeathCountTest`,
> `DeadMemberTest`, `ExpeditionDeathCountTest`, `ExpeditionDeathCountRunTest`, `MechanicsRunsInstancesTest`.

## Player notes

### Party quests

- [New] Bots run party quests themselves: Kerning City, Orbis, Ludibrium, Henesys, the six Cafe areas, LudiMaze, Pirate, Treasure, Save Delli, Magatia and its Zenumist band, Ellin Forest, Crimsonwood Keep and the Sharenian guild quest. They talk to the NPCs, collect the coupons and passes, hit the reactors, take the portals and clear the stages on their own.
- [New] Lead a party quest with bots and your own alts following you.

### Bosses

- [New] Bot parties join boss expedition fights instead of standing outside: Zakum, Horntail, Chaos Zakum, the five Twin Dragon caves, Maha, Castellan, Papulatus, Krexel, Showa, Balrog, Scarga, Elemental Thanatos, Pink Bean, Captain Latanica, the Hard bosses Lucid, Verus Hilla and Darknell, the six GuardianNex caves, the eight BossRush waves and the Zakum-mines chest hunt.
- [New] Chaos Zakum is playable at the same altar, as its own expedition, with no daily entry limit for players.
- [New] Bots can die in a boss fight now, like any other member. A Bishop's Resurrection brings one back, and the run waits for a dead bot instead of ending.
- [New] A friendly bot can tell you how a run ended, in one line.
- [Change] Von Leon's castle: only the Audience Room fight needs rebirth 1. The corridor, prisons and roofs are open to everyone.
- [New] Your own alt follows you into an expedition you're in, and counts as one of the party.

### Friends, bots and chat

- [New] Type @bonds to see how your friends, partner, guild, family and alts feel about you, alts that are out included.
- [New] Bots now remember each other: they greet, chat, swap small gifts and make plans to meet, and some get along better than others.
- [Change] Bots pick what to say from what is actually going on around them, so their answers fit where they are and what you asked.
- [Fix] A bot no longer answers a busy player, answers in the wrong chat, or replies with several lines at once. Whispers get whispered back.
- [Fix] A chat line you send no longer disappears when the server hits an error while delivering it.

### Gear and forging

- [New] Normal gear drops from monsters all the way to level 200, and Nimble's guide lists clearer sources for levels 120 to 149.
- [Change] Rebirth forging is simpler: fewer materials per step, the later stages use the tokens hard bosses already give, and Nimble swaps one class weapon core for another for free.
- [Change] Nimble checks your level, rebirth and the exact items you picked before taking anything, and forged gear keeps its HP and MP.
- [Change] When you craft a Star Forced item into new gear, its STR, DEX, INT and LUK now carry over with the same 20% bonus that weapon and magic attack already get.
- [Change] A failed Star Force upgrade now costs a star, and a superior item that breaks is destroyed and tells you so.

### New places and quests

- [New] The Lion King's Castle questline is open: its quest drops, Von Leon's NPCs and Murt.
- [New] The casino chip shop is back, and chips sell back at their face value.

### Exchange

- [New] The exchange trades items now: scrolls, potions and other use items, and etc items, each with its own market price next to the NX and meso markets. Find them under Items on the website's exchange page.
- [New] Item sales on the exchange are capped at 150,000,000 mesos per account per day, enough for a few Chaos Scrolls. The cap resets at midnight UTC.

### Marriage and alts

- [New] You can marry your own alt at the cathedral or chapel: bring it out, talk to the priest and pay one wedding ticket. You both get the medal and the rings.
- [New] Married couples hunting on the same map earn bonus EXP: +10% with a bot spouse, +5% with your own alt.
- [Fix] If your wedding booking expires because the couple never showed up, one ticket goes back to whoever booked it (by Duey if they're offline).

### Fixes

- [Fix] Crack in Time and the Temple of Time no longer trap you: the portals lead where they should.
- [Fix] Buying throwing stars or bullets costs the same as selling them back, so the buy-low, sell-high loop is closed.
- [Fix] Vega's Spell uses the scroll you feed it.
- [Change] Maker NPCs refuse characters below the recipe's level.
- [Fix] A failed storage save no longer wipes your storage.
- [Fix] Attacking with no MP does no damage, and a party Heal you can't afford heals nobody.
- [Fix] Three megaphone items are no longer used up without any effect, and they're off Rooty's shop.
- [Fix] Only a party or alliance leader can use leader actions.
- [Fix] Weddings: chapel booking works, the ticket is spent only once the booking and invitations succeed, the booking text never shows "null", the divorce fee is charged only after the marriage ends, and a missing ring no longer breaks a divorce or blocks a new proposal.
- [Fix] A failed save no longer locks your alt out until the next restart, and when an alt is held back it tells you why.
- [Change] An alt can't be deleted while it's married (divorce first), and an alt turned away at someone else's wedding is told why.
- [Fix] Family reputation pays out, and the family window's buttons work.
- [Fix] Monsters in some newer areas that had no drop list now drop the map's usual items again (NX cards, leaves, scrolls).
- [Fix] Alcaster's Crystal completes its quest anywhere on its map, and scripted item uses no longer leave you frozen.
- [Fix] Monster Book: choosing a cover no longer drops your login, the link medal goes to the character that earned it, and tiers survive a relog.
- [Fix] Omok and memory-game rooms: closed rooms leave the map, boards reset, and ties are handled.
- [Change] Party search remembers 10 re-entries and only matches players near you.
- [Fix] A note that fails to send tells you why.
- [Fix] Quests: nine medal quests that could never finish are hidden, wrong-job quests finish their text, and quests 2233 and 2234 finish properly.
- [Fix] Job advancement NPCs no longer offer jobs that don't exist here, and only rebirthed level-1 characters choose a path.
- [Fix] Cutscenes: the Cannoneer and subway openings, the Evan tutorial pictures and Mr. Lim's menu work again.
- [Fix] The teleport rock refuses destinations that aren't on your list. Reward-box gear lasts hours, not seconds, and the world message names the prize. The glove stimulator gives the glove you paid for.
- [Fix] A silent leader can no longer freeze a Carnival challenge.
- [Fix] Magatia PQ's third stage can be finished again: the beakers you need to break are in the right room now.
- [Fix] Gates, chests and altars react to items again, even when a bot dropped one of the items on them.
- [Fix] A boss whose next form is missing no longer stops everything else on the map from dying properly: the loot, the clear and the next form all happen.

### Client

- [Change] You need the latest client (build 10740 or later). Older clients are told to update through the launcher.
