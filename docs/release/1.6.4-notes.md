> **Staff status, 2026-11-03 (expedition policy, branch `164-expedpolicy`):** two policy switches, both **on by
> default** and needing no line in any start script - the conf lists them so Server Dev can see them:
> `-Dslums.exped.botcaptain` (a bot may captain an expedition; a real player in the party always outranks the bot
> captain, `preferredLeader` inside `EventManager.startInstance`) and `-Dslums.exped.altfollow` (an owned alt follows
> its owner into any expedition the owner is a member of and takes a membership slot, so it counts toward the size
> gate exactly like a party bot - `Expedition.members` was always the only membership record). **In 1.6.4 a
> bot-captained run has exactly one caller: the GM command `!mechanics boss <TYPE> [n]`** (`BossStaging` →
> `ExpeditionBots.startAsCaptain`); there is no scheduler, no lobby tick and no autonomous trigger, because a bot that
> opens boss rooms by itself is bot farming and needs an economy decision first. Every refusal prints a sentence
> (`STARTED`, `HUMAN_LEADS`, `ROOM_TAKEN`, `NO_SCRIPT`, `TOO_FEW`, `SWITCH_OFF`, `REFUSED`). Checks: `BossStagingTest`
> (6), `ExpeditionBotsCaptainTest`, `ExpeditionBotsTest`.

> **Staff status, 2026-11-03 (mortality, branch `164-botmortal`): bots can die in boss rooms now.** Before 1.6.4 a bot
> inside an event instance could not die at all (`BotDeaths.protectedFromDeath` turned every lethal hit into nothing).
> Now a lethal hit kills the bot; a dead bot is a lost member and its hold goes back; only a Bishop's Resurrection ends
> the dead state (a heal cannot lift 0 HP); with `-Dslums.bossfight.revivepause=true` - which the conf sets - the run
> pauses for the dead bot and resumes when it is registered and alive again, and with the switch off the run ends through
> the ordinary member paths instead of waiting on a corpse. A bot's death also spends the expedition's death-count
> budget when `-Dslums.bossfight.deathcount` is on (default off: a five-bot wipe would spend the default budget of 10 in
> one room). The old immortality is one switch, `-Dslums.bots.immortal=true`, default **false**, TEST only. This is a
> visible change in every boss room. Checks: `BotMortalityTest`, `BotResurrectionTest`, `BotDeathCountTest`,
> `DeadMemberTest`, `ExpeditionDeathCountTest`, `ExpeditionDeathCountRunTest`, `MechanicsRunsInstancesTest`.

> **Staff status, 2026-11-03 (Amoria, branch `164-amoria`): TEST only, and no row.** A bot counts as an Amoria party
> member behind `-Dslums.mechanics.amoriapq.botcount`, **default off** and listed under "stays off" in

> row was written, deliberately** - stages 2-4 are hand-work by the script's own design, and whether a mixed party is
> wanted is an owner decision, not a lane's. Check: `AmoriaBotCountTest`.

## Player notes (draft)

### Party quests

- [New] Bots run party quests themselves: Kerning City, Orbis, Ludibrium, Henesys, the six Cafe areas, LudiMaze, Pirate, Treasure, Save Delli, Magatia and its Zenumist band, Ellin Forest, Crimsonwood Keep and the Sharenian guild quest. They answer the NPCs, collect the coupons and passes, hit the reactors, take the portals and clear the stages on their own timers.
- [New] A party quest can be started by the bots themselves from a lobby, or led by you with bots and your own alts following.

### Bosses

- [New] Bot parties share boss expedition fights instead of standing outside: Zakum, Horntail, Chaos Zakum, the five Twin Dragon caves, Maha, Castellan, Papulatus, Krexel, Showa, Balrog, Scarga, Elemental Thanatos, Pink Bean, Captain Latanica, Magnus, the four Hard bosses (Will, Lucid, Verus Hilla, Darknell), the six GuardianNex caves, the eight BossRush waves and the Zakum-mines chest hunt.
- [New] Chaos Zakum is playable at the same altar, as its own expedition, with the arms and the bodies in the order the client expects.
- [New] Bots can die in a boss fight now, like any other member: a Bishop's Resurrection brings one back, and a party that loses its bots has to cope. When revive-pause is on, the run holds for the dead bot instead of ending.
- [New] A friendly bot can tell you how a run ended, in one line.
- [New] Your own alt follows you into an expedition you are already in, and it counts as one of the party.

> Every bot party quest and boss row is switch-gated and off by default. Server owners turn on what they want with `-Dslums.mechanics` (party quests), `-Dslums.mechanics.boss` (bosses) and, per row, `-Dslums.mechanics.<row>`; `docs/release/1.6.4.conf` is the list live gets.

### Fixes

- [Fix] Magatia PQ's third stage asked you to break three beakers that stand in the room next door, so the stage could never finish. It is the right room now, and the route in takes you there.
- [Fix] Items dropped by bots on the ground could stop a gate, chest or altar reacting to the other items lying on it. A drop's owner was looked up through a client the server had cached, and a bot's drop carried one that was already gone; item reactors now get the drop's own owner, so the gate, the chest and the altar react again.
- [Fix] A boss part that revives into a monster the data does not have no longer breaks the death handling: the missing form is skipped, named once in the server log, and the rest of the death - the loot, the clear, the next form - runs. Before, that one id could stall every death on the map.
