## Track D: economy stats and balancing (owner, 2026-10-25)
The owner asked for this ("we gotta look into economy stats and figure out balancing"), so the old "no economy work
unless asked" rule is lifted for this track. One data layer, three faces:
1. **Economy stats (the data):** daily snapshots of meso supply (players vs bots, per level band), sources (mob
   drops, NPC sales, quests, JQ chests, Donation Box) and sinks (shops, Job Master fees, upkeep, Gold Compass, repairs,
   taxes), item prices (FM listings, bot/player trades, NPC buy/sell), wealth distribution (top 1%/10%, Gini),
   inflation over time, the Maple Leaf and NX flows. Built on what bots already log ([Wealth], [Scroll], [Shop],
   trades) plus a small nightly job; no player-identifying data on public pages.
2. **Admin dashboard (35, live behind auth):** the full private view: graphs of all of the above, per-bot/per-player
   drill-down, alerts (a sudden meso faucet, a price crash, an item duplicated).
3. **Basil Market (79):** the public market page: item prices with history (candles like nutnnut's /market), live FM
   listings, recent trades, item icons, old-school look.
4. **Hidden Street (78):** the public game guide in the old style: items, mobs, maps, quests, NPCs (from the NPC
   catalogue), drop tables, plus a few public economy numbers.
5. **Balancing:** after two weeks of data, a balancing report: where mesos come from vs go, which faucets are too
   big (e.g. Smoker's 183m at lv 72), which sinks are weak, price outliers. The owner decides each change; we adjust
   rates/fees/drops in small steps and watch the stats move.
