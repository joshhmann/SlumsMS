# SlumsMS 1.6.4 "Bots play the mechanics"

The release that puts the bot mechanics engine in front of players: bot parties clear party quests, share boss
expedition fights, and the two long-standing crashes they uncovered are fixed. Owner decision 2026-11-03 (question 24):
**1.6.4 ships with what is done here**; the rest stays in `docs/roadmap/1.7.0.md`. Branch `164-all-merge` in
`/root/work/164-all`, built from `release-1.6.4`, twelve lanes merged into one tree.

Shape follows `docs/roadmap/1.6.1.md`: one section per family, each with status, switch, check id and what is still
outstanding. The release settings are `docs/release/1.6.4.conf` (draft) and the player-facing lines are
`docs/release/1.6.4-notes.md`.

## Update 2026-11-03 (bots play the mechanics, branch 164-all-merge, ticket 130)

**Done: step 1 (perfect runs).** 57 rows, 29 boss and 28 party quest, 201 stages, one `*.yaml` per row and all 57 listed
in `content/index.txt`. A row names its event script, gates, maps and per-stage steps; solvers drive the run, and
`MechanicsRuns`/`Orchestrator` own the lifecycle. Switch: `-Dslums.mechanics` (on live since 1.6.1) and
`-Dslums.mechanics.boss` for boss rows, plus one flag per row that names one (20 switches over 32 rows; 25 rows need
none beyond the two). Checks: `t-mx-1`..`t-mx-6` (Kerning PQ end to end, a stopped run, two channels, a player leading
alts, a player party taking the channel), `t-ex-1`/`t-ex-2` (Zakum expedition from the party side and from a GM), `t-in-3`
(lobby invite), and the row nets `MechanicsRowWzIntegrityTest` / `ContentRowWzIntegrityTest` (row ids, maps, mobs,
reactors and door items against the dump). Outstanding: **step 2 (imperfection by persona — mistakes, deaths, ragequits,
wrong answers) is not started**, nobody has played a row from a real client on this branch, the row lobbies
(`-Dslums.mechanics.<row>.lobby`) stay off, and 21 files the branch changes are named by no auto-QA check
(`REPORT-merged.md`, "What auto-QA covers").

## Update 2026-11-03 (the row net that should have existed first)

**Done.** `MechanicsRowWzIntegrityTest.everyStageThatNamesAReactorHasItOnItsOwnGround` reads `Map.wz` and asserts every
reactor a stage names — `hitReactors`, the clear's `reactorId`, an objective's numeric `reactor:` — stands on that
stage's `map:` or one of its `maps:`, with a negative control. It found one real defect already shipped: Magatia PQ stage
3 named `926110200` while the three `2618000` beakers and the `2618006` door stand on `926110100`, so the stage could
never clear. Fixed (map and route). Switch: none (a test). Check: itself, run with `-Dwz-path`; `0` mismatches over 86
reactor references. Outstanding: it needs a WZ dump, so it SKIPs in a checkout without one.

## Update 2026-11-03 (PQ difficulties: the mechanism, not the numbers)

**Mechanism done, and it ships off.** `kpq`, `hpq`, `lpq` and `opq` each carry one HARD tier; `TierScaling` applies the
row's per-mob multiplier, level bonus and time bonus through the run's `Tier`, and a tier a row does not name reads
NORMAL. Switch: `-Dslums.mechanics.tiers` (read a row's `tiers:` at all) and `-Dslums.mechanics.tier` (which tier a run
asks for). **Both off in `1.6.4.conf`.** Check: none of its own — the mechanics ids cover the files, and no auto-QA check
has read a scaled room. Outstanding (moved to 1.7.0): the numbers themselves (`DIFFICULTY-CHAOS-PLAN.md` section 9 is a
proposal), the entry NPC's tier menu (nothing picks a tier in game), rewards/EXP per tier (no hidden bonus EXP), bots'
behaviour per tier, the site leaderboard, and Expert/Master, which exist in the enum and in no row.

## Update 2026-11-03 (Chaos Zakum in, Chaos Horntail stays behind)

**Chaos Zakum: done.** Row `zakum-chaos` plays `ChaosZakumBattle.js` on the same altar (`280030001`), arms
`8800103..8800110` then bodies `8800100/101/102` as a revive chain, the body shielded while its arms stand, and the clear
recorded through `BossLogEntry.CHAOS_ZAKUM` (before, a chaos clear was dropped silently). Switch:
`-Dslums.chaos.zakum` on top of `-Dslums.mechanics.boss`; **on in `1.6.4.conf`**. Check: `t-ex-2`. Outstanding: no
client has fought it from this branch, and how many chaos clears a character or a day may take is still the owner's call
(it is not enforced anywhere today).

**Chaos Horntail: not in 1.6.4, and not for scheduling reasons.** No script, no row, no rewards; its body id `8810121`
has no `Mob.wz` entry (that absent id is what `Monster.reviveTarget`'s guard exists for, `REPORT-bossrows.md:1225`), its
parts die into ordinary corpses so `MapleMap.isHorntailDefeated()` (`src/main/java/server/maps/MapleMap.java:4905`,
which needs every id in `MobId.DEAD_HORNTAIL_MIN..MAX` = 8810010-8810017 on the map) can never fire, and
`ExpeditionType.CHAOS_HORNTAIL` is used by nothing but the min-size test. It moves to `docs/roadmap/1.7.0.md` with that
reason attached.

## Update 2026-11-03 (run chatter, the real-run quota, the seams, Guild Quest and the Zenumist band)

**Run chatter: done.** A run that ends leaves its win or loss line on the leader and one other bot. Switch
`-Dslums.mechanics.chatter`, **code default on** (chat only); no switch in the conf because the default is the intended
behaviour. Check: covered by the mechanics ids; `RunChatterTest`/`RunChatterRunsTest` unit tests. Outstanding: it has never
posted on a live server.

**Real-run quota and simulated runs: built, off.** `-Dslums.mechanics.realruns` caps concurrent bot-only runs;
`.simruns` (8) / `.simfloor` (5) let simulated runs stand in only over five real samples of that content and tier. With
`.realruns` unset no run is refused, so simulation never happens. **Unset in `1.6.4.conf`**: not seen under load.
Check: none (unit tests only).

**Engine seams: done, no switch.** Reactor by object id, the stage clock, `branches:`, the bound-client fix,
`EquipItem`/`UnequipItem`, queue staging. Check: `t-in-3` and the mechanics ids; the seams' own files are mostly in the
21 files no check names.

**Guild Quest: a row.** `guildquest`, seven stages (waiting room, outskirts and Protector Rock, the statues' pattern, the
four spears, the vassals' offerings, the four-room maze, the boss), `onTimeLimit: advance` and `branches:`.
Switch `-Dslums.mechanics.guildquest`, **on in the conf**. Check: the mechanics ids plus the row nets. Outstanding: no
bot has walked it, and no client has seen a stage-1 clock expiry change the phase.

**Zenumist band: a row.** `magatiapqz`, six stages, switch `-Dslums.mechanics.magatiapqz`, **on in the conf**. The
earlier "Zenumist is unwinnable for humans too" verdict was **wrong**: the counter `2618000` stands in both bands' rooms
and every property the Zenumist band reads has a writer; `2619000` is Carson's quest reactor on `926100401`, not the
PQ's. So the blocked list lost that item and gained a bug fix instead.
