FAQ and known issues

Questions

Who are all these people?

Bera's regulars. They've been here a while; they train, trade, gossip and come and go like anyone. Say hi — see The town's regulars.

Is this GMS v83?

The client is v83, the content is MapleRoot: custom jobs and skills, rebirths, Arcane River, Grandis, Lion King's Castle, Twilight Perion and more. See Jobs and skills.

Where do I job advance?

The Job Master in Base of Roots (FM button). No quests.

Where's the cash shop?

The SHOP button opens it in a window over the game.

Does it rain?

Yes. Outdoor maps have day and night and weather (looks only, no effect on combat).

My NPC chat is stuck.

Type @dispose.

The game closes at start with -2147467259.

Your main display is a virtual one (Parsec etc.). See Getting started.

Can I play with friends who aren't on the list?

Ask the owner; the server is friends-only.

Rules

The rules are yours to write. MapleRoot's old book said: "do whatever you want as long as you don't ruin other people's experience or break the server", bans for racism, discrimination and toxicity, no donations, no voting sites, no planned wipes. Say which of these hold for SlumsMS (multi-client? macros? selling for real money?).

Known issues

  • A virtual or streaming main display (Parsec, Sunshine, Steam Link...) crashes the client at start. Fix: make your real monitor the main display.
  • No windowed-mode wrapper yet (dgVoodoo is planned, ticket 39).
  • The launcher can't show whether the server is up.
  • @quiet settings reset when the server restarts.
  • The guide book in your ETC tab still has MapleRoot's old text until the next client update.
  • Scripted world events (ticket 21) and companion alts (@bot, tickets 13/14) are not built yet.

Sources: docs/live-handoff-20261019.md (SHOP, weather), docs/real-bots/spec.md, tickets 21, 27, 39, 40, 46, 47; MapleRoot's guide book (rules).
