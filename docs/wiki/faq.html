FAQ and known issues

Questions

Who are all these people?

Bera's regulars. They've been here a while; they train, trade, gossip and come and go like anyone. Say hi — see The town's regulars.

Is this GMS v83?

The client is v83; the content is SlumsMS's own: custom jobs and skills, rebirths, Arcane River, Grandis, Lion King's Castle, Twilight Perion and more. See Jobs and skills.

Where do I job advance?

The Job Master in Base of Roots (or Ellinia) advances you without the job test, for a fee: 1st job free, 2nd 4m, 3rd 75m, 4th 500m. Your class instructor's test is still free.

Where's the cash shop?

The SHOP button opens it in a window over the game.

Does it rain?

Yes. Outdoor maps have day and night and weather (looks only, no effect on combat).

My NPC chat is stuck.

Type @dispose.

The game closes at start with -2147467259.

Your main display is a virtual one (Parsec etc.). See Getting started.

Can a friend join?

Ask an admin for an invite code; they sign up on the website or launcher.

Can I have more than one account?

Yes.

Forgot your login?

Use Forgot? on the sign-in page; an admin approves it and you set a new one.

My antivirus flags the launcher or the game.

Some antivirus programs guess that modified game files are dangerous (names like Wacatac.B!ml or Phonzy.A!ml). See Getting started for what to do.

What's Slums Market / Slums Street?

Two parts of the website, both for signed-in players. Slums Market has prices, the Free Market shops and the Buy/Sell board. Slums Street is the database of items, monsters, maps and NPCs.

Rules

The rules are yours to write. The old guide book said: "do whatever you want as long as you don't ruin other people's experience or break the server", bans for racism, discrimination and toxicity, no donations, no voting sites, no planned wipes. Say which of these hold for SlumsMS (multi-client? macros? selling for real money?).

Known issues

  • A virtual or streaming main display (Parsec, Sunshine, Steam Link...) crashes the client at start. Fix: make your real monitor the main display.
  • No windowed-mode wrapper yet (dgVoodoo is planned, ticket 39).
  • The launcher can't show whether the server is up.
  • The guide book in your ETC tab still has the old text until the next client update.
