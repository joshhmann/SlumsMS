Where to train

Where people train, by level and job. This is the same list the town's regulars use to pick their maps, so you will usually find company there. Maps are in rough order of popularity in each group (bigger weight first). Monster levels are the range on the map.

Cygnus Knights follow the matching explorer route after the Ereve starter band (Dawn Warrior → warrior, Blaze Wizard → Fire/Poison, Wind Archer → bowman, Night Walker → thief, Thunder Breaker → brawler). A job with no list of its own in a band uses the "Any job" list.

Some late maps need rebirths before you can enter (see Rebirths); they are marked R1/R2/R3.
