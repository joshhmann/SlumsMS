Changelog

The launcher's "Latest updates" panel shows each release's news. This page lists the releases; paste the news text in when publishing.

VersionDate (UTC)What
1.6.52026-11-07Zakum's party quest runs to the end and your party walks the mines with you, your alts and the regulars fight Horntail (and, from the hotfix on 8 Nov 07:21 UTC, in his two head rooms too, with Horntail only counting as cleared once every part is down), shop buyback and hired merchants can't lose or duplicate items or mesos, the Incubator hatches Pigmy Eggs again, regulars who warp in with your party always show up, a Duey package can be claimed on the second it arrives, party leadership and guild expel work in more cases, and a banned account is signed out of the website within a minute.
1.6.42026-11-05The regulars run party quests and boss expeditions together (and can die in a boss room, so a Bishop's Resurrection matters), every scroll, use item and etc item trades on the Slums Exchange (sales capped at 150,000,000 mesos a day), you can marry your own alt, @bonds lists your friends, partner, guild, family and alts, Slums Street moved to /street/, and fixes including Crack in Time, the Temple of Time portals, the Lion King's Castle questline and the Evan tutorial pictures.
1.6.32026-11-02The NX desk: NX trades instantly at SlumsBank's own buy and sell prices around a slowly drifting rate, instead of an order book, in game with @nx and on the website; every open NX order was cancelled and refunded when it switched over, and item tickers keep their order books. Your alts take lanes orders, a spot each. Moren's Maker test takes an Intermediate Monster Crystal made either path. Maple TV prizes always pay. Fixes for the late-October freezes, slow logins and Free Market regulars quoting prices for things that have none; the regulars log back in gently after a restart (about 25 minutes to fill the towns again).
1.6.22026-10-31Past level 120: the Future, Twilight Perion and lower Grandis at level 120 or your 1st rebirth, the rest of Grandis after the 1st, the Arcane River after the 2nd, and the Lion King's Castle open to everyone. Wyatt the Reborn makes the Essence Stones. Achievements and medals (32 goals), Family perks that work, Slums Stamps from the Daily Check-In, the Slums Exchange for NX and mesos, your alts as companions with @botme and @alts, the Maker skill back, regulars who do quests and answer for what they said, Slums-chan, the Lazy Penguins, starting on Maple Island, @invite and @bug, and a new client: Edit UI, the HUD with EXP rate and DPS, short damage numbers, hiding other players' effects, crash reports.
1.5.0(not released yet)The social update: the regulars remember you (friends and rivals), talk in plain words, form ten guilds and go to war, and date you if you opt in (@romance on). Gachapon in 12 towns, the regulars' FM shops and haggling, @price, Hanako, partner rings. Five channels, @goto, bounces by class, @settings and the @guide tour.
1.3.12026-10-20The world is now Bera (8 channels, Bera-1 to Bera-8). The launcher updates itself.
1.3.02026-10-20The regulars arrive: Bera's towns fill up with people who train, travel, keep shops and chat. @quiet to hide their noise.
1.2.12026-10-19Server 1.2.1, client 1.2.2, launcher 1.2.1.
