Base of Roots and the FM

Base of Roots — the main hub

The TRADE button by the chat bar (the "FM button") takes you to Base of Roots, an Arcane-style hub. Nearly every custom NPC is there, so it is the place to go back to between trips.

NPCWhat for
Job MasterEvery job advancement: 1st at 10 (Magician 8), 2nd at 30, 3rd at 70, 4th at 120, and the 5th tier. It skips the job test for a fee: 1st job free, 2nd 4m, 3rd 75m, 4th 500m (your class instructor's test is still free). At 4th job it also teaches the Leafre master levels. See Jobs.
Wyatt the RebornRebirths at level 200. See Rebirths.
Key BinderPuts skills from your past lives on your keys, and sets skill macros. Also gives you 100 SP once per rebirth.
4th job skills (Gold Compass)Teaches the 4th-job skills that normally need quests or skill books, for 50,000,000 mesos (500,000,000 for a Beginner). Your own job line and your past lives' lines only.
Cotton WarperWarps to boss maps; with 3 rebirths also to Grandis towns and Arcane River & Future towns.
Nimble (Master Forger)Forges equipment and refines raw minerals from your materials. Also at the FM entrance.
RootyOne shop with Nx Goodies, Donor items, Maple weapons and mastery books; also Retro JQ rewards and server information.
Cotton StylistStylist and salon: hair, face, skin.
GerrardStorage.
Lab TechShows your expedition (boss) attempts.
Glowing TreasureStar Force: enhance your equipment (up to 50m mesos a try).

The classic Free Market

The old v83 Free Market (the entrance and its 22 rooms) is kept as it was. Get there by:

  • the portal in Base of Roots, or
  • the Free Market portal in any town (27 towns have one).

Leaving the FM takes you back to where you came in (Base of Roots if it can't tell). The TRADE button still goes to Base of Roots, so your hub bookmark is never lost.

At the entrance:

  • Fredrick collects what your hired merchant didn't sell, and its mesos.
  • Scrooge: storage.
  • Hanako sells gachapon tickets and cosmetics for mesos (see Gachapon, shops and trading).
  • Nimble, the Master Forger, as in Base of Roots.
  • The Buy/Sell board, with the posts from the website's board.
  • The Maple TV: a scroll generator, 5 prizes a day.

The rooms have player shops and the merchants among the regulars. Their price is the price on the shop; buying works the same as from anyone else.

Crafting NPCs in town

  • Coco: Chaos Scroll 60%, at most 5 per talk.
  • Agent E: accessories. The random roll gives belts only; stat rings cost 1-2m mesos plus materials.
  • Dalair: merges items. You need level 160 and Maker level 3, and one item takes at most 3 merges.
  • Spiegelmann: ore refining.

Other handy places

  • @shop opens the all-in-one shop from anywhere.
  • @bp opens your backpack; @backpack turns the backpack system on or off.
