Base of Roots and the FM

Base of Roots — the main hub

The FM button on your screen takes you to Base of Roots, MapleRoot's Arcane-style hub. Nearly every custom NPC is there, so it is the place to go back to between trips.

NPCWhat for
Job MasterEvery job advancement, no quests: 1st at 10 (Magician 8), 2nd at 30, 3rd at 70, 4th at 120, and the 5th tier. See Jobs.
Wyatt the RebornRebirths at level 200. See Rebirths.
Key BinderPuts skills from your past lives on your keys, and sets skill macros.
4th job skillsTeaches all the 4th-job skills that normally need quests or skill books, for 10,000,000 mesos (100,000,000 for a Beginner).
Cotton WarperWarps to boss maps; with 3 rebirths also to Grandis towns and Arcane River & Future towns.
Nimble (Master Forger)Forges equipment and refines raw minerals from your materials.
RootyRooty's shop, the donor shop, Retro JQ rewards and server information.
Cotton StylistStylist and salon: hair, face, skin.
GerrardStorage.
Lab TechShows your expedition (boss) attempts.

Rooty's own shop sold "NX goodies" in MapleRoot; SlumsMS also has the windowed cash shop (SHOP button). Is the donor shop meant to be open on SlumsMS? There is also an unnamed NPC (2616, star force) in the hub; its script says "Disabled for now" for enhancing.

The classic Free Market

The old v83 Free Market (the entrance and its 22 rooms) is kept as it was. Get there by:

  • the portal in Base of Roots, or
  • the Free Market portal in any town (27 towns have one).

Leaving the FM takes you back to where you came in (Base of Roots if it can't tell). The FM button still goes to Base of Roots, so your hub bookmark is never lost.

At the entrance: Fredrick (collects what your hired merchant didn't sell, and its mesos), Scrooge (storage) and the Maple TV.

The rooms have player shops and the merchants among the regulars. Their price is the price on the shop; buying works the same as from anyone else.

Other handy places

  • @shop opens the all-in-one shop from anywhere.
  • @bp opens your backpack; @backpack turns the backpack system on or off.
