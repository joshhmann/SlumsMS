Making mesos

What the rates mean for your wallet

  • Monster mesos are the big one. Mob and quest mesos are x10, but NPC prices are normal. So potions, stars and arrows cost very little next to what you earn. Running out of potions stops being a worry after 1st job.
  • Selling junk to NPCs is a small share of your income here (on a 1x server it's a third or more).
  • Prices in the FM sit higher than you remember from GMS. Items compare to each other much as they did in v83 (a White Scroll is still worth far more than a 60%).

Good to know by job

  • Hermit / Night Lord: Meso Up raises the drop before the x10, so it keeps its value.
  • Chief Bandit: Pickpocket mesos don't get the x10, so it is weaker here than in v83.
  • Night Lord: a full recharge of 800 Ilbis costs about 640 mesos. Buy Ilbis once; they're not an ongoing cost.

Shops and trades

  • Tax on trades, personal stores and hired merchants is taken from the seller: none under 100,000; 0.8% from 100k; 1.8% from 1m; 3% from 5m; 4% from 10m; 5% from 25m; 6% from 100m. Since 1.5.0, Free Market shop sales (personal stores and hired merchants) pay at least 3%.
  • Fredrick at the FM entrance keeps your hired merchant's unsold items and mesos. After 100 days they are gone.
  • Owl of Minerva search works.
  • A Maple Leaf sells to any shop for 500,000.
  • The regulars' shops in the FM sell at posted prices. Since 1.5.0 you can also trade and haggle with them in person, and check prices with @price: see Gachapon, shops and trading.

The Slums Exchange: NX for mesos

Since 1.6.3, NX is bought and sold at SlumsBank's NX desk: the bank quotes one price to buy NX from you and one to sell you NX, in game with @nx and on the website under Slums Market → Exchange. SlumsBank sets buy and sell prices around a rate that drifts slowly with what players and bots buy and sell (capped per day).

Trading at the quote

  • Prices are mesos per 1 NX, and the desk posts both. @nx on its own shows the rate and the two prices; @nx buy 1000 or @nx sell 1000 asks what 1,000 NX would cost. There is no price to type: @nx confirm does the trade.
  • Trades are instant at the quote, in whole NX from 100 up. Nothing waits in a book, and there is no queue to join.
  • The gap between the two prices is what the desk charges: 2% each side of the rate, and no fee on top. A fill is a normal trade: buying NX takes the mesos from the character you name, selling puts them there, and NX is your account's.
  • When 1.6.3 switched NX to the desk, every open NX order was cancelled and refunded: what it held went back to its owner. There are no more NX orders and no NX order book.

Where the rate goes

  • The rate moves at most 0.5% an hour and 20% a day, and always stays between 2,500 and 10,000 mesos per NX. Buying NX from the desk pushes it up and selling to it pushes it down; the day's limit is counted from the rate at the start of the day (UTC). The desk's rate is the official NX rate.
  • A quote is held for 60 seconds. If the rate moved in between you don't get the old price: you see the new one, and @nx confirm again.

What the desk holds, and when it closes

  • The desk only sells NX it owns and only buys with the mesos it has. "SlumsBank is buying NX but has none to sell yet" means exactly that: sell it some NX and buying opens up. "The desk has no mesos to buy with right now" is the other way round, so buying still works and selling waits.
  • The desk can be closed, and its page and @nx then say so and take no trades. Items trade as usual.

On the website

  • You need a sign-in from the last 12 hours, and after a server restart it asks you to sign in again.
  • Every sale asks for your password for that trade, and so does a buy of more than 10,000,000 mesos' worth in 12 hours.

Other things on the exchange

Maple Leaves, Power Elixirs (shown as PELIXIR; up to 10,000 an order), Chaos Scroll 60%s and White Scrolls (up to 1,000) keep their order books: post a buy or a sell at your own price and the game matches them, at any price from 1 meso each to 1,000,000,000 each. Each side pays a 1% fee on the mesos when an item order trades. The 2,500 to 10,000 window is only for NX, and item orders last 7 days.

Since 1.6.4 every scroll, use item and etc item has a ticker too. On the website's Slums Exchange page the Items panel lists them with a search box, categories and sorting, and a Buy and a Sell button on every row. They work like the other item tickers. Selling on the website takes the items out of that character's bags, so log out of the game first, or sell while you're playing that character. You can sell up to 150,000,000 mesos' worth of items per account per day.

The regulars use it too

On the item tickers, about a third of them buy and sell what they find while hunting, and a few keep small standing orders 3%, 6% and 9% away from the going price so there is always something to trade against. Some regulars trade NX at the desk too, within hourly limits, so their buying and selling moves the rate like yours.

The in-game @nx on its own has the desk's prices, but @nx tickers and @nx book still list NX as an order book with no prices until the next update. The PELIXIR ticker is called elixir in game: @nx elixir sell 10 500k.

Slums Stamps

  • Stamps are earned with the Daily Check-In (@daily): 1 a day, and 6 on days 7, 14, 21 and 28, so 48 a cycle of 28 days. You need to be level 10, and you keep your streak by claiming within 48 hours.
  • They belong to your account, not to one character, and cannot be traded. @stamps shows your balance and your next bonus day; @points shows the same balance.
  • Rooty in Base of Roots sells Donor NX cosmetics for 6 stamps each, and the Donor Shop's Rose Vac and Hyper Teleport Rock for 40 stamps each.
  • Vote points and Billion Coins were turned into stamps when they became stamps: 6 stamps for each one you held.

Things that cost a lot

  • Job Master skips: 2nd job 4m, 3rd 75m, 4th 500m (see Jobs).
  • The 4th-job skill package: 50m.
  • Silver Mane: 50m plus materials.
  • A 10k NX code from a regular: 50m (see Gachapon, shops and trading).
  • Star Force at the Glowing Treasure: up to 50m a try.
