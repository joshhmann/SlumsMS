Travel routes

Ways across the world that are not a walk through a portal. Fares and timings are the ones the server's own NPC and event scripts use; the regulars ride the same boats on the same schedule.

"Trip" is the longest it takes from boarding to arrival, waiting room included, at the normal travel rate. Scheduled rides let you board for most of each cycle (every 5 minutes; the subway every minute).

Victoria Island regular cabs

Every Victoria town (and Nautilus) has a cab to the others. Fare: 1,000 mesos to most towns, 800 mesos to Ellinia and Nautilus; Beginners pay a tenth.

World Tour

Spinel stands in the main towns of Victoria, Orbis, Ludibrium, Leafre, Mu Lung and Ariant. She takes you to Mushroom Shrine (Japan) for 3,000 mesos and brings you back, free, to the town you left from.

Orbis Station platforms

In Orbis Station, Isa the Station Guide sends you to the right platform (Ellinia boat, Ludibrium train, Leafre flight, Mu Lung crane, Ariant genie, Ereve ferry) for free.

The Cotton Warper in Base of Roots also warps you to boss maps (and, with 3 rebirths, Grandis and Arcane River towns). Whether it has other menus on live is not checked here.
