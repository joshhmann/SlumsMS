Commands

Type these in normal chat. @help (or @commands) lists them in game, and @guide (or @tutorial) opens the beginner's tour.

Getting started

CommandDoes
@guide / @tutorialThe beginner's tour in a window: the buttons, where things are, the regulars, commands, trading. It opens by itself once, at each character's first login.
@goto <town>Hop to a town; @goto lists them. Not from events, party quests or maps you can't leave (more).
@price <item>What an item sold for over the last 7 days: middle price, range, number of sales.
@gacha <town>A gachapon machine's prizes and odds.
@guildwarYour guild's war score; @guildwar peace (master or jr. master) ends a war with a regular guild.
@romanceWho you're with. Also @romance on / off, wedding, breakup <name>, divorce.
@daily / @checkinThe Daily Check-In window.

Chat and the regulars

CommandDoes
@settingsA window with your settings for the regulars (bot drama, romance, their megaphones and chatter), each with its state: click one to switch it.
@botdrama off / onOff: no regular ever holds a grudge against you (no rivals, trash talk or KS wars).
@romance on / offOn: a regular who really likes you may flirt, ask you out and propose (off by default).
@quiet megaphonesHide the regulars' megaphones (@quiet megaphones off shows them again).
@inviteMake a one-use invite code for a friend (a level 30+ character, 2 a week). @invite list shows your codes.
@quiet botsHide the regulars' chatter, and stop whispers, invites and buddy requests you didn't ask for (people you're talking to or partied with still show; @quiet bots off undoes it).
@quiet all / @quiet offHide both / show everything again.
@botme [name]List your other characters, or bring one along as a party member (up to 5 companions).
@dismiss <name>|allSend a companion home, or all of them.
@worldchatWorld chat on or off.
@roll [n]Roll 0 to n (default 100).

Real players' chat and megaphones and GM notices are never hidden. Your @quiet choice is kept.

Help and fixes

CommandDoes
@gm <message>Message the game masters.
@bug <text>Report a bug (@reportbug works too). You get a number back, like #12.
@idea <text>Suggest a feature or change. Same: you get a number back.
@disposeUnstick an NPC chat.
@enableauthReset the PIC cooldown.
@changelLanguage setting.
