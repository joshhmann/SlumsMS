Jobs and skills

Advancing

The Job Master in Base of Roots (or Ellinia) advances you without the job test, for a fee: 1st job free, 2nd 4m, 3rd 75m, 4th 500m. Your class instructor's test is still free.

What SlumsMS changed

  • New attacks: custom 4th-job skills for every explorer 4th job (the Bishop gets the passive Buff Master), for example Rising Rage (Hero) and La Mancha Spear (Dark Knight).
  • Removed: all boosters, Steal, Big Bang, Heaven's Hammer, Slow, Taunt, Ninja Ambush, Assassinate, Barrage, Snatch, Demolition, Transformation and a few others; the warriors' axe, blunt and pole-arm lines. Warriors are sword or spear only.
  • Mages: almost every weapon has as much magic attack as attack.
  • Same skill slot, new skill: Brandish → Raging Blow, Seal/Doom → Fire Rush, and others.

The Maker skill

1.6.2 brought back Maker, the crafting skill. Moren in Magatia teaches it: from level 45, three lessons at 10,000 mesos each, and the skill itself has three levels.

MakerLevelWhat it takes
145Finish Moren's first quest and its three lessons (10,000 mesos each). You also get a copy of "A Guide to Using the Maker Skill".
275Start Moren's second round, then make an Intermediate Monster Crystal 1 with Maker. Either Maker path counts: a crystal made from a monster's leftovers, or one made by taking an item apart. A crystal you bought or made before the quest started doesn't.
3105Carry a note for Moren and his friend, then make a Gold Anvil with Maker.

Use the skill from your Beginner tab to open the Maker window. It shows what each recipe costs in mesos before you press anything, and the fee comes out first, then the materials. The window also takes things apart, and turns a hundred leftovers from a monster's drops (the Etc tab ones) into one monster crystal. Bonus materials and stimulants work on the recipes that take them; a stimulant works nine times in ten.

Maker is also the key to Dalair's equipment merge (he stands in many towns): he merges equipment you don't need into the gear you are wearing, for extra stats. It needs level 160 (110 for Cygnus Knights) and Maker level 3, costs 50,000 mesos, and one piece of gear takes at most three merges.

The 4th-job skill package

The 4th job skills NPC (Gold Compass) in Base of Roots teaches the 4th-job skills that normally need a quest or a skill book, for 50,000,000 mesos (500,000,000 for a Beginner). It teaches your own job line and your past lives' lines only.

The 5th tier

  1. Reach your 4th job and rebirth three times.
  2. Get a World's Engine: the quest "Magnus the Tyrant!" (level 200, after the quest before it) — defeat Magnus, then turn it in to Wyatt the Reborn.
  3. Talk to the Job Master. The World's Engine is used up and you learn your tier's four skills.

The ticket calls these "hyper/5th skills". The code only has the 5th tier (job x13) and no separate hyper-skill system. Is "hyper" just another name for it?
