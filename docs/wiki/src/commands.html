Type these in normal chat. @help (or @commands) lists them in game.

Info

CommandDoes
@ratesYour own rates.
@showratesWorld and character rates.
@checkWorld rates, server time, time to the daily and weekly reset.
@time / @uptimeServer time / how long the server has been up.
@onlinePlayers online.
@ranksPlayer rankings.
@pointsYour point total.
@equiplvLevels of your equipped items.
@weeklyYour weekly boss attempts.
@elbExpedition leaderboard.
@creditsThe people who made the server possible.

Monsters and drops

CommandDoes
@whodrops <item> (@wd)Which monsters drop an item.
@whatdropsfrom <monster> (@wdf)What a monster drops.
@mobhp / @bosshpHP of the monsters / bosses on your map.
@droplimitThe drop limit of your map.
@dps <seconds>Measure your damage over some seconds.
@gachalistGachapon rewards.

Character

CommandDoes
@str @dex @int @luk <amount>Put AP into a stat.
@resetapReset your AP.
@resetspSet all skill levels to 0 (get your SP back).
@toggleexpTurn all EXP gain off or on.
@gfx on|offSkill effects on or off.

Items and mesos

CommandDoes
@shopThe all-in-one shop.
@sellSell everything in an inventory tab.
@qsQuick sell (type @qs for how).
@bbBuy back what you just sold, or list recent sales.
@bankStore or take out mesos.
@bp / @backpackOpen the backpack / turn the backpack system on or off.
@exchangeTrade ores and crystals for another kind (two for one).

Chat and the regulars

CommandDoes
@quiet megaphonesHide the regulars' megaphones.
@quiet botsHide the regulars' chatter (people you're talking to or partied with still show).
@quiet all / @quiet offHide both / show everything again.
@worldchatWorld chat on or off.
@roll [n]Roll 0 to n (default 100).

Real players' chat and megaphones and GM notices are never hidden. @quiet lasts until the server restarts.

Help and fixes

CommandDoes
@gm <message>Message the game masters.
@reportbug <text>Report a bug.
@disposeUnstick an NPC chat.
@enableauthReset the PIC cooldown.
@autologinAuto login to your current channel and character, on or off.
@changelLanguage setting.

Fun

@minesweeper, @memory, @joinevent / @leaveevent (when a GM runs an event), @mylawn (claim the current map).

Several of these come from MapleRoot unchanged and weren't tried on SlumsMS (@mylawn, @changel, @points, @elb). Remove any you don't want players to see.
