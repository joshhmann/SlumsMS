Your second rebirth takes a Red Essence Stone. Wyatt the Reborn makes it from one Rock of Time and one Von Leon's Seal. This page walks the road to both.

Before you start

  • You have your 1st rebirth, and you are level 200 on a 4th job.
  • For Von Leon: level 120 or higher, quest Defeat the Lion King started or done, a party of 1 to 6.

The steps

  1. Get a Rock of Time. It drops from Pink Bean (about 4%), or the Temple keeper hands one out after a cleared Pink Bean run. Pink Bean needs level 120 or higher and an expedition of 6 to 30.
  2. Reach Lion King's Castle. El Nath's Sharp Cliff leads to the Desolate Moor, then the Castle Entrance, then the towers, then the Audience Room Corridor. The castle pit is jumpable again for every job, and the two ropes across it still work.
  3. Beat Von Leon. His recruiter stands in the Audience Room Corridor. The fight has a limit of 18 clears a day.
  4. Get Von Leon's Seal. It is not a drop. After the clear, stay in the room and talk to Plunk: he hands it to you.
  5. Make the stone. Click Wyatt the Reborn in Base of Roots and pick the last option in his list. That opens his rebirth menu. Shortcut: type @rebirth while you are in Base of Roots. Choose Make an Essence Stone. You need both parts and a free Etc slot, and you cannot already hold a stone.
  6. Be reborn. Bring Wyatt the stone and choose Explorer, Cygnus or Aran.

If something goes wrong

  • You want another go at Von Leon. Once you have started Defeat the Lion King, Ifia on the Roof of the Fifth Tower can send you back to the Audience Room corridor: pick Please send me to the Audience Room Corridor in her list. This is new and a first version.
  • Wyatt will not make a second stone while you hold one.

After the second rebirth

  • Your EXP rate is 2x (see Rates). It stays 2x after you log out and in.
  • Your skills stay learned, and so do your items and mesos. Every stat point you had comes back to you as a free point to spend again.
  • Keys you bound before the rebirth stay bound. A skill only casts when you meet its needs, though: a sword skill on a key will not fire while you are a Beginner with no sword.

The general rules are on Rebirths.
