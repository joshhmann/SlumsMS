Achievements: 32 goals, a medal for each

1.6.2 gave the game a wall of small goals. Each one you finish hands you a medal in the Equip tab of your inventory: no stats, just something to wear. You see a yellow line in the chat when one is earned, and the medal is listed on your character's card.

Seeing where you stand

  • @achievements lists the goals you are still working on, with your progress: [3/5] and so on, plus "Achievements: 4 of 32 earned." and the ones you have.
  • @achievements all lists every goal, done or not.
  • If your Equip tab is full when a medal is due, it waits: the list says "(medal waits for room)".

What is on the list

Four for the job advancements, four for levels (30, 70, 120 and your class's maximum), seven for the classic bosses (Mano, Stumpy, Mushmom, Jr. Balrog, Papulatus, Zakum, Horntail), two for fishing, four party quests (Kerning, Ludibrium, Orbis, Pirate), a wedding, 50 quests, a guild, the 7-day and 28-day check-in streaks, three for visiting Victoria Island, Ossyria and Aquarium, 10 trades, buying five times from player shops, and your first rebirth.

What counted before 1.6.2

The things the game already knows about your character count the next time you log in: your job, level, guild, wedding, rebirths. Bosses you killed, party quests you cleared, quests you did, fish you caught and check-in days from before the update do not: those start from zero.

Family

Families were in the game before 1.6.2, but the perks did nothing until 1.6.1. Now they work, and the regulars can be your juniors.

Reputation

Family perks are paid for with reputation, which you earn by hunting:

WhatReputation
Each monster you kill4
Each boss you kill20
Each level you gain200

Your senior gets the reputation you earn, and so does their senior. If your senior is a lower level than you, they get half. Reputation is not shared with the whole family, only up the line you are in.

The family buffs do not stack with each other: take a 2x EXP bonus and it replaces a 1.5x one, it does not add to it. They do stack with EXP and drop coupons and with whatever the world is running. A buff keeps running through a server restart, and it runs out on time.

Juniors

  • You add a junior from the Family window. They have to be on the same map as you, above level 10, and within 20 levels of you.
  • A family only grows so wide: the game refuses to link two branches when the generations would add up past the limit.
  • Regulars can be your juniors. Ask one and it answers within 30 minutes; after that the offer is gone. A regular junior hunts with you and passes its reputation up to you, but at most 300 a day, so a regular that grinds all evening cannot pay for every perk.
