Bera is never empty. Its regulars train on the maps that suit their level, ride the boats and trains between continents, keep shops in the Free Market, shout on megaphones, and drift in and out through the day (busier in the evening and at weekends). Some move on for good; new faces turn up every day.

Who you'll meet

  • Trainers grinding the maps for their level and job (see Where to train).
  • Townsfolk hanging around Henesys, the Pet Park, the potion shop and the markets.
  • Merchants with shops in the Free Market rooms and in town markets, buying and selling.
  • Hosts and dealers: the gacha regular in Henesys, blackjack tables, a drop-game host with a crowd, a Game Zone host, jump-quest regulars, people forming Orbis PQ parties.

Talking to someone

Talk in plain words: "wanna party?", "come to henesys", "how much for the work gloves". They do it, then answer like a player, at typing speed.

  1. Say their name in normal chat, or part of it (a nickname like "fire" for xFirex works). Without a name, the one you are facing answers, or the one standing closest to you. They answer in a chat bubble.
  2. Keep talking. For about 50 seconds after each line they keep listening, so follow-ups don't need the name. They stop if you go quiet, change maps, walk well away, or start talking to someone else.
  3. Not sure who you meant? If a line nearly matches a name, they may ask "u talking to me?"; answer "ya".

Their menu and whispers

  • Double-click someone to open their character info and then their menu (talk, party, where do you train...). Merchants, the gacha, dealers and hosts have their own menus.
  • Whispers work too, and they whisper back. Busy people may only send a short "busy". Mistyped name? You'll get a "did you mean...?" for people nearby.

They remember you (1.5.0)

Every regular keeps a feeling about each player, and it changes with what you do.

  • Friends: partying, buffs, heals, rescues, trades, gifts, fame and friendly chat. Friends greet you, send buddy requests, invite you to parties and buff you. A friend may give you potions, a scroll or 100k-500k mesos, at most once a week. A friend always joins your party; a stranger about one time in three.
  • Rivals: KS, taking their drops, defame and rude chat. Rivals trash-talk (about your game: level, gear, deaths, KS count), steal a few mobs on that map and won't trade or party. A rival you KS'd may KS you back or call you out on a megaphone; their friends and guildmates only side-eye and gossip. Saying "sorry" calms it.
  • Grudges fade over a few weeks, friendships over a couple of months.
  • @botdrama off keeps everyone neutral or friendly toward you: no rivals at all.
  • They leave you alone while you're in a party quest or event, at a boss, trading, in a shop, talking to an NPC, or away.

Buddies

Add a regular to your buddy list. One who knows you answers buddy chat like a person, after a few seconds to a few minutes; a stranger ignores you. Ask "where r u" and they name their map and invite you over.

Guilds and wars

  • Ten regular guilds: Legendz, MoonLiteX, MesoMafia, SlumLords, ZakumSlayerz, KoreanBBQ, SnailGang, Pwnage, NoobTeam and HenesysKidz. They chat in guild chat, recruit, and run boss trips you can join by partying a member.
  • Ask an officer to join one. Regulars who like you join your guild too, but get picky once it is half regulars.
  • Rival guilds go to war for a week over grind maps. A guild can declare war on a player guild it can't stand: @guildwar shows the score, and @guildwar peace (master or jr. master) ends it.
  • Guild rankings, regulars and players together: Heracle in Orbis (Guild Base) or any Honorable Rock.

Romance (opt-in)

Off unless you type @romance on, or flirt with someone first (then only that one flirts back). @romance off stops it all. With it on, a regular who really likes you may flirt and ask you out, or you can ask. A partner turns up when you're online, sometimes follows you to a new map, and whispers now and then. Propose with an engagement ring box from Moony in Amoria (level 50, any genders), and marry in Amoria with @romance wedding. Ignore a partner for four days and you get dumped. @romance off stops it; @romance divorce ends a marriage. It stays PG-13.

Companions and your alts

@botme lists your other characters; @botme <name> brings one along as a party member (up to 5 companions). @dismiss <name> or @dismiss all sends them home.

@settings

One window for bot drama, romance, their megaphones and their chatter, each with its state: click a line to switch it.

Your own characters

1.6.2 lets you call your other characters to play with you: they join your party, follow you, fight and loot.

  • @botme <name> calls one; you can also invite it by typing its name in the party window, which works while it is offline (the buddy list can't, because an offline character shows offline there).
  • Five companions at once, regulars and your own characters together. Changing channel sends your own ones home. @dismiss <name>, or @dismiss all, sends them home and saves them.

Give them orders with @alts <order>, or just say the order in party chat with their name or slot number in front:

When you are rude

  • One rude line gets a brush-off, nothing more. A second rude line to the same regular within 10 minutes, and that regular cools on you and remembers it.
  • Be rude to three or more regulars on one map within 10 minutes and word gets around: everyone on the map goes a little colder and may tease you about it for an hour (not with @botdrama off).
  • A real apology to the regular you were rude to clears it. Death wishes and slurs are not laughed off at all.

Good to know

  • Be decent and they'll be friendly. Try to scam, beg or mess with them and they'll be cold, may refuse to party, and may tell their friends.
  • Gifts come only from friends. Otherwise trade, and haggle: see Gachapon, shops and trading.
  • They use the Slums Exchange too: their item orders sit a few percent either side of the going price, and about a third of them buy and sell the leaves, elixirs and scrolls they find. NX is the bank's desk, not theirs.
  • Too much chatter? See @quiet. @quiet bots also stops whispers, invites and buddy requests you didn't ask for.
