Bera is never empty. Its regulars train on the maps that suit their level, ride the boats and trains between continents, keep shops in the Free Market, shout on megaphones, and drift in and out through the day (busier in the evening and at weekends). Some move on for good; new faces turn up every day.

Who you'll meet

  • Trainers grinding the maps for their level and job (see Where to train).
  • Townsfolk hanging around Henesys, the Pet Park, the potion shop and the markets.
  • Merchants with shops in the Free Market rooms and in town markets, buying and selling.
  • Hosts and dealers: the gacha regular in Henesys, blackjack tables, a drop-game host with a crowd, a Game Zone host, jump-quest regulars, people forming Orbis PQ parties.

Talking to someone

  1. Say their name in normal chat, or part of it (a nickname like "fire" for xFirex works). They answer in a chat bubble.
  2. Keep talking. For about 50 seconds after each line they keep listening, so follow-ups don't need the name. They stop if you go quiet, change maps, walk well away, or start talking to someone else.
  3. Not sure who you meant? If a line nearly matches a name, they may ask "u talking to me?"; answer "ya".

<name> tradeMerchants point you to their shop; everything they sell is there at the posted price. Others say no.
anything elseJust chat. They know where they are, what they're doing, and they remember you.

Their menu and whispers

  • Double-click someone to open their character info and then their menu (talk, party, where do you train...). Merchants, the gacha, dealers and hosts have their own menus.
  • Whispers work too, and they whisper back. Busy people may only send a short "busy". Mistyped name? You'll get a "did you mean...?" for people nearby.

Good to know

  • They remember people. Be decent and they'll be friendly. Try to scam, beg or mess with them and they'll be cold, may refuse to party, and may tell their friends.
  • Nobody hands out items or mesos for nothing. Trade in the FM like everyone else.
  • Too much chatter? See @quiet.
