Two bosses that people get stuck on, written for how this server's scripts actually run them.

Zakum: the three trials

Before you can fight Zakum you pass three trials. Adobis runs all of them from The Door to Zakum in El Nath. If you haven't earned the Chief's Residence Council's approval yet, he only warns you that the power of old has not been forgotten; get the approval first, then come back and face the trials.

Haste and the other movement skills do not work in the mines or in the Breath of Lava jump quest. Those maps switch them off, for you and for the regulars. Plan to walk and jump on your own.

Part 1: the Unknown Dead Mine (party quest)

  • Make a party of 1 to 6 players, level 50 or higher, all standing in The Door to Zakum. Only the party leader talks to Adobis and picks Enter the Unknown Dead Mine (Stage 1). You get 30 minutes.
  • Inside, Aura explains the job: somewhere in the mine is a Fire Ore. Find it and bring it to Aura. Your leader hands it over, not you. If a regular is in the party, they can't reach one key in the mines yet (fixed in the next update); grab that one yourself.
  • Optional: there are Paper Documents under the rocks. Bring Aura 30 of them as well and every member also gets 5 Return Scrolls for Dead Mine, which take you back to the mine's entrance.
  • Whichever way you finish, every member gets a Piece of Fire Ore from Aura. Make sure everyone has room in their bags first, then leave by the portal.

Part 2: the Breath of Lava (jump quest)

  • Talk to Adobis again and pick Face the Breath of Lava (Stage 2). You need the Piece of Fire Ore, and you must not already hold The Breath of Lava.
  • It is a jump quest over lava, alone: fail it and there is a real chance you die. No Haste, no movement skills. Amon inside the map lets you leave if you want to start over.
  • Finish it and you hold The Breath of Lava.

Part 3: forging the Eyes of Fire

  • Talk to Adobis and pick Forging the Eyes of Fire (Stage 3). You need the Piece of Fire Ore, The Breath of Lava and 30 Zombie's Lost Gold Tooth.
  • He takes all three and gives you 5 Eyes of Fire, and the trials are done. The expedition leader must carry an Eye of Fire to start the Zakum expedition with Adobis.

Horntail: the head rooms

Horntail's heads are fought in separate head rooms. Your alts and the regulars on your expedition fight Horntail, melee classes included.

  • Since the hotfix after 1.6.5 (live 8 Nov, 07:21 UTC), your own alts fight in Horntail's two head rooms too. Before it, they could stand idle there.
  • Horntail now only counts as cleared once every part is down. If the body drops while parts are alive, the fight is not over: finish the parts.

Slums Stamps belong to your account

Stamps (@stamps, from the Daily Check-In) are kept per account, not per character. Any character on the account sees the same number and spends from the same pile, and they can't be traded. Details are on Money.
