The short version is in game: type @guide (or @tutorial). It also opens by itself the first time each character logs in.

The buttons by the chat bar

ButtonDoes
SHOPOpens the Cash Shop in a window. You stay on your map while you shop.
TRADETakes you to Base of Roots, the hub (Job Master, rebirths, warper, storage, the Free Market portal). It doesn't work during an event or in a mini-dungeon. The old Maple Trade System is gone.
MENU and SHORTCUTThe game's usual v83 menus for your windows and options. SlumsMS doesn't change them.

Five channels

Bera has five channels. Channel 1 is the busiest (about 45% of the regulars) and channel 5 the quietest. At the evening peak channel 1's bar shows full. You can still pick it, and real players are never turned away because of the regulars.

@goto <town>

Everyone can hop between towns. Type @goto for the list. The names include henesys, ellinia, perion, kerning, lith, sleepywood, orbis, elnath, ludi, aqua, leafre, mulung, herb, nlc, showa, shrine, ariant, magatia, singapore, amoria and temple.

  • Towns only. Boss maps and dungeons are not on the list.
  • It doesn't work from events, party quests, mini-dungeons or maps you can't leave.
  • The "take me there" lines in @guide follow the same rules.

Bounces by class

  • Thieves and Night Walkers keep the strong Ninja Bounce and Vertical Bounce.
  • Everyone else gets a Ninja Bounce as strong as Flash Jump and a shorter Vertical Bounce.
  • Ninja Bounce only works in the air, like Flash Jump.
  • No bouncing or Flash Jump on jump quest maps.
  • Double-tap jump is off by default. To make a second jump in the air do your Ninja Bounce, set doubletapjump=true under [clienthax] in config.ini.

Daily Check-In

Log in each day for a reward. The window opens by itself when a reward is waiting, or type @daily (or @checkin). The streak runs for 28 days, one per account, from level 10.

World events

Every evening around 8 PM Pacific something hits Henesys on channel 1: a Giant Cake to whack or a Henesys Invasion, with warnings 10 and 1 minutes before. Smaller events pop up at busy hours. Rewards are the event's prizes and the monsters' own EXP and drops.

MENU and SHORTCUT: the client and server have no code for them, so they should be the stock v83 menus. Nobody has checked what's in them on the SlumsMS client. Look once and fill in the list.
