Base of Roots — the main hub

The TRADE button by the chat bar (the "FM button") takes you to Base of Roots, an Arcane-style hub. Nearly every custom NPC is there, so it is the place to go back to between trips.

Star force and Spell Traces

Glowing Treasure in Base of Roots raises your equipment's star level. Pick an item and it shows the mesos, the Spell Traces, the stat gains and the exact success chance before you say yes; the mesos are capped at 50m a try. The Spell Traces (item 4000999, the scrap of paper in your Etc tab) are what this part of the page is about.

Every try spends both, whether it works or not. The traces go up with the item's required level and with its star level: for a level 120 or higher item, a try at 0 stars costs 25 traces, at 5 stars 45, at 10 stars 95; for a level 70 to 99 item, 15, 25 and 65. Superior gear is a flat 50 plus 5 per star. The success chance starts at 100% and falls as the stars go up (60% at 5 stars, 20% at 10, 1% from 24 on). Ordinary gear that fails keeps every stat — only the mesos and traces are gone. Superior gear loses a star and that star's stats when a try fails, and a bad roll leaves it as it is: star force never destroys an item.

Where Spell Traces come from

Monsters don't drop them. There are two ways to get them:

  • Boss rewards. Plunk, the NPC at the exit of a cleared boss room, hands them out once per clear to every player in the fight (regulars get nothing): Cygnus 500 every time, Von Leon 200 on a 1-in-5 roll, Will, Lucid, Verus and Darknell 800 on normal and 2,000 on hard, Magnus 4,000. Zakum and Horntail give none.
  • The Maker (Nimble, the Master Forger). Crafting with a star-forced piece of gear in the star force slot uses that gear up: its stars' stats go onto the item you craft, and you get back 30% of the traces those stars cost, rounded up. A level 120 or higher item at 10 stars gives back 122.

Star force is end-game gear, so the traces are meant to come from boss runs and from gear you've outgrown, not from grinding.

The classic Free Market

The old v83 Free Market (the entrance and its 22 rooms) is kept as it was. Get there by:

  • the portal in Base of Roots, or
  • the Free Market portal in any town (27 towns have one).

Leaving the FM takes you back to where you came in (Base of Roots if it can't tell). The TRADE button still goes to Base of Roots, so your hub bookmark is never lost.

At the entrance:

  • Fredrick collects what your hired merchant didn't sell, and its mesos.
  • Scrooge: storage.
  • Hanako sells gachapon tickets and cosmetics for mesos (see Gachapon, shops and trading).
  • Nimble, the Master Forger, as in Base of Roots.
  • The Buy/Sell board, with the posts from the website's board.
  • The Maple TV: a scroll generator, 5 prizes a day.

The rooms have player shops and the merchants among the regulars. Their price is the price on the shop; buying works the same as from anyone else.

Crafting NPCs in town

  • Coco: Chaos Scroll 60%, at most 5 per talk.
  • Agent E: accessories. The random roll gives belts only; stat rings cost 1-2m mesos plus materials.
  • Dalair: merges items. You need level 160 and Maker level 3, and one item takes at most 3 merges.
  • Spiegelmann: ore refining.

Lazy Penguins

Fifteen Lazy Penguins nap around the world, one to a town: Henesys, Ellinia, Perion, Kerning City, Lith Harbor, Sleepywood, Nautilus, Orbis, Ludibrium, Aquarium, Leafre, Mu Lung, Showa Town, New Leaf City and Happyville. Click one and it says something lazy.

Starting on Maple Island

The first time you log in with a brand-new starter (levels 1 to 9, a Beginner, Noblesse, Legend or Evan), the game asks once: start on Maple Island?

  • Yes takes you to Mushroom Town, the classic beginner island with its quests and tips. Once you are level 7, talk to Shanks at Southperry: he sails you to Lith Harbor for free (anyone else pays his 150 mesos, or shows him Lucas's letter). You don't come back to the island afterwards.
  • No leaves you where you are, with the usual start.

Existing characters are asked too, once, if they never were and are still under level 10.
