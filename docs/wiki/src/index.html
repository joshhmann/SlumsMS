SlumsMS is a small, friends-only MapleStory v83 world. It runs on MapleRoot (custom jobs and skills, rebirths, Arcane River and Grandis maps, a 5th tier for every class), and its towns are full of regulars who train, trade, chat and come and go through the day.

New here? Start with Getting started, then read The town's regulars to learn how to talk to people. In game, the guide book in your ETC tab has the short version.
