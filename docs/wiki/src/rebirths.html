SlumsMS is a rebirth world. At level 200 you can start over at level 1, keep your old job's skills, and do it up to 3 times.

How

  1. Be level 200 and fully advanced (4th job).
  2. Ask Wyatt the Reborn in Base of Roots to make you an Essence Stone. He makes the one your next rebirth needs, and if you are missing a part he tells you which one and where it drops.
  3. Bring him the stone (it is used up) and choose to start again as a Beginner (Explorer), Noblesse (Cygnus) or Legend (Aran).
RebirthStoneWyatt makes it fromWhere the parts come from
1stBlue Essence StoneZakum Diamond + Nine Spirit's EggZakum, Horntail

3rdBlack Essence StoneCygnus Circlet + Cygnus Knight EmblemCygnus; the emblem drops from the guards of the Cygnus Knights' stronghold or from Chief Alex's Lost Emblem quest

For the Black stone he only needs to see the Cygnus Knight Emblem: he takes the Circlet and you keep the emblem. He will not make a second stone while you are holding one, and he asks you to free up a slot in your Etc tab first if it is full.

What you keep

  • Your past jobs and their skills. The Key Binder puts them on your keys. Max a skill before you rebirth: it comes along at the level you left it.
  • Your items and mesos.
  • After each rebirth, claim 100 SP from the Key Binder (once per rebirth).

EXP after a rebirth

1st rebirth 3x, 2nd 2x, 3rd 1x (see Rates). After the 3rd rebirth you can level past 200, up to 255, at 1x.
