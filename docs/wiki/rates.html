Rates

RateValueWhat it means
EXP3xLevels come three times faster than GMS v83.
Meso10xMonster mesos and quest mesos are x10. NPC shop and sell prices are normal.
Drop4xScrolls, equips, ores and ETC drop four times as often.
Boss drop3xBoss items (Zakum helm, Horntail necklace pieces, books...).
Quest mesosfollow the meso rateQuest meso rewards are x10 as well.
Party questsbonus EXPPQ EXP is boosted (PQ item rewards are not).
Partiesbonus EXPTraining in a party gives extra EXP.
EXP cards2x or 3x2x (7 days) or 3x (1 day) from the Cash Shop, all day. Type @coupons to see yours.

Rebirths and EXP

After a rebirth your own EXP rate is set by how many rebirths you have:

RebirthsEXP rate
none3x (the world rate)
13x
22x
31x (also 1x past level 200)

The old guide book in your ETC tab says 85x / 45x / 18x / 4x. Those are another server's numbers and are not SlumsMS's.

Check in game

@rates shows your own rates and @showrates shows the world and character rates. @check shows the world rates, the server time and how long until the daily and weekly (Thursday) resets.

These values are the live config recorded on 2026-10 (Scania then; the world is Bera since 1.3.1).
