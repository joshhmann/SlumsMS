Rebirths

SlumsMS is a rebirth world (from MapleRoot). At level 200 you can start over at level 1, keep your old job's skills, and do it up to 3 times.

How

  1. Be level 200 and fully advanced (4th job).
  2. Have the right Essence Stone for your next rebirth.
  3. Talk to Wyatt the Reborn in Base of Roots. Choose to start again as a Beginner (Explorer), Noblesse (Cygnus) or Legend (Aran).
RebirthStoneMade from
1stBlue Essence StoneZakum Diamond + Nine Spirit's Egg
2ndRed Essence StoneRock of Time + Von Leon's Seal
3rdBlack Essence StoneCygnus Circlet + Cygnus Knight Emblem

The crafting script (craft.js) exists, but @craft is switched off in CommandsExecutor and no NPC opens it. How do players make Essence Stones on SlumsMS? (MapleRoot's old book says @exchange, but @exchange is an ore exchange.)

What you keep

  • Your past jobs and their skills. The Key Binder puts them on your keys. Max a skill before you rebirth: it comes along at the level you left it.
  • Your items and mesos.

EXP after a rebirth

1st rebirth 3x, 2nd 2x, 3rd 1x (see Rates).
